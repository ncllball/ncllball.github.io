Group ticket days for NCLL families: Mariners Little League Day and the UW Baseball and Softball NCLL Days. The 2027 dates come out in the new year, and tickets go up here as soon as they open.

Ticket Events

Mariners Little League Day

Wear your NCLL jersey to T-Mobile Park: Little League ticket prices and, in past years, a pregame parade on the field.

  • 2027: dates coming soon (2026 was May 3 and May 31)
  • Tickets: Little League prices (2026: $18 / $10)
  • Questions: email Akiko, our ticket-day coordinator
