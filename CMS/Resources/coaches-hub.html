Google Chat for Coaches

With hundreds of coaches, we can’t adapt to everyone’s preferred communication apps, so we ask you to adapt to the one we use. That way everyone is in the same place.

We set up a Google Chat Space for every baseball and softball division and invite you. Just accept the invite.

Why You Should Use It

Here’s the payoff: every Space starts with the president, registrar, scheduler, equipment, uniforms and your division coordinator.

Want a direct line to them and your fellow coaches? This is it.

It’s been a big success in the divisions that have used it.

Getting Set Up

  • After team formation, we invite your NCLL-registered email to your division’s Space. Just accept.
  • Use the Google Chat app (iOS/Android), chat.google.com, or Chat inside Gmail.
  • Turn on notifications for your division Space.
  • No invite? Email registrar@ncllball.com.

Your Division Space

  • We create one Space per division, named like “2027 Majors BB Coaches” or “2027 AAA SB Coaches.”
  • Use it for schedule changes, rainouts, field swaps, equipment and uniforms, and umpire coordination.
  • Check the pinned messages at the top of your Space for schedules, contacts and field info.

Ground Rules

  • Keep posts on-topic: games, fields, schedules and volunteers. Smack talk is welcome if it’s shorter than a rain delay and your team backs it up Saturday.
  • Reply in threads so the main feed stays scannable.
  • Tag people (@name) when you need action; skip @all unless it’s urgent.
  • No player evaluations, umpire complaints or personal disputes.

Tips & Troubleshooting

  • Muted Space? Tap the Space name → Notifications → set to "All" or "Mentions & replies."
  • Trouble getting in? Initial acceptance into a Space can be “sticky” — just persevere. The treasure awaits you on the other side!
  • Want to use a different email? That’s totally fine — we just need to know what it is. Your division coordinator can help get that to the registrar.

Rain, Reschedules & Fields

  • Before first pitch: you and the other coach decide; umpires advise.
  • After first pitch: the umpire crew chief decides.
  • Lightning or thunder: stop play at once.
  • Weekdays: get the Parks daily field emails (12:30 and 3:30).

When in doubt, if you can leave it better than you found it, then play ball. Otherwise, take the week off.

When a game is off

  1. Make sure the other coach knows.
  2. Tell your umpires if they weren’t in on it.
  3. Tell your team and families.
  4. Home team: tell your division head.
  • Another team on your field? Show our permit; be gracious.
  • Field damage: fields@ncllball.com

Diamond Leader Training (All-Stars)

Only for coaches who want to manage or coach a tournament team, like All-Stars. Regular-season coaches aren’t required to take it, but it really is a good program.

It’s free, self-paced and one-time: real-life scenarios on how you shape players’ confidence, focus and love of the game.

Details

  • Time: Self-paced
  • Cost: Free
  • Frequency: One-time; certificates from past seasons still count
  • Deadline: Before you’re named a tournament manager or coach, and before any tournament practice
  • Proof: Keep your certificate; District 8 can ask to see it

How to Complete

  1. Access the Platform
    • Go to Little League University – Diamond Leader Training Program
    • Create an account (first-time) or log in (returning)
  2. Enroll
    • Search for “Diamond Leader Training Program” if not immediately visible
    • If asked, search for North Central Little League
    • Open the course and click Enroll / Register

How to Complete (cont.)

  1. Complete Modules
    • Work through the real-life scenario modules at your own pace
    • Review the supporting resources in each section
  2. Receive Your Certificate
    • A certificate is issued upon completion — save or print for your records
    • Keep it: it’s your proof of tournament eligibility

Tips & Reminders

  • One-time only: Unlike Abuse Awareness, you do not need to retake this course each year
  • Revisit anytime: LLI strongly encourages returning to review content and resources throughout your coaching career
  • Technical issues: Use the LLU Help / Support link, or try a fresh browser tab or incognito mode

Get Started

Planning to manage or coach All-Stars or another tournament team? Complete this free, self-paced course from Little League International first.
