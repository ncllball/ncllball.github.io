Umpire Training & Clinics

Two short sessions get you ready: a classroom orientation and an on-field mechanics clinic, led by veteran NCLL umpires.

  • When: March (2027 dates TBA)

The two sessions

  • Orientation & Rules: a classroom session on expectations, safety, game flow and the key rules.

Game Sign-ups

Grab assignments that match your division comfort level. We post baseball, softball, and interleague needs.

  • Scheduler: Updated weekly during spring
  • Open to: AA and higher in both softball and baseball
  • Tip: Claim early to pair with your preferred partner

Signup tips

  • Note plate/base preferences in the comment field so schedulers can balance workloads.
  • Flag travel or interleague dates that require extra briefing on ground rules.
  • Use the same form to release a game if plans change—last-minute swaps keep coverage solid.

Need a specific mentor? Add their name to the request so we can co-assign whenever possible.

Youth Umpire Track

Teens 13 and up can umpire: earn money or service hours and build real leadership skills.

  • Who: league age 13+
  • Start: on the bases, with a veteran nearby
  • Earn: paid per game (14+), or service hours

How it works

  • Start on the bases; plate games come with experience.
  • $30/game on the bases, $40/game behind the plate (plate with UIC approval), or service hours instead.
  • A parent or guardian gives consent at sign-up. The background check applies at 13 and up.
  • We document hours for school requirements or awards.

Know a teen who’d be great?

Older siblings, former players, neighbors: send them our way. Training covers everything they need.
