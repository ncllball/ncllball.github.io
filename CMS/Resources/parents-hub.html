What Is a Club Team?

Exploring the “ifs” and “whos” of committing to a club/select/travel team can be daunting. You’re not alone; lots of us have been there.

This guide will help you demystify how club/select works alongside Little League, costs, travel, and fit for your player.

Three names, one idea: a team you try out for that practices, travels and costs more than Little League. Expect weekend tournaments throughout the season, more chances to improve, and a step toward high school ball.

NCLL often hosts clinics and events with local clubs: a low-pressure way to meet coaches, let them see your player, and find out if your child is ready for the next level (and wants it).

Little League

  • Starts with Tee Ball, as young as 4.
  • Moves up through coach pitch and player pitch into the teens.
  • The focus is playing time, fundamentals and fun.

Club Teams

  • More competitive, with tryouts.
  • More practice and travel, so more money and time.
  • Some start at 7 or 8; most players join between 9 and 12.
  • Often aimed at high school and college ball.
  • Most clubs have several teams by skill level.

Before You Commit

  • Starting ages vary by club, and every child develops at their own rate.
  • Think about your child’s physical, emotional and social readiness.
  • Leave room for school and other activities.
  • Check each club’s tryouts and costs, in money and in time.
  • Our lists are a starting point. Ask coaches and parents who know your player.

Our Take

Choosing your child’s path in baseball or softball deserves care. NCLL doesn’t run or endorse any club.

Note : Views differ within NCLL, but most of us agree that 7-8 is early to start club ball, and in softball, club teams that young are hard to find. Focus on fun and a love of the game.

All volunteers (coaches, team parents, board members, etc.) must complete a volunteer application annually. Choose the appropriate form based on your status:

  • New Volunteers : Complete the full Volunteer Application which includes a background check authorization
