Donate Equipment

Gear collecting dust in the garage? Send it back to the field.

What We Take

  • Gloves, bats, catcher’s gear, balls. No helmets.
  • Field tools: rakes, drags, chalkers, tarps.
  • Bins and shelves for our sheds.

Where It Goes

We check every item, pass good gear to families who need it and send extras to Pitch In For Baseball & Softball. NCLL is a 501(c)(3), so donations may be tax-deductible.

Drop It Off

Contact Ernesto at equipment@ncllball.com or (626) 806-3031 (call or text). Running a gear swap? We’ll help.

One Mom, One Magazine

Last winter, an NCLL mom of two called with an offer. She runs Seattle Saver, a quarterly magazine that lands in about 16,000 Fremont and Wallingford mailboxes, and she had a page to give us.

The catch: the print deadline was close. We already had the ask (balls, lots of them) and our Venmo code, so we said yes and got it to her in time.

She didn’t write a check. She gave us what her business already does, and 16,000 homes heard about NCLL.

"POP UP" Volunteer Lists

It's baseball: Pop-Ups happen anytime. These lists are for one-shift, as-needed help.

Can't commit to a whole season? Join a list: the Ad Hoc Heroes or the Ground Gremlins.

1. Ad Hoc Heroes

Keep special events moving all season.

  • Assessments - setup, check-in
  • Photo Day - cat wrangling
  • Home Run Derby - pitch, track, safety
  • Skills Clinics - station help
  • Fundraisers - raffles, setup
  • Season-end celebrations
  • Time: 2-4 hours per event
  • We email sign-up links before each event

2. Ground Gremlins

The field-first crew keeping every diamond safe and game-ready.

  • Upkeep - weeding, raking, dugouts
  • Field prep - drag, line, set bases
  • Mounds - repair, pack clay
  • Sheds and field equipment
  • Safety checks - report hazards
  • Spring prep, season-end cleanup
  • Time: flexible (often early Saturday texts)

Sign Up

Drop-in help: Even 15 minutes of raking between games makes a difference. We’ll message the lists when help is needed.
