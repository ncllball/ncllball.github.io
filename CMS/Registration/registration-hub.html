Eligibility & Boundaries

NCLL plays in Washington District 8, serving Wallingford, Green Lake, Fremont, Phinney Ridge, Greenwood, and Ballard. Players qualify by meeting one of these:

  • Residency — live inside the NCLL boundary
  • School enrollment — attend a school inside the boundary

Registration asks for proof of eligibility, a Little League International question. NCLL doesn’t require it: skip it and your registration still goes through. We’ll only ask for paperwork if your player makes All-Stars.

Residency

Players who live inside the official NCLL boundary are eligible. Use the Little League International League Finder Tool to confirm your home league.

School Enrollment

Players enrolled at schools inside the NCLL boundary qualify even if they live outside it. Preschools do not affect eligibility; T-ball registration is open regardless of school or residence.

  • Elementary (K–5): BF Day, Bagley, Greenwood, John Stanford, McDonald, Meridian, West Woodland
  • K–8: Pacific Crest, St. Benedict, St. John
  • Middle & high: Hamilton (6–8), Bishop Blanchet (9–12), Lincoln (9–12)

Questions?

Outside the boundary and not at a listed school? New players need a waiver to play; for that and all waiver matters, contact NCLL’s president.

If your school is inside the boundary but missing from the registration list, choose “Other” and type the school name.

Financial Aid

NCLL ensures every child can play baseball or softball regardless of financial situation. No player is turned away for inability to pay.

We offer full and partial scholarships through our confidential need-based assistance program.

Aid is for players who live or go to school in the NCLL boundary.

What Assistance Covers

  • Registration fees (full or partial)
  • Baseball gear (when available)
  • Off-season programs like Winterball and Summerball

How to Apply

  • Fill out the Financial Aid form first, before you register.
  • Then email the Registrar to let us know your form is in.
  • Wait to register until we reply: approved families get a code by email to enter at checkout.

Questions? Everything you send is kept confidential.

Team Formation

Tee-ball through AA players are placed on teams. AAA and Majors teams are formed by a draft, using scores from assessments, so every team in a division is evenly matched.

  • Placement: Tee-ball, Kindy, A and AA
  • Assessments: AAA and Majors (baseball and softball)
  • Draft: AAA and Majors
  • Teams set: by late February

Tee-ball through AA

Baseball Tee-ball, Kindy Coach Pitch, A and AA, and softball A and AA.

  • No assessments or draft.
  • Siblings, schoolmates and friends are grouped together when possible.
  • Registration asks for a coach request and a buddy or friend request.
  • Teams are placed by the Player Agent, the division coordinators and the Registrar.

Assessments

  • Not tryouts: every player makes a team. Assessments are about fit: the division where each player is safe and develops best.
  • Required for AAA and Majors, baseball and softball.
  • Baseball: 9’s not drafted to AAA play AA; 10’s and 11’s not drafted to Majors play AAA.
  • No make-up date: if you miss, placement relies on past seasons and coach input.
About Assessments

Why we draft

Majors has always drafted. In 2024 AAA went back to a draft, as it was before COVID. The area leagues we checked with all draft AAA; we were the only one that didn’t.

  • Balance: talent spread evenly, closer games.
  • Coaching: players learn from different coaches.
  • Community: kids meet families beyond their school.
  • Fairness: a process everyone can see.

Draft day

  • Coaches get each player’s assessment scores and a 1–5 rating.
  • A coach’s own child is placed in the round that matches their rating.
  • A short trade window afterwards is for roster fit (schoolmates, carpools), within the same rating band; not for talent.
  • Playing up: requests to play in a higher division are reviewed for safety, coach feedback and division balance.

After the draft

Teams are finalized by late February, then matchups and schedules are built. Everyone hears the results at the same time, so please keep draft details to yourselves until then.

Policies

Our refund and privacy policies in short. The full text is on the Registration Policies page.

  • Refunds
  • Privacy

Refunds

  • No refunds once a player has taken part in a sanctioned game or practice.
  • Before that, the fee is refunded less an administrative fee (equal to the late fee).
  • Families found to live outside the boundary are refunded less $10, unless their own league has no program for them.
  • If Intermediate (50/70) doesn’t come together, players can move to Juniors or get a refund.

Privacy

We only use your information to run registration and the season. It is never sold or shared outside NCLL, and card numbers are never stored on our systems.

Core Operating Costs

  • Fields: permits, rentals, and on‑field materials/maintenance (chalk, bases, mound clay).
  • Uniforms: jerseys, hats/caps, socks (AAA+ also receive hoodies).
  • Equipment: shared gear — catcher’s equipment, helmets, bats, tees, practice balls, first‑aid kits.
  • Umpires: training and per‑game fees where applicable.
  • Coach/Player development: clinics, assessments, scheduling tools, and in-season development.
  • All‑Stars: tournament uniforms, coach and player development, and tournament fees.
  • Summerball: uniforms for summer programming.
  • Operations & overhead: registration systems, communications, and administration.
