2027 registration opens Monday, November 2. Use the steps below to find your player’s league age and division, then register online. Have questions? Contact our registrar.

Step 1: Determine Eligibility for NCLL

  • NCLL is in Washington's District 8 and covers Wallingford, Greenlake, Fremont, Phinney Ridge, Greenwood, and Ballard
  • Players must live within NCLL boundaries or attend an eligible school
  • New players outside boundaries need a waiver to play; for that and all waiver matters, please contact NCLL's president

Step 2: Determine Your Player's Division

League Age

  • League Age : Your player's age on August 31st (Baseball) or December 31st (Softball) determines their league age for that season
  • League Age is not the same as chronological age (the age they are on their birthday)
  • Baseball: If a player will be 9 years old on August 31, 2027, they are considered "league age 9" for the 2027 season
  • Softball: If a player will be 9 years old on December 31, 2026, they are considered "league age 9" for the 2027 season (Softball uses different dates)
  • Use the age charts and calculator in Step 2(a) below to determine league age

Assessments

  • Required for all AAA, Majors, or Junior division players, for both softball and baseball, regardless of previous division
  • Not tryouts—all players will be placed on a team. Used for skill evaluation and competitive balance
  • The Player Agent will conduct the assessments and make recommendations based on the players' skills and abilities
  • Families will be notified of assessment dates, times, and locations via email

Team Assignments

  • Teams are formed by late February (AAA and Majors by draft)
  • Playing up: Requests to play in higher divisions reviewed based on safety, coach feedback, and division balance

Baseball Notes (Click to expand)

Note: All kids play Tee Ball. Baseball is open to boys and girls. Softball divisions are gender-specific and are for girls only, starting from A Division.

1 Tee Ball (5-6) is for 5's and 6's; 6's with a year of Tee Ball should consider KCP or Baseball A.

2 Kindergarten Coach Pitch (KCP, 6-7) moves at a faster pace than Tee Ball — live coach pitching with the tee available as a fallback. League age 6 required per Little League rules. Played on the Tee Ball field.

3 Baseball A (7-8) uses a coach-pitch and machine pitch blend with safety hardball and is for 7's/8's, but 6's with a year of Tee Ball are encouraged to register.

4 Baseball AA (8-9) is mainly for 8's/9's, though skilled 7-year-olds occasionally qualify.

5 Baseball AAA (9-11) is the primary division for 10's and 11's, though skilled 11's (and a few returning All-Star 10's) may be drafted into Majors. 9's fill remaining roster spots based on assessment scores, with eligibility finalized in late January (see Assessments for details). Highly-skilled 8's are placed here on rare occasions, and only if roster needs dictate. Registering for AAA as a 9 doesn't guarantee a spot: players must be assessed and drafted. 9's who aren't drafted to a AAA roster will be placed on a AA team, and the top AA players go into a AAA call-up pool.

6 Baseball Majors (10-12) is for league age 12's, plus the 11's who score best at assessments and a few highly-skilled returning All-Star 10's who may pose a safety risk or competitive imbalance in lower divisions. Registering for Majors as a non-12 doesn't guarantee a spot: players must assess highly and then be drafted. 11's and 10's who aren't drafted to a Majors roster will be drafted to a AAA team, and the 15-20 highest-assessing AAA players go into a Majors call-up pool.

7 Baseball Intermediate (50/70, 13) is for 13's, with 70-ft bases and a 50-ft mound. Practices can be at Lower Woodland; games are at local fields (Robert Eagle Staff, Loyal Heights, Lower Woodland #1 and Dahl) based on available 50/70 permits. Intermediate isn't guaranteed: it depends on finding a coach, a 50/70 field and enough players, and NCLL will also look at co-leaguing like last season. If it doesn't come together, your player can move to Juniors or get a refund.

8 Baseball Juniors (13-14) is for 13's & 14's. While 12's can join, it's not advised as they'd forego a year of Majors and would be playing on high school-sized field dimensions.

9 Baseball Seniors (15-16) is mainly for 15's & 16's, though as young as 13's are eligible.

10 Juniors & Seniors play on 90ft fields when available. These fields have limited availability and different scheduling constraints than standard fields — days of the week and start times may vary more than other divisions.

11 Days of week reflect recent seasons and the district-level cadence coordinated with other District 8 leagues. Final schedules are confirmed after registration when field allocations and team counts are set, and may shift due to availability, weather, and league-wide constraints; these days are planning guidance rather than a guarantee.

Step 3: Register Online

Registration for the 2027 season opens Monday, November 2, in Sports Connect.

  • Specify your preferred program and division during registration. You may also enter preferred coaches and teammate requests — both are optional.
  • Pay with credit card (Visa or MasterCard)
  • After registration closes, spots are based on availability. Join the waitlist if your division is full
NCLL believes that no one should be denied the ability to play ball due to economic circumstances. Each year, we grant scholarships to families who may need assistance. If you are interested, please fill out the Scholarship Request form or contact our registrar.
