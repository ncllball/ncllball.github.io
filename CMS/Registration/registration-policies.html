Registration Policies

REFUND POLICY

North Central Little League (NCLL) is glad to refund registration fees in full before the season starts. When you register, your payment includes a $3 service fee charged by Sports Connect, the registration platform Little League International requires us to use. That $3 goes directly to Sports Connect to process the payment; it never comes to the league, so it isn’t ours to return. Everything NCLL received is refunded.

AAA and Majors: once a player has been drafted, NCLL reserves the right to decline a refund. Teams are built in the draft for competitive balance, and a player who leaves afterward leaves a team short for the whole season.

All other divisions: NCLL reserves the right to decline refunds once teams have been formed.

All divisions: no refunds are issued after a player has taken part in a sanctioned game or practice.

Exceptions: requests based on injury, a medical reason or a move out of the area are considered case by case; email the Registrar.

Out-of-boundary families: before sanctioned games or practices, a family found to be registered from outside the NCLL boundary without a waiver will be asked to leave the league, unless no program is available to them in the league whose boundary they live in. A refund will be issued less $10.

Intermediate (50/70): if the division does not come together, registered players may move to Juniors or receive a refund.
