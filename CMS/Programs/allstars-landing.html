All-Stars is NCLL’s post-season tournament program. Each summer the league forms Baseball and Softball teams across several age brackets to represent North Central in the Little League District 8 tournament — and, for teams that keep winning, at State, Regionals and the Little League World Series.

This season: the 2026 teams have been announced — see Past Teams & Posters. 2027 dates and the application will be posted on this season’s All-Stars page in the spring.

How it works

  1. Apply. Every interested player must turn in an application — no one is selected automatically.
  2. Tryout. Coaches run a side-by-side tryout, but it’s one input among many: games, practices, attitude and work ethic across the whole season all count.
  3. Rosters. Teams are announced to families by email in late May.
  4. Practice. Practices start in early June — plan on up to 5–6 events a week, with most practices and every game required.
  5. Tournament. District 8 is double-elimination and runs late June to mid-July. Being available for it is required to make a team.

Who can apply: players who meet Little League’s residency/school and age rules, have played the required share of the regular season on an NCLL team, and are available for the District tournament. The exact participation rule for each season is on the season page.

Cost: there’s no registration fee for All-Stars. Teams pay a uniform fee (financial aid is available), and families are expected to help with fundraising.

All-Stars Teams

10's

The youngest All-Star bracket — most players' first taste of tournament baseball or softball.

  • Sport: Baseball & Softball
  • League Ages: 8–10
  • Tournament: District 8 → State

Who & What to Expect

Players who are League Age 8, 9 or 10 can apply. Eligibility is based on League Age, not the division a player played in during the regular season.

Teams play a double-elimination District 8 tournament; the winner advances to State.

How to Apply

Every player has to apply — no one is selected automatically. The application, deadline and tryout times are on this season’s All-Stars page.

Questions? Baseball: baseball@ncllball.com · Softball: softball@ncllball.com

12's / Majors

The flagship All-Star bracket — the 12's are the path to the Little League World Series.

  • Sport: Baseball & Softball
  • League Ages: 10–12
  • Tournament: District 8 → State → Regionals → World Series

Who & What to Expect

Players who are League Age 10, 11 or 12 can apply. This bracket draws the most interest, and selection weighs the whole season, not just the tryout.

District 8 → Washington State → Regionals → the Little League World Series for teams that keep winning. Availability for the full tournament window matters most here.

How to Apply

Every player has to apply — no one is selected automatically. The application, deadline and tryout times are on this season’s All-Stars page.

Questions? Baseball: baseball@ncllball.com · Softball: softball@ncllball.com
