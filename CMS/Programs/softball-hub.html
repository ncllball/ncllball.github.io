NCLL offers softball for ages 6–16, from first-time players to teams preparing for high school. Softball is designated for girls. Each division matches developmental stage, skill focus, and game format.

How to choose: Divisions are based on League Age (age on December 31 of the year before the season). Review the divisions below, including schedules, fields, and what makes each unique. If you're unsure, contact our VP of Softball Gayle.

2027 registration opens November 2. Assessments for AAA, Majors and Juniors are in early February, with team formations shortly after.

If your player may fit a higher division, indicate that during registration. Final placement is decided at assessments (AAA, Majors & Juniors).

Note for coaches: division rules, pitching rules, trainings, key dates and field permits are on the Coaches Hub.

Softball Divisions

Division rules: tap the rules book on any division's card for that division's rules of play.

A - Coach Pitch

Coach-pitch softball for first-time players.

  • League ages: 6–7
  • Format: Coach pitch, 10″ safety ball
  • Schedule: Tue & Sat games
  • Fields: Lower Ross and BF Day

Game Format

No score is kept.

  • Seven pitches per batter; no walks.
  • 3–4 innings in about 75 minutes.
  • About five runs an inning, by agreement.
  • Players rotate positions, and everyone bats.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft.

  • Registration asks for a coach request and a teammate request. Both are optional. They aren’t guaranteed, but we make every effort to place players by those requests first, then with siblings, then by school.
  • Teams are kept even, so games stay fun.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form the first week of March.
  • Practices start in late March.
  • Games start in late April and run to early June.
  • Last season: home games at Lower Ross and BF Day; away games against RUG at Green Lake, Maple Leaf, James Baldwin and Pinehurst.

AA - Coach/Player Pitch

Where girls start pitching.

  • League ages: 7–9
  • Format: Player pitch with coach relief, 11″ ball
  • Schedule: Mostly Saturday games
  • Fields: Lower Woodland and BF Day

Game Format

No score or standings are kept.

  • Players pitch, and coaches step in when needed.
  • Five-run limit per half-inning, none in the last inning.
  • Games last 90–105 minutes.
  • Limited stealing early in the season.
  • Every player gets an infield inning.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft.

  • Registration asks for a coach request and a teammate request. Both are optional. They aren’t guaranteed, but we make every effort to place players by those requests first, then with siblings, then by school.
  • Teams are kept even, so games stay fun.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form the first week of March.
  • Practices start in mid-March.
  • Games start in late March and run to early June.
  • Last season: home games at Lower Woodland and BF Day; away games against RUG, Queen Anne and Ballard at Green Lake, Ravenna, Maple Leaf, Dahl, West Queen Anne and Gilman.

AAA

The first full season of player pitch.

  • League ages: 9–11
  • Format: Player pitch, 11″ ball
  • Schedule: Tue, Thu & Sat games
  • Fields: Lower Woodland

Game Format

Score and standings are kept, and the season ends with a District 8 tournament.

  • 6 innings or 2 hours.
  • Five-run limit per half-inning; the last inning is open.
  • 10-run rule after 4 innings.
  • Pitchers are limited to 3 innings a game through April.
  • Every player gets 9 defensive outs, including 3 innings in the infield.

Assessments & Draft

Assessments in early February, then a draft.

  • Attend assessments to play AAA. There’s no make-up date.
  • Coaches draft to keep teams even.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form in mid-February; practices start the first week of March.
  • Games run late March to late May, then the District 8 tournament into early June.
  • Last season: home games at Lower Woodland; away games against RUG, Northwest, Magnolia, Ballard and Northeast at Maple Leaf, Ravenna, Soundview, Northacres, Lawton, Ballard Community Center and View Ridge.

Majors

Faster pitching from 40 feet.

  • League ages: 10–12
  • Format: Player pitch, 12″ ball, 40 ft
  • Schedule: Mon, Wed & Fri games
  • Fields: Lower Woodland

Game Format

Score and standings are kept, and the season ends with a District 8 tournament.

  • No new inning after 2 hours 15 minutes.
  • Five-run limit per half-inning through April; after that, a mercy rule replaces it.
  • Steals are allowed once the pitch is released.
  • Pitchers are limited to 3 innings a game through April.
  • Every player gets 9 defensive outs, including 3 innings in the infield.

Assessments & Draft

Assessments in early February, then a draft.

  • Attend assessments to play Majors. There’s no make-up date.
  • Coaches draft to keep teams even.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form in mid-February; practices start the first week of March.
  • Games run mid-March to mid-May, then the District 8 tournament into early June.
  • Last season: home games at Lower Woodland; away games against Magnolia, Queen Anne, Northwest, Northeast, Shoreline and RUG at Magnolia, Hamlin Park, Shorewood, Magnuson Park, West Queen Anne, Maple Leaf and Soundview.

Junior Division

High-school-style softball.

  • League ages: 12–14
  • Format: Player pitch, 12″ ball, 43 ft
  • Schedule: Mon, Fri & Sun games
  • Fields: Lower Woodland

Game Format

Score is kept.

  • Seven-inning games; no new inning after 2 hours 15 minutes.
  • Mercy rule: 15 runs after 4 innings, 10 after 5, 8 after 6.
  • Steals are allowed once the pitch is released.
  • Every player gets 9 defensive outs.

Assessments & Teams

Assessments in early February.

  • Attend assessments. There’s no make-up date.
  • In 2026, NCLL fielded one Juniors team.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form in mid-February; practices start in early March.
  • Games run late March to mid-May.
  • Last season: home games at Lower Woodland; away games against teams from Seattle, Shoreline, Bothell, Woodinville and Renton at Woodinville, Shorewood, Maple Leaf, Kiwanis Park, Soundview, SWAC, Tambark Creek and Magnolia.

Senior Division

Summer softball for high-school-age players.

  • League ages: 13–16
  • Format: Player pitch, 12″ ball, 43 ft
  • Schedule: Starts in June
  • Fields: Set by the partner league

Game Format

Seven-inning games.

  • Mercy rule: 15 runs after 4 innings, 10 after 5, 8 after 6.
  • Steals are allowed once the pitch is released.
  • Headfirst slides are allowed when advancing.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft.

  • Registration is a waitlist; a team runs only if enough players sign up.
  • NCLL partners with nearby leagues to form a team.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Sign up any time; the waitlist stays open.
  • The season starts in June, after high school softball.
