North Central Little League offers baseball for players ages 5-16, from first-time players through high school age. Baseball is open to boys and girls; for girls-only play, see our softball divisions.

How to choose: Divisions go by your child’s League Age, their age on August 31, 2027. The cards below cover schedules, fields and what makes each division different. Not sure? Email the Registrar.

Registration opens Monday, November 2. Start with the 2027 Registration guide and Key Dates.

AAA and Majors: Assessments are estimated for Saturday, January 23, with teams formed by about February 20. Please sign up and plan on attending, because placement comes from assessments.

Ready for a higher division? Signing up for a division doesn’t place your player there. Below AAA, the Registrar and division coordinators decide, so email the Registrar.

Coaches: rules and training materials are in the Coaches Hub.

Baseball Divisions

Division rules: tap the rules book on any division's card for that division's rules of play.

Tee Ball

For 5’s and 6’s, typically in Kindergarten or their last year of pre-school.

  • League ages: 5–6
  • Format: Tee only, with a safety ball
  • Schedule: One weeknight practice; Saturday game
  • Fields: Mostly Upper Ross

Game Format

First swings and throws, in a fun, confidence-building setup. No score is kept.

  • No pitching, walks or strikeouts: players swing until the ball is in play.
  • No outs and no umpires.
  • Six players bat each half-inning.
  • Games run 60 minutes or 3 innings.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft. Players are placed on teams.

  • Registration asks for a coach request and a teammate request. Both are optional. They aren’t guaranteed, but we make every effort to place players by those requests first, then with siblings, then by school.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form mid-March.
  • Practices start around Mar 29.
  • Games start around Apr 3 and run to mid-June.
  • Last season: Saturday games at Upper Ross. Weeknight practices there or at Upper Woodland.

Kindy Coach Pitch

A step up from Tee Ball, with a moving ball and real outs.

  • League ages: 6–7
  • Format: Coach pitch; tee as backup
  • Schedule: One weeknight practice; Saturday game
  • Fields: Mostly Upper Ross

Game Format

A slightly more challenging game than Tee Ball, adapted for kids at different skill levels.

  • Coaches pitch, with occasional tee use as a fallback.
  • Players make real outs.
  • Rules adapt through the season.
  • Score is kept.

Ready for Kindy?

Kindy is for players with a year of Tee Ball, or who are already 6. League age 6 is the minimum. Still 5 with a summer birthday and haven’t played? Tee Ball is the place to start. Signing up for Kindy doesn’t place your player there. Questions? Email the Registrar.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft. Players are placed on teams.

  • Registration asks for a coach request and a teammate request. Both are optional. They aren’t guaranteed, but we make every effort to place players by those requests first, then with siblings, then by school.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form mid-March.
  • Practices start around Mar 27.
  • Until games start, Saturdays are a second practice.
  • Games start around Apr 3 and run to mid-June.
  • Last season: Saturday games at Upper Ross. Weeknight practices there or at Upper Woodland.

A - Coach Pitch

The next step after Tee Ball or Kindy.

  • League ages: 7–8
  • Format: Coach & machine pitch; RIF5 hardball
  • Schedule: One weeknight practice; Saturday game
  • Fields: BF Day and Lower Ross

Game Format

Real baseball, at a pace built for 7’s and 8’s. No score is kept.

  • Three swinging strikes is an out. There are no walks.
  • Five-run limit per inning; 75-minute games.
  • Real base running: doubles and triples count.
  • Everyone plays both infield and outfield.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft. 6’s with a year of Tee Ball are welcome.

  • Registration asks for a coach request and a teammate request. Both are optional. They aren’t guaranteed, but we make every effort to place players by those requests first, then with siblings, then by school.
  • Teams are kept even, so games stay fun.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form the first week of March.
  • Practices start around Mar 13.
  • Games start around Mar 27 and run to mid-June.
  • Last two seasons: games and practices at BF Day and Lower Ross.

AA - Coach/Player Pitch

Where kids start pitching.

  • League ages: 8–9
  • Format: Kid pitch; coach pitch at ball four
  • Schedule: Fri & Sat: one game, one practice
  • Fields: The Cloverleaf; some at Lower Ross

Game Format

Score is kept; playoffs end the season.

  • No walks: at ball four, a coach pitches to that batter.
  • No stealing until midseason, then only on passed balls and wild pitches.
  • Five-run limit per inning; 90-minute games.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft. 9’s not drafted to AAA play here.

  • Registration asks for a coach request and a teammate request. Both are optional. They aren’t guaranteed, but we make every effort to place players by those requests first, then with siblings, then by school.
  • Teams are kept even, so games stay fun.
  • Top AA players form a AAA call-up pool.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form the first week of March.
  • Practices start around Mar 13.
  • Games start around Mar 27 and run to mid-June.
  • Fridays and Saturdays; game day changes year to year.
  • Last two seasons: nearly everything at The Cloverleaf, with a few games at Lower Ross.

Volunteer

AAA

For 10’s and 11’s, plus 9’s who assess in. Registering doesn’t guarantee a spot.

  • League ages: 9–11
  • Format: Full kid pitch
  • Schedule: Mon, Wed & Sat
  • Fields: Mostly The Cloverleaf

Game Format

Score is kept; playoffs end the season.

  • 46-ft mound and 60-ft bases; no new inning after 2 hours.
  • Five-run limit per half-inning, with some late-game exceptions.
  • Mercy rule: up 15 after 3 innings, 10 after 4, or 8 after 5.
  • Stealing is allowed, but no delayed steals.
  • Every player plays at least 9 defensive outs.

Assessments & Draft

A draft keeps teams evenly matched.

  • Assessments: around Sat, Jan 23. Required, with no make-up date.
  • Coaches draft from the scores; the order is drawn from a hat.
  • Coaches’ kids are placed by score.
  • 9’s not drafted to AAA play AA.
  • The top 15–20 form the Majors call-up pool.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form around Feb 13–20.
  • Practices start around Mar 1.
  • Games start around Mar 20 and run to early June.
  • Last two seasons: games at The Cloverleaf, a few away. Practices there or at Bitter Lake or Green Lake.

Majors

For 12’s and top 11’s. Registering as an 11 doesn’t guarantee a spot.

  • League ages: 12’s, top 11’s, a few returning All-Star 10’s
  • Format: Full kid pitch
  • Schedule: Tue, Thu & Sat
  • Fields: Mostly The Cloverleaf

Game Format

Score is kept; playoffs end the season.

  • 46-ft mound and 60-ft bases; no new inning after 2 hours 15 minutes.
  • No run limit per inning.
  • Mercy rule: up 15 after 3 innings, 10 after 4, or 8 after 5.
  • Stealing once the ball reaches the batter; no leadoffs.
  • Every player plays at least 9 defensive outs.

Assessments & Draft

A draft keeps teams evenly matched.

  • Assessments: around Sat, Jan 23. Required, with no make-up date.
  • Every 12 plays Majors.
  • Coaches draft from the scores; the order is drawn from a hat.
  • Coaches’ kids are placed by score.
  • 11’s and 10’s not drafted play AAA; the top 15–20 AAA players form the call-up pool.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form around Feb 13–20.
  • Practices start around Mar 2.
  • Games start around Mar 20 and run to early June.
  • Last season: home games at the Cloverleaf. Away games at Hamlin Park, Bayview, Maple Leaf and West Queen Anne; some playoff games at Soundview.

Intermediate (50/70)

New for 2027: the 50/70 step between Majors and Juniors.

  • League ages: 13
  • Format: Full kid pitch on a 50/70 field
  • Schedule: To be set
  • Fields: Eagle Staff, Loyal Heights, Lower Woodland or Dahl

Game Format

Score is kept; a District 8 tournament ends the season.

  • Leadoffs, balks and an on-deck batter, like the big field.
  • Seven-inning games; no new inning after 2 hours 15 minutes.
  • Mercy rule: up 15 after 4 innings, 10 after 5, or 8 after 6.
  • Every player plays at least 9 defensive outs.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft. Spots go in sign-up order, up to 18 players, then a waitlist.

  • Intermediate isn’t guaranteed: it needs a coach, a 50/70 field and enough players.
  • If it doesn’t come together, your player can move to Juniors or get a refund.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form in early March.
  • Practices start around mid-March.
  • Games start in late March and run to mid-June.
  • New in 2027, so there’s no field history yet.

Junior Division

The full 90-foot field, for 13’s and 14’s.

  • League ages: 13–14
  • Format: Full kid pitch on a 90-ft field
  • Schedule: Mostly weeknight games
  • Fields: Lower Woodland #1

Game Format

Score is kept; District 8 holds a wood-bat tournament after the season.

  • 60′6″ mound and 90-ft bases; leadoffs, balks and an on-deck batter.
  • Seven-inning games; no new inning after 2 hours 15 minutes.
  • Mercy rule: up 15 after 4 innings, 10 after 5, or 8 after 6.
  • Every player plays at least 9 defensive outs.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft.

  • NCLL fields its own team when enough players sign up (one team of 19 in 2026).
  • Otherwise we partner with a nearby league, as we did with RUG in 2024.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form around mid-February.
  • Practices start in early March.
  • Games start in late March and run to late May.
  • Last season: home games at Lower Woodland #1. Away at Shorewood HS, Northshore, Whitman, West Queen Anne and Loyal Heights.

Senior Division

For 15’s and 16’s, built around high school baseball.

  • League ages: 15–16
  • Format: Full kid pitch on a 90-ft field
  • Schedule: May to late June
  • Fields: Set by the partner league

Game Format

A District tournament ends the season.

  • Teams may bat nine, with a designated hitter.
  • Seven-inning games; no new inning after 2 hours 15 minutes.
  • Mercy rule: up 15 after 4 innings, 10 after 5, or 8 after 6.
  • Every player gets at least 9 defensive outs and an at-bat.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft.

  • Registration is a waitlist that stays open for high school timing; players are admitted in waitlist order.
  • NCLL usually partners with nearby leagues to form a team, as it did in 2025 and 2026.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Sign up through April; teams form in late April.
  • Games run from May to late June.
  • The District tournament starts in late June.
  • Last season: practices included Bitter Lake; games were set by the partner league.

Volunteer

Seniors runs on families who coach, keep score and help with the fields. Want to help? Visit the Volunteer Hub. Volunteers complete a background check, required by Little League International and applicable law.
