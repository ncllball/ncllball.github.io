North Central Little League offers baseball for players ages 5-16, from first-time players through high school age. Baseball is open to boys and girls; for girls-only play, see our softball divisions.

How to choose: Divisions go by your child’s League Age, their age on August 31, 2027. The cards below cover schedules, fields and what makes each division different. Not sure? Email the Registrar.

Registration opens Monday, November 2. Start with the 2027 Registration guide and Key Dates.

AAA and Majors: Assessments are estimated for Saturday, January 23, with teams formed by about February 20. Please sign up and plan on attending, because placement comes from assessments.

Ready for a higher division? Signing up for a division doesn’t place your player there. Below AAA, the Registrar and division coordinators decide, so email the Registrar.

Coaches: rules and training materials are in the Coaches Hub.

Baseball Divisions

Division rules: tap the rules book on any division's card for that division's rules of play.

Tee Ball

For 5’s and 6’s, typically in Kindergarten or their last year of pre-school.

  • League ages: 5–6
  • Format: Tee only, with a safety ball
  • Schedule: One weeknight practice; Saturday game
  • Fields: Mostly Upper Ross

Game Format

First swings and throws, in a fun, confidence-building setup. No score is kept.

  • No pitching, walks or strikeouts: players swing until the ball is in play.
  • No outs and no umpires.
  • Six players bat each half-inning.
  • Games run 60 minutes or 3 innings.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft. Players are placed on teams.

  • Registration asks for a coach request and a teammate request. Both are optional. They aren’t guaranteed, but we make every effort to place players by those requests first, then with siblings, then by school.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form mid-March.
  • Practices start around Mar 29.
  • Games start around Apr 3 and run to mid-June.
  • Last season: Saturday games at Upper Ross. Weeknight practices there or at Upper Woodland.

Kindy Coach Pitch

A step up from Tee Ball, with a moving ball and real outs.

  • League ages: 6–7
  • Format: Coach pitch; tee as backup
  • Schedule: One weeknight practice; Saturday game
  • Fields: Mostly Upper Ross

Game Format

A slightly more challenging game than Tee Ball, adapted for kids at different skill levels.

  • Coaches pitch, with occasional tee use as a fallback.
  • Players make real outs.
  • Rules adapt through the season.
  • Score is kept.

Ready for Kindy?

Kindy is for players with a year of Tee Ball, or who are already 6. League age 6 is the minimum. Still 5 with a summer birthday and haven’t played? Tee Ball is the place to start. Signing up for Kindy doesn’t place your player there. Questions? Email the Registrar.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft. Players are placed on teams.

  • Registration asks for a coach request and a teammate request. Both are optional. They aren’t guaranteed, but we make every effort to place players by those requests first, then with siblings, then by school.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form mid-March.
  • Practices start around Mar 27.
  • Until games start, Saturdays are a second practice.
  • Games start around Apr 3 and run to mid-June.
  • Last season: Saturday games at Upper Ross. Weeknight practices there or at Upper Woodland.

A - Coach Pitch

The next step after Tee Ball or Kindy.

  • League ages: 7–8
  • Format: Coach & machine pitch; RIF5 hardball
  • Schedule: One weeknight practice; Saturday game
  • Fields: BF Day and Lower Ross

Game Format

Real baseball, at a pace built for 7’s and 8’s. No score is kept.

  • Three swinging strikes is an out. There are no walks.
  • Five-run limit per inning; 75-minute games.
  • Real base running: doubles and triples count.
  • Everyone plays both infield and outfield.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft. 6’s with a year of Tee Ball are welcome.

  • Registration asks for a coach request and a teammate request. Both are optional. They aren’t guaranteed, but we make every effort to place players by those requests first, then with siblings, then by school.
  • Teams are kept even, so games stay fun.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form the first week of March.
  • Practices start around Mar 13.
  • Games start around Mar 27 and run to mid-June.
  • Last two seasons: games and practices at BF Day and Lower Ross.

Volunteer

AA - Coach/Player Pitch

Where kids start pitching.

  • League ages: 8–9
  • Format: Coach pitch, then player pitch
  • Schedule: Fri & Sat: one game, one practice
  • Fields: The Cloverleaf; some at Lower Ross

Game Format

Score is kept; playoffs end the season.

  • Coaches pitch early; kids take the mound by midseason.
  • Three straight walks or hit batters, and a coach finishes the inning.
  • No stealing until midseason, then only on passed balls and wild pitches.
  • Five-run limit per inning; 90-minute games.

Team Placement

No assessments or draft. 9’s not drafted to AAA play here.

  • Registration asks for a coach request and a teammate request. Both are optional. They aren’t guaranteed, but we make every effort to place players by those requests first, then with siblings, then by school.
  • Teams are kept even, so games stay fun.
  • Top AA players form a AAA call-up pool.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form the first week of March.
  • Practices start around Mar 13.
  • Games start around Mar 27 and run to mid-June.
  • Fridays and Saturdays; game day changes year to year.
  • Last two seasons: nearly everything at The Cloverleaf, with a few games at Lower Ross.

Volunteer

AAA

For 10’s and 11’s, plus 9’s who assess in. Registering doesn’t guarantee a spot.

  • League ages: 9–11
  • Format: Full player pitch
  • Schedule: Mon, Wed & Sat
  • Fields: Mostly The Cloverleaf

Game Environment

The first division with full player pitch, played on the full rules with a few limits for age.

  • 46-ft mound and 60-ft bases.
  • Five-run limit per half-inning, with some late-game exceptions.
  • No new inning after 2 hours.

Assessments & Draft

A draft keeps teams evenly matched.

  • Assessments: around Sat, Jan 23. Required, with no make-up date.
  • Coaches draft from the scores; the order is drawn from a hat.
  • Coaches’ kids are placed by score.
  • 9’s not drafted to AAA play AA.
  • The top 15–20 form the Majors call-up pool.

Season Rhythm

Dates and fields are approximate.

  • Teams form around Feb 13–20.
  • Practices start around Mar 1.
  • Games start around Mar 20 and run to mid-June.
  • Last two seasons: games at The Cloverleaf, a few away. Practices there or at Bitter Lake or Green Lake.

Majors

The Majors Division features all 12-year-olds, top 11s, and a few returning All-Star 10s who are ready for Little League's highest tempo.

  • League ages: 12s + top 11s (returning All-Star 10s)
  • Format: Player pitch; LL rules + Local D8
  • Schedule: Tue practice; Thu & Sat games
  • Fields: BF Day or The Cloverleaf

Assessments & Draft

Focus: blend varsity-style competition with instruction so players sharpen mechanics, decision-making, and leadership ahead of all-stars and junior baseball.

Tryouts ensure every roster has arms, bats, and leaders ready for the full Majors slate.

  • All 10s/11s attend tryouts to be draft eligible.
  • Every 12-year-old participates to finalize rosters.
  • Makeup sessions keep late signups in the draft pool.

Competitive Environment

Majors layers high-stakes moments on top of continued instruction so development never stops.

  • Managers call full offensive/defensive playbooks.
  • Game-speed reps reinforce fundamentals and baseball IQ.
  • Minimum play rules keep innings and at-bats balanced.

Player Development Goals

Consistency is the goal—pitching, hitting, and situational defense all receive weekly focus.

  • Pitchers refine command, secondary pitches, and arm care.
  • Hitters own the zone, master situational bunting, and track quality ABs.
  • Defenders develop communication, cut systems, and double-play footwork.

Roster Construction

Managers draft using tryout data plus prior-season notes to keep parity across the league.

  • Pitching and catching depth spreads evenly among teams.
  • Coach feedback informs player roles and development plans.
  • Tryout attendance (or approved makeup) required.

Season Rhythm

Expect three to four touchpoints most weeks with built-in recovery windows around tournaments and holidays.

  • Tuesday practice plus Thursday and Saturday games, with optional makeups.
  • In-season sessions blend arm care, defensive installs, and situational work.
  • Additional scrimmages prep all-star hopefuls.

Fields & Next Steps

Majors teams primarily use the Lower Woodland Cloverleaf, with BF Day available for extra bullpens and cage work.

  • Friday lights or Monday makeups keep the schedule balanced.
  • Volunteer help with scorekeeping and pitch charts is always welcome.

Intermediate (50/70)

Intermediate (50/70) bridges the Majors diamond and the full 90-foot field. New for 2027 and open to league-age 13s; it runs if we find a coach, a 50/70 field and enough players.

  • League ages: 13 (2027)
  • Format: 70' bases; 50' mound; D8 interleague
  • Schedule: TBD
  • Fields: Lower Woodland practices; games at 50/70 fields (Robert Eagle Staff, Loyal Heights, Lower Woodland #1, Dahl)

Ballpark Dimensions & Mechanics

The 50/70 field is a step up from Majors in size and in the rules that come with it.

  • Bases are 70 feet apart and the mound is 50 feet from home.
  • Runners can lead off, and pitchers can be called for balks.
  • Games use a Senior Division ball; bats can be USABat or BBCOR, up to 34 inches.

Pitching & Safety

Pitch counts and catching limits protect arms through a longer game.

  • Up to 70 pitches a game before May 1, then Little League's daily limits by age.
  • A catcher who catches more than 3 innings can't pitch that day.

District Competition

Intermediate teams play District 8 interleague games under the district's Intermediate rules.

  • Games are seven innings, with no new inning after 2 hours 15 minutes.
  • On school nights, no new inning starts after 9:15 pm.

Junior Division

The Junior Division is built for league-age 13-14 players, mirroring prep baseball dimensions and pace.

  • League ages: 13-14
  • Format: 90' bases; D8 Schedule; drafted teams
  • Schedule: Tue/Thu games; Sun practice
  • Fields: Greenlake or Lower Woodland #1

Ballpark Dimensions & Mechanics

Focus: elevate varsity-level mechanics, strategy, and leadership while competing across District 8.

Junior teams play on a full 90' diamond, matching high school rules and the physical demands that come with it.

  • Pitchers work from 60'6" with varsity-style pitch counts.
  • Defenders refine long throws, deeper cuts, and communication.

Playing Time & Safety Standards

Even with varsity-level competition, NCLL safeguards participation and workload balance.

  • Minimum-play rules ensure every roster spot sees action.
  • Pitch count and catching guidelines manage arm and knee health.

District Competition

Junior teams represent NCLL in District 8 play, facing peers from across North Seattle.

  • Regular opponents include Ballard, Queen Anne, Magnolia, Richmond, Northwest, and Northeast Seattle.
  • Regular season sets seeds for District and All-Star tournaments.

Assessments & Draft

All athletes attend tryouts; every player lands on a roster via the Junior Division draft.

  • Tryouts slot players so managers can balance pitching and key positions.
  • Snake draft keeps parity front and center.
  • Approved makeups keep late signups eligible.

Fields & Travel Notes

Green Lake and Lower Woodland #1 host most practices, with occasional nights at Bitter Lake or Magnuson.

  • Expect travel across District 8 for Friday or Sunday showcase games.
  • Coaches share scouting reports and logistics early so families can plan carpools.

Senior Division

The Senior Division is built for league-age 15-16 players who want Little League reps alongside high school baseball.

  • League ages: 15-16
  • Format: 90' bases; D8 schedule; draft parity
  • Schedule: Mon/Wed games; Sun practice
  • Season notes: NCLL registration mirrors HS timelines
  • Fields: Greenlake or Lower Woodland #1

Ballpark Dimensions

Focus: maintain varsity-level conditioning, keep arms on safe workloads, and align with high school timelines.

Seniors play on a full 90' field with varsity distances and timing.

  • Matches high school mound, base paths, and fence dimensions.
  • Prepares players for summer travel and prep tryouts.

Playing Time & Safety Standards

Even at the oldest level, NCLL maintains participation guarantees and workload guardrails.

  • Minimum-play requirements ensure equal opportunities.
  • Pitching guidelines align with high school rest expectations.

District Competition

Senior teams compete across District 8, keeping travel local while elevating the level of play.

  • Opponents include Ballard, Queen Anne, Magnolia, Richmond, Northwest, and Northeast Seattle.
  • Regular season feeds directly into District tournament play.

Assessments & Draft

Tryouts pace players into the draft; every athlete lands on a roster with balanced pitching depth.

  • Managers evaluate hitting, defense, and pitching at tryouts.
  • Snake draft keeps parity across staffs and key positions.

Fields & Travel Notes

Practices are anchored at Green Lake and Lower Woodland #1, with occasional sessions at Bitter Lake or Magnuson.

  • Travel stays within Seattle for league play.
  • Coaches coordinate carpool options and scouting notes.

High School Coordination

Most Seniors athletes juggle high school baseball; the league stays flexible to keep them on the field.

  • Registration stays open to accommodate high-school cut timelines.
  • Coaches coordinate with families around conflicting game nights.
  • Contact the league with roster or scheduling questions.
