2027 Assessments

Not tryouts: every player makes a team. Assessments help us place players and build even teams for the draft.

  • Baseball: AAA and Majors, Sat, Jan 23 (est.)
  • Softball: AAA and Majors, Sat, Feb 6 (est.)
  • Status: Planning

Winterball27 BB AAA/Majors Training

Small-group indoor sessions to keep rising AAA and Majors players sharp over the winter: movement prep, hitting and conditioning.

  • Where: Focus or The Hive (planned)
  • When: Mon & Thu evenings and more; dates TBA (last year: Nov 3 – Jan 26)
  • Division: Baseball (rising AAA & Majors, league ages 9–12)
  • Cost: TBA (last year: $260 / player)
  • Status: Planning
  • How it works: weekly small groups (9–12 players). Pick your day when you register.
  • Led by: NCLL volunteer coaches, with parent helpers.
  • Breaks: none Thanksgiving week or Winter Break.
  • Bring: glove, bat, helmet and water.
  • Questions: Jay Tufte

Winterball27 BB A & AA Fundamentals

A fundamentals clinic for A and AA baseball: throwing, catching and fielding, to start the spring with good habits.

  • Where: Wallingford Boys & Girls Club
  • When: Two weekends; dates TBA (last year: Feb 28 & Mar 7)
  • Division: Baseball (rising A & AA, league ages 7–9)
  • Cost: TBA (last year: $20 / session)
  • Status: Planning
  • How it works: one-hour blocks, A then AA. Each session is its own sign-up (up to 15 players, then a waitlist).
  • Led by: Mark Linden (Baseball Positive), a former NCAA D-I head coach.
  • Parents: stay and join the drills; everyone gets an instruction guide.
  • Bring: glove, indoor shoes (no cleats) and water.
  • Questions: Olin Wick

Winterball27 BB Free February (Outdoor)

Free Saturday turf sessions for AAA and Majors players: station-based reps to build arms and knock off rust before teams form.

  • Where: Robert Eagle Staff MS turf
  • When: February Saturdays, TBA (last year: Feb 7, 14 & 21, 6–8 PM)
  • Division: Baseball (rising AAA & Majors, league ages 9–12)
  • Cost: Free
  • Status: Planning
  • How it works: station-based reps run by NCLL volunteer coaches (coaches sign up too). Sign up for each Saturday; only claim a spot you'll use.
  • Weather: rain or shine unless unsafe; dress in layers.
  • Bring: glove, water, cleats or athletic shoes (no metal spikes) and a batting helmet (a few loaners).
  • Questions: VP of Baseball

Winterball27 Softball

Winter softball training is being planned: pitching, hitting, throwing and fielding reps to get players ready for the spring.

  • When: TBA (last year: Saturdays, Jan 3 – Mar 7)
  • Division: Softball
  • Cost: TBA (last year: free)
  • Status: Planning
  • How it works: Saturday blocks with a cap on each one so every player gets plenty of reps. Come to as many as you can.
  • Parents and coaches: welcome to help; you can sign up to volunteer when you register.
  • Bring: glove, pitching gear if you pitch, indoor shoes (no cleats) and water.
  • Questions: softball@ncllball.com

Winterball27 BB Teen Batting

Indoor winter sessions for league-age 13–16 players: hitting pattern work, athletic movement and arm care before school-ball tryouts.

  • When: TBA (last year: Nov 9 – Feb 22)
  • Division: Baseball (Teen, 13–16)
  • Cost: TBA (last year: $250 / player)
  • Status: On hold
  • How it works: one small group (up to 12 players) meeting weekly in the batting cages at Focus.
  • Bring: bat, glove, turf or indoor shoes and water.
  • Interested? Email the registrar to get on the list.
