Assessments are required for AAA and Majors, in both baseball and softball. They’re how we build balanced teams and find the right division for each player. They aren’t tryouts: every player makes a team.

Details for 2027 are still being set. Assessment sign-ups will open here. Dates below are estimates until then, and there’s no make-up date, so please plan around them.

League age 9’s can register for AAA, but it doesn’t guarantee a spot. 9’s fill remaining roster spots based on assessment scores. Those not drafted to AAA play AA.

Volunteers

Helping with check-in, warm-ups and station timing keeps assessments moving and gets players more reps. To help, email baseball@ncllball.com or softball@ncllball.com.

What to bring

  • Glove, water and warm clothing.
  • Turf shoes, sneakers or cleats, with no metal spikes. Indoor locations need clean indoor shoes.
  • Helmet (loaners may be available). A bat is optional.
