Assessments are required for AAA and Majors in baseball, and for AAA, Majors and Juniors in softball. They’re how we build balanced teams and find the right division for each player. They aren’t tryouts: every player makes a team.

Details for 2027 are still being set. Sign-ups will open on this page. Dates below are estimates until then.

There’s no make-up date, so please plan around it. It isn’t a penalty: the day takes a lot of volunteers to put on, and it’s fairest when every player goes through it together. Players who miss are placed using last season’s coach evaluations.

Can’t make it? Please email baseball@ncllball.com (softball: softball@ncllball.com) to let us know. It won’t change how your player is placed, but hearing from you is always better than a no-show.

New to assessments? Read What to Expect, the FAQ and how the draft works.

Baseball: league age 9’s can register for AAA, but it doesn’t guarantee a spot. 9’s fill remaining roster spots based on assessment scores. Those not drafted to AAA play AA.

Parents are welcome and encouraged to stay. Timing on the day is fluid, so plan to be there until your player has finished every station.

Volunteers

Helping with check-in, warm-ups and station timing keeps assessments moving and gets players more reps. To help, email baseball@ncllball.com or softball@ncllball.com.

What to bring

  • Glove, water and warm clothing.
  • Turf shoes, sneakers or cleats, with no metal spikes. Indoor locations need clean indoor shoes.
  • Helmet (loaners may be available). A bat is optional.
