Assessments are required for AAA and Majors in baseball, and for AAA and Majors in softball. They’re how we build balanced teams and find the right division for each player. They aren’t tryouts: every player makes a team.

Details for 2027 are still being set. Sign-ups will open on this page, and we’ll email everyone registered for these divisions when they do. Dates below are estimates until then. Baseball AAA and Majors: please register by Sunday, January 17, so you’re set for assessments.

There’s no make-up date, so please plan around it. It isn’t a penalty: the day takes a lot of volunteers to put on, and it’s fairest when every player goes through it together. Players who miss are placed using last season’s coach evaluations. If you can’t make it, please email baseball@ncllball.com (softball: softball@ncllball.com). It won’t change how your player is placed, but a dialogue is always better than a no-show.

New to assessments? Read What to Expect, the FAQ and how the draft works.

Baseball: just because registration lets a league age 9 sign up for AAA doesn’t mean there’s a spot. 9’s fill any AAA roster spots left after the 10’s and 11’s, based on assessment scores. Those not drafted to AAA play AA.

Parents are welcome and encouraged to stay. Timing on the day is fluid, so plan to be there until your player has finished every station.

Volunteers

No baseball or softball experience needed. We need parents to help check players in, pin on bibs, and get each group to the right station at the right time. It keeps the day moving and gets players more reps. Volunteer sign-ups will open in the Schedule & sign-ups table above, where you pick a block of time.

Planning to coach AAA or Majors? Please also plan to take a volunteer block when volunteer sign-ups open above.

What to bring

Come ready to play, dressed and equipped like it’s a practice.

  • Glove, water and warm clothing.
  • Turf shoes, sneakers or cleats, with no metal spikes. Indoor locations need clean indoor shoes.
  • Helmet (loaners may be available). A bat is optional.
  • Catcher’s gear is provided. Players are welcome to bring their own.
