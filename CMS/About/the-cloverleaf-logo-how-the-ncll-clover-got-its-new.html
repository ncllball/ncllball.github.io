NCLL’s clover and badge came out of a deliberate, collaborative redesign in late 2024, launched in spring 2025. Here are the milestones that got us to the final badge.

Redesign Highlights

  1. Initiation: Identified the need for a contemporary, inclusive mark that represents both softball and baseball.
  2. Collaboration: Partnered with an NCLL parent designer while board members provided feedback along the way.
  3. Constraints: Ensured alignment with Little League requirements (no ball illustration, clarity at small sizes, cross-format flexibility).
  4. Concept Presentation (Nov 19, 2024): Three directions were shown: “Field of Dreams,” “Sandlot,” and “League of Our Own.”
  5. Direction Chosen: “Field of Dreams” advanced so the team could fine-tune color balance, gender representation, and scalability.
  6. Refinement: Two review cycles produced clean geometry, simplified line weights, and a standard palette (red, black, white).
  7. Final Approval (Dec 17, 2024): The Board confirmed the final badge along with its supporting lockups.

What Changed

  • Unified clover and letterform system that works across digital, apparel, and print.
  • Simplified color palette for easy reproduction on swag, signage, and web.
  • Improved legibility, especially at small sizes like social avatars or browser tabs.
  • Clear hierarchy: League Badge for formal use, Primary Clover for everyday gear, plus supporting variants.

Summary

The refresh balanced creativity, compliance, and community representation, producing a system that keeps the NCLL identity recognizable in every format.
