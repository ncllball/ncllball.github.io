## Questions for the board — quick list

Every genuine board-level question across all nine baseball divisions, pulled into one flat
list so it can be worked through without reading the full tracker. Each links to the fuller
discussion below or in its own division doc. Pure documentation gaps that don't need a
person's judgment (just more digging, or "presumably same as X") are **not** included here —
see the per-division sections below for those. Softball has its own separate list — see
`softball-open-board-decisions.md`.

**Roster size targets** (LL only sets outer bounds — 10–15 players, or up to 20 for a
division fielding only one team — the actual target is NCLL's call):
1. What's the roster size target for AAA, Juniors, and Seniors? (Majors already has one:
   11–13.)
2. What's the roster size target for A, AA, T-ball, and Kindy? Not set anywhere found for
   any of the four lower divisions either.

**Reporting & workflow, above AAA:**
3. Does Majors' pitch-count reporting workflow (home team records, teams compare each
   inning, exchange at the plate meeting, warning/1-game/season-suspension schedule for
   violations) also apply to AAA, Juniors, and Seniors — or does any of them need something
   different?
4. Does Majors' "no league-level score-reporting requirement, team-to-team only" answer also
   apply to AAA, Juniors, and Seniors?
5. Does the "home team supplies the game balls" convention (already true by rule at Majors/
   AAA) also apply at Intermediate, Junior, and Senior, where D8 doesn't actually say so?

**Rules that stop at Majors/AAA — should they extend upward?**
6. Does the bat-throwing warning-then-removal rule (an NCLL invention, not an LL/D8 rule)
   extend to Juniors and/or Seniors? In a co-league year, the partner league would also need
   to agree, not just NCLL's board.
7. Does a Juniors/Seniors call-up or pool-player mechanism exist, the way the Majors↔AAA
   Call-Up program does? No source found either way for either division.

**Majors:**
8. Has the board formally adopted dual-rostering (the Majors↔AAA Call-Up program) as NCLL's
   chosen local option under LL's dual-roster rule — or has it just grown into practice
   without a formal vote?
9. How should a dual-rostered Call-Up player's pitch count be aggregated across their AAA
   and Majors games on the same day? LL requires this (the daily limit is per player across
   all teams); no mechanism exists anywhere documented — this needs to be built, not just
   decided.

**AAA:**
10. Does AAA guarantee an equal number of interleague games against every other D8 AAA team,
    the way Majors explicitly does?
11. Does AAA keep standings? D8 doesn't track any; it's purely NCLL's call whether to.

**Intermediate:**
12. Does NCLL want to revisit running a real in-house Intermediate (50/70) division at all,
    instead of skipping straight from Majors to the Juniors co-league? A facility/coach
    question, not something any document can resolve.

**Senior — the single most consequential open item in the whole baseball set:**
13. Does NCLL's Senior team bat a Continuous Batting Order (like every other division) or a
    traditional bat-nine lineup with a Designated Hitter available? D8 allows either; nothing
    found documents which way NCLL has gone, and it affects mandatory-play and late-arrival
    handling too.
14. Is the Senior team's regular season the same RUG + Northwest Seattle partnership as its
    confirmed All-Star co-league, or a separate arrangement? Not confirmed either way.

**A / AA — team formation:**
15. Do A and AA have any formal team-formation or assessment process, or is placement purely
    registration-based? Not documented for either division.

**T-ball:**
16. Is there any end-of-T-ball-season event? Likely none, given the explicitly
    non-competitive framing, but not stated anywhere found.

**Kindy — the largest documentation gap in the whole project:**
17. What is Kindy's actual in-game format — innings or time-based, how outs work, whether
    score is kept, baserunning rules? Nothing found documents this at all; this needs a
    conversation with current/recent Kindy coaches, not more research.
18. What's the exact coach-pitch/tee-fallback mechanic at Kindy — how many pitches before
    falling back to the tee, and who decides?
19. Does NCLL's ASAP/insurance coverage extend to Kindy games? Not a rulebook question (Reg V
    doesn't reach a non-chartered offering), but a genuine ops item worth a registrar confirm.

**Minor copy fixes** (not board decisions, just page text worth a quick confirm or edit):
20. `registration-main.html`'s eligibility-note sentence names "AAA, Majors, or Junior
    division players" but not Senior, even though Senior tryouts clearly happen — oversight
    or intentional?
21. `registration-main.html` calls A Division "a coach-pitch and machine pitch blend," but
    `a.html`'s actual rules (confirmed current) describe machine pitch as primary with
    coach-pitch only as an equipment-failure backup — the registration page is the outlier
    and needs the fix.
22. AA's live page still says a pitcher needs "41 or more" pitches to trigger the

    Confirmed still live 2026-09-03.
23. AA's live "No Balks" bullet still literally reads "Balks are not called in **Tee Ball**"
    — a copy-paste leftover from the Tee Ball page. Confirmed still live 2026-09-03.

## Cross-division patterns worth answering once

A few items are the *same underlying question*, repeated per division because each doc was
built independently. Answering these once at the board level would close multiple rows
across multiple files simultaneously — worth doing before working the list division-by-division:

- **Pitch-count recording workflow** (baseball-majors-rules.md C3 mechanism already answered — home team
  is recorder, both teams compare each inning, exchange at the pre-game plate meeting;
  violations follow a warning/1-game/season-suspension schedule). **Does this same workflow
  apply to AAA (baseball-aaa-rules.md C3), Juniors (baseball-junior-rules.md C3), and Seniors (baseball-senior-rules.md C3)?** If the
  board just says "yes, same as Majors," four open items close at once. If any of them need
  something different, that's the real decision.
- **Score/results reporting** (F6 — baseball-majors-rules.md already answers "no league-level requirement,
  team-to-team only." Does the same "no requirement" answer apply to baseball-aaa-rules.md F6, baseball-junior-rules.md
  F6, and baseball-senior-rules.md F6? Likely yes, but hasn't been said out loud for those three.)
- **Roster size target** (baseball-majors-rules.md's 11–13 is the only division with an actual number.
  baseball-aaa-rules.md G4, baseball-junior-rules.md G4, and baseball-senior-rules.md G4 are all open. Worth deciding all four divisions'
  targets in one board conversation rather than four separate asks — LL's bound differs per
  division: Majors/AAA are 10–15; Juniors and Seniors are 10–15, or up to 20 if NCLL fields
  only one team in that division.)
- **Game-ball provision** (F5 — baseball-majors-rules.md/baseball-aaa-rules.md don't flag this as open since D8 states it
  for those two divisions; Intermediate, Junior, and Senior all have the same D8 omission
  and the same open item. One "home team supplies, same as everywhere else" answer likely
  closes all three at once.)
- **Bat-throwing rule extending upward** (D13 — an NCLL-invented Majors/AAA rule; whether it
  extends to Juniors and/or Seniors is open in both docs with the same reasoning. One board
  answer likely covers both, though a co-league year would still need the partner league's
  separate sign-off for whichever division is co-leagued that year.)
- **Call-up/pool mechanism above AAA** (G7 — baseball-junior-rules.md and baseball-senior-rules.md both ask whether
  anything like the Majors↔AAA Call-Up program exists heading into Juniors/Seniors. Neither
  has a source either way; likely the same answer for both.)

## Resolved cross-division, 2026-09-03 (no longer open anywhere)

- **NCLL's D8 grouping.** Confirmed NCLL is a D8 CITY league (home field Lower Woodland;
  grouped with Ballard/Magnolia/Northwest Seattle/Queen Anne/RUG/Northeast Seattle/
  Shoreline), not North-End (North Bothell/North Lake/Northshore/Woodinville). This closes
  the post-season/G9 question for both Majors and AAA — NCLL's non-participation in the
  North-End Tournament of Champions is structural, not a discretionary yearly choice.
- **Co-league partners are always City-group leagues.** Confirmed for both Juniors and
  Seniors — never a suburban North-End partner. Seniors' specific partnership (RUG +
  Northwest Seattle) is directly named in the 2026 D8 tournament brackets; Juniors' specific
  partnership isn't independently confirmed (see Junior section below) but the City-vs-
  suburban question itself is closed for both.
- **Bat standard (B4) — closed across aa.md/a.md/tball.md/kindy.md.** LL Rule 1.10:
  non-wood bats must bear the USABat mark, no BPF-1.15 bats, ≤33"/≤2⅝" barrel, wood
  ≥15/16" (≥7/8" under 30"); Tee Ball bats are marked "ONLY FOR USE WITH APPROVED TEE BALLS."
  One citation, one board decision (already made — this is just the rulebook baseline),
  applies identically to all four lower divisions.
- **Minor League age structure (A1/C1) — closed across aa.md/a.md.** Reg V: "local league
  establishes the age structure for the Minor League Division"; Reg VIII(a)/(d) sets the
  7–12 national band. NCLL's A=7-8/AA=8-9 split is this authority in action, not a gap.
- **Machine-pitch operation (C1) — closed in a.md.** Reg VIII(d) quoted in full is the
  *entire* rulebook machine-pitch spec (working order + adult-approved operator, nothing on
  velocity/distance/model) — confirms NCLL's UPM 45/speed/arc detail is entirely local.
- **Tee Ball age (A1) — closed in tball.md.** Reg V's Tee Ball text quoted in full; NCLL's
  league-age-5 local minimum reconfirmed as valid "local league establishes the age
  structure" authority.
- **AA D14/D6 corrections.** D14 — Rule 7.08(a)(4) ("Little League (Major) and below only")
  actually does reach AA/Minor League; an earlier draft wrongly said no equivalent rule
  existed. D6 — dropped-third-strike-OFF is the LL *default* at Minor League (Rule 6.09(b)
  is scoped to Majors/Intermediate/Junior/Senior only), not just an NCLL local choice.
- **Kindy Reg V question — closed.** Regulation V has no bearing on Kindy (non-chartered,
  doesn't touch LL eligibility tracking). The only adjacent question is an NCLL ops one
  (ASAP/insurance coverage) — see Kindy section below.
