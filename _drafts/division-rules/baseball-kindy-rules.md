## A. Division identity

### A1. Age eligibility (league age)

- **LL Rulebook:** n/a — Kindy isn't a chartered LL division, so LL doesn't set an age floor/ceiling for it directly.
- **D8 Interleague:** n/a.
- **NCLL:** League age 6 required, per Little League's own age-eligibility rules (Kindy borrows LL's league-age framework even though it isn't itself chartered). Confirmed live: "This division allows league age 6 kids an opportunity to play." Registration page footnote: "League age 6 required per Little League rules." Framed as a step above T-ball, not a replacement for it — "Requires one year of tee ball experience or coach approval" for team selection (see G1).
- **Notes:** Cite: Programs/Baseball/kindy.html + _Registration/registration-main.html (footnote 2 — "Kindergarten Coach Pitch (KCP, 6-7) moves at a faster pace than T-ball — live coach pitching with the tee available as a fallback. League age 6 required per Little League rules. Played on the T-ball field."). Registration age band shown is 6-7, birth-date range Sept 1, 2019 – Aug 31, 2021.

### A2. Purpose / what this division develops

- **NCLL:** "This division allows league age 6 kids an opportunity to play a slightly more challenging game with a moving ball and actual outs, but is also adaptive for kids at varying skill levels." Explicitly a coordination/readiness bridge: "Parents considering signing up their player for this division should consider whether the kid has the coordination, attention span for hit balls, and emotional maturity to handle outs." Two seasons piloted before this was written (2024-2025-ish, per "piloted for 2 years" as of the source page).
- **Notes:** Cite: Programs/Baseball/kindy.html (intro paragraph). See T:\project_kcp_criteria.md (Tower/homelab memory) for additional eligibility/selection criteria context if useful — not independently re-verified against a live NCLL source in this pass.

### A3. Participation in two divisions

- **NCLL:** n/a — no mechanism found, and the age/readiness framing (see A2) argues against a Kindy player also playing up into T-ball or A in the same season.

## B. Field & equipment

### B1. Base path distance

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** n/a.
- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated on kindy.html. Played "on the T-ball field" per the registration footnote — likely inherits T-ball's field setup (see baseball-tball-rules.md B1), but not confirmed independently for Kindy.

### B2. Pitching distance & mound type

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated on kindy.html (no distance given for coach-pitch position). Likely similar to T-ball's field/mound setup since Kindy plays on the T-ball field — not confirmed.

### B3. Ball standard

- **NCLL:** "Coach pitch with a safety ball, occasional tee use." A soft/safety ball, not a hardball — consistent with T-ball's "Safe-T-Ball," though kindy.html doesn't name a specific product the way tball.html does.
- **Notes:** Cite: Programs/Baseball/kindy.html ("Introduction to Hitting and Fielding" — "Coach pitch with a safety ball, occasional tee use").

### B4. Bat standard

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03.** LL Rule 1.10 (Minor/Major Divisions): non-wood bats must bear the USABat (USA Baseball) mark; all BPF-1.15 bats are prohibited; ≤33 inches, ≤2⅝-inch barrel; wood bats ≥15/16″ (≥7/8″ for bats under 30″). Tee Ball bats specifically carry a "ONLY FOR USE WITH APPROVED TEE BALLS" mark and may be used in Coach/Machine Pitch with approved tee balls. Same citation closes this row across baseball-aa-rules.md/baseball-a-rules.md/baseball-tball-rules.md B4. LL doesn't police a non-chartered offering like Kindy directly, but there's no reason the practical standard would differ once a player swings a real bat.
- **NCLL:** Not independently stated on kindy.html; the same practical fitting guidance A/AA/Tee Ball use almost certainly applies (see baseball-tball-rules.md B4), but not confirmed independently for Kindy specifically.

### B5. Home-run distance / outfield line

- **NCLL:** n/a — not a meaningful concept at this level; not mentioned on kindy.html.

### B6. Helmets / protective equipment

- **LL Rulebook:** Same NOCSAE rules as every other division apply once helmets are worn (rulebook-wide).
- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated on kindy.html specifically; likely follows the same practical guidance as tball.html/aa.html (helmet inspection, lice concerns, player-owned equipment at coach's discretion) — not confirmed independently for Kindy.

## C. Pitching

### C1. Pitching format — the core Kindy decision

- **LL Rulebook:** n/a (Kindy isn't chartered, so Reg VIII's Coach-Pitch/Machine-Pitch local-option language doesn't technically apply to it the way it does to A/AA — though NCLL's design clearly borrows the same coach-pitch concept).
- **NCLL:** **Coach pitch, with occasional tee use as a fallback** — this is Kindy's defining mechanic, distinguishing it from both T-ball (tee only, no pitching at all) and A/AA (which use pitching machines and/or full coach-pitch at a fixed distance). "Adaptive rules through the season to keep the play challenging but appropriate" — implies the coach-pitch/tee balance shifts as the season progresses, though the exact mechanism isn't spelled out.
- **Notes:** Cite: Programs/Baseball/kindy.html ("Introduction to Hitting and Fielding"). ⚠ The exact mechanics of the coach-pitch/tee-fallback decision (who decides mid-at-bat to switch to the tee, how many pitches before falling back) aren't documented — see Open board decisions.

### C2. Coach/machine mechanics

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not documented beyond "coach pitch with a safety ball, occasional tee use." No stated pitch count before falling back to the tee, no stated pitching distance, no stated walk/HBP handling. Genuine gap — see Open board decisions.

### C3–C5. Pitch counts, catcher restrictions, walks/balks

- **NCLL:** n/a — no pitch-count, catcher-restriction, or balk concept applies at this level; not mentioned on kindy.html.

## D. Game play

### D1–D15 (innings, time limits, mercy rule, dropped third strike, stealing, bunting, batter's box, on-deck, courtesy runner, infield fly, bat throwing, sliding, malicious contact)

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** n/a throughout.
- **NCLL:** ⚠ **None of this is documented for Kindy.** kindy.html is a program overview (age/readiness criteria, team-selection factors, schedule, location) — it doesn't describe game mechanics like innings, scoring, outs, or baserunning at all. This is the single biggest gap in this doc: a family or new coach reading kindy.html has no way to know how a Kindy game is actually structured game-to-game. Likely inherits something close to T-ball's format (no outs recorded, no walks, batting-order-clears-the-bases mechanic — see baseball-tball-rules.md D-section) given "actual outs" is specifically called out as what distinguishes Kindy FROM T-ball ("a slightly more challenging game with a moving ball and actual outs") — implying outs ARE recorded at Kindy, unlike T-ball. Beyond that inference, nothing is documented.
- **Notes:** » NCLL DECISION: document Kindy's actual in-game format (innings or time-based, how outs work, whether/how scoring is kept) — currently exists only as institutional knowledge among Kindy coaches, not written down anywhere found.

## G. Season structure

### G1. Team formation

- **NCLL:** Teams are formed by: coach/teammate requests, sibling placement, school affiliation, PLUS a readiness gate — **"requires one year of tee ball experience or coach approval."** This is not a draft or assessment-based process the way AAA/Majors/Junior/Senior are — it's a request-and-approval model, closer to T-ball's informal team formation.
- **Notes:** Cite: Programs/Baseball/kindy.html ("Team Selection Criteria").

### G2. Assessments / tryouts

- **NCLL:** No formal assessment — team selection is criteria-based (see G1), and eligibility itself has a built-in readiness check (see A2: coordination, attention span, emotional maturity for handling outs) rather than a competitive tryout.

### G3. Playing up / down

- **NCLL:** The "one year of tee ball experience OR coach approval" clause (see G1) functions as Kindy's playing-up gate — a player without prior T-ball experience can still join with a coach's sign-off. No formal appeal process documented.

### G4. Roster size target

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not documented.

### G5. Practices / start dates

- **NCLL:** Pre-season: 2 practices/week. Once the season begins: 1 weeknight practice + 1 Saturday game per week — a lighter cadence than every other division. Registration page confirms 2 events/week, "Any/Sa" scheduling, team formation mid-March, practice starts late March, games start mid-to-late April, season ends mid-June, fee $155.
- **Notes:** Cite: Programs/Baseball/kindy.html ("Practice and Game Schedule") + _Registration/registration-main.html (age-matrix table, KCP row + footnote 2).

### G6. Schedule type

- **NCLL:** Entirely in-house — no interleague, no D8 involvement at this level (see header note).

### G7. Player pool / call-ups

- **NCLL:** n/a.

### G8. Standings

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not documented; given the developmental framing, almost certainly none, but not stated explicitly for Kindy.

### G9. Post-season

### G10. All-Star / tournament team

## Open board decisions

*(Also tracked in `open-board-decisions.md` — update both together.)*

Kindy has by far the most undocumented rows of any division doc so far, because
`Programs/Baseball/kindy.html` is explicitly a program overview, not a rules-of-play page.
Most of the D-section (game mechanics) and half of E/F are genuine gaps, not decisions with
a knowable-but-unfound answer:

1. **C1/C2 — Exact coach-pitch/tee-fallback mechanics.** How many pitches before falling
   back to the tee, who decides, whether this differs early vs. late season ("adaptive
   rules through the season").
2. **D-section, whole — In-game format.** Innings or time-based, how outs work, whether
   score is kept, baserunning rules. This is the single biggest documentation gap in the
   whole division-rules project so far — likely exists as institutional knowledge among
   Kindy coaches but isn't written down anywhere found.
3. **E-section, whole — Roster/lineup mechanics.** Batting order format, substitution,
   mandatory play — likely everyone-bats/everyone-plays by inference from the division's
   developmental framing, but not confirmed.
4. **F-section — Operational basics.** Dugout assignment, umpiring (if any), game-ball
   provision.
5. **G4 — Roster size target.**
6. **G8/G9 — Standings and any end-of-Kindy-season event.**

Given the scale of what's missing, this doc may be better served by a direct conversation
with current/recent Kindy coaches or the Player Development lead than by more document
research — there may simply be no NCLL-wide public source for most of this D-section
content to find.
