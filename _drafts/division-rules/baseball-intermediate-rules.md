## A. Division identity

### A1. Age eligibility (league age)

- **LL Rulebook:** LL Regulation V governs age-eligible player selection into the division.
- **D8 Interleague:** League age 11, 12, 13 — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Intermediate Baseball, "Official Rules Specific to..." §1 (+ LL Regulation V), rev. 2025-03-22. Matches `_Programs/allstars-main.html`'s own "11/12/13 year-olds (Intermediates 50/70)" listing exactly.
- **NCLL:** No in-house eligibility to set — see the header note. 11s and 12s in this age band are covered by AAA/Majors' own age eligibility (see baseball-majors-rules.md A1, baseball-aaa-rules.md A1); 13s who aren't in Majors go to Juniors. The only NCLL-side use of this age band is All-Star tournament-team selection (see G10).
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/allstars-main.html ("11/12/13 year-olds (Intermediates 50/70) [Pending]").

### A2. Purpose / what this division develops

- **LL Rulebook:** Intermediate (50/70) is LL's bridge division between the small (Major/AAA) diamond and the full-size (Junior/Senior) diamond — 70-foot bases, a 50-foot mound, full leadoffs and balks now in effect, on-deck permitted. It's meant to be a step, not a skip.
- **NCLL:** NCLL doesn't currently use this step — the league's regular-season track goes straight from Majors (60 ft) to Juniors (90 ft, its own independent team — see baseball-junior-rules.md G6). Intermediate only shows up when NCLL has a coach and enough interested 11–13s to field a District 8 All-Star tournament team, which isn't guaranteed year to year.
- **Notes:** Written 2026-09-03 from the header-note research; not a citation, same treatment as baseball-majors-rules.md A2.

### A3. Participation in two divisions

- **NCLL:** N/A — no in-house Intermediate roster exists to combine with anything.

## B. Field & equipment

### B1. Base path distance

- **LL Rulebook:** 70 feet for Intermediate (50/70) Division — CONFIRMED. Cite: LL Rule 1.04 field diagram.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play (see header note). Governs District Tournament play if NCLL's All-Star team is fielded.

### B2. Pitching distance & mound type

- **D8 Interleague:** 50 feet, fixed distance (not portable-mound territory the way Majors/AAA are) — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Intermediate Baseball §3, rev. 2025-03-22.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### B3. Ball standard

- **D8 Interleague:** **LL-approved Senior Division baseball** — not the standard smaller-diamond LL ball Majors/AAA use. CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Intermediate Baseball §2, rev. 2025-03-22.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### B4. Bat standard

- **LL Rulebook:** **Genuine rules flip from Majors/AAA — CONFIRMED.** Bats meeting the USABat standard OR the BBCOR performance standard are both permitted at Intermediate (Majors/AAA are USABat-only, no BBCOR). Max length 34" (vs. 33" at Majors/AAA). Cite: LL Rule 1.10 NOTE 2 (Intermediate/Junior subsection) — "permitted for the Intermediate (50-70) Division and Junior League Division are bats meeting the BBCOR performance standard." **2026 equipment changes, propagated 2026-09-04 (rulebook-verify pass):** pine tar or any other similar adhesive substance is now permitted on the bat handle/grip at all levels (Rule 1.10 NOTE 2, a *different* NOTE 2 — the general "grip" subsection, not the BBCOR one just cited) — reverses the prior prohibition; may not be applied to the barrel/taper or cover the USA Baseball logo **or the BBCOR mark specifically called out for Intermediate/Junior/Senior**, penalty for violation. Thumb protectors are permitted; choke-knobs and choke-up assists are not (Rule 1.10 A.R. 2). Rule 1.11(a)(3): the pitcher's undershirt-sleeve rule no longer singles out neoprene — any sleeve a pitcher wears, exposed or not, must be one solid color and not white or gray. Cite: `2026-little-league-baseball.md` "2026 Significant Updates" (lines 59–63); full text — pine tar NOTE 2 (line 1687), A.R. 2 (line 1697), Rule 1.11(a)(3) (line 1711).
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play; governs District Tournament equipment checks if the All-Star team is fielded.
- **Notes:** Worth flagging to families/coaches if the Intermediate All-Star team is fielded — a BBCOR bat that's illegal at Majors/AAA becomes legal here, and the max length is an inch longer.

### B5. Home-run distance / outfield line

- **D8 Interleague:** 250 ft cones on a non-fenced field — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Intermediate Baseball §4, rev. 2025-03-22.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### B6. Helmets / protective equipment

- **LL Rulebook:** Same NOCSAE/no-repaint/decal rules as Majors/AAA (rulebook-wide, not division-specific).
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

## C. Pitching

### C1. Pitching format

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** Full kid pitch, same as Majors/AAA — this doesn't change at Intermediate.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### C3. Pitch-count limits & required rest

- **D8 Interleague:** **70 pitches before May 1** (vs. 50 for Majors/AAA) — CONFIRMED, then the standard Reg VI daily-max chart by league age applies. Catcher restriction: catches more than 3 innings can't pitch that day; catches 1 pitch in the 4th inning can't pitch that day; a catcher planning to pitch should catch a maximum of 3 innings. Cite: D8 Suppl. Intermediate Baseball, "Local Rules That May Differ," rev. 2025-03-22.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### C4. Catcher → pitcher restriction

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** Same mechanic as Majors/AAA (catch 4+ innings ⇒ can't pitch that day) — covered under C3 above at the Intermediate tier.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### C5. Walks / HBP / balks

- **LL Rulebook:** **Genuine rules flip from Majors/AAA — CONFIRMED. Balks are in effect at Intermediate.** An illegal pitch with runners on base is a balk; runners advance one base. This is the tier where the rulebook's Major/Minor "no balk" exception ends. Cite: LL Rules 2.00 (BALK), 8.05 PENALTY FOR ILLEGAL PITCH — "[a balk in Intermediate (50-70) Division/Junior/Senior League]."
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play; would be genuinely new territory for NCLL coaches/umpires the first time an Intermediate team plays, since balks don't exist anywhere else in NCLL's current divisions.
- **Notes:** This is one of the most important coach-facing flags if the All-Star team is ever fielded — coaches and parents used to Majors/AAA's "no balk" rule need to be told this explicitly changes here.

### C6. Mound visits *(new 2026 rule, no equivalent row above)*

- **LL Rulebook:** **2026 rule clarification, applies at this tier.** Rule 8.06(b) Note (new for 2026): in the Intermediate (50/70), Junior, and Senior divisions, the number of remaining mound visits does not prevent a pitcher who was removed from the mound from returning as pitcher later in the game. Cite: 2026 Rulebook Significant Updates.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play; relevant only if/when NCLL fields a real Intermediate team or its All-Star team plays under this rule.
- **Notes:** A 2026-specific change — flag it as new when this doc is next reviewed, since a coach relying on an older understanding of mound-visit limits could otherwise assume a pitcher removed from the mound is done for the game.

## D. Game play

### D1. Innings for a complete game

- **D8 Interleague:** 7 innings — CONFIRMED (vs. 6 at Majors/AAA). Cite: D8 Suppl. Intermediate Baseball §6, rev. 2025-03-22.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### D2. Time limits

- **D8 Interleague:** No new inning after 2:15, no new batter after 2:30, reverts to the last completed inning, ties allowed — CONFIRMED. (Same window as Majors; the 2025 supplement dropped the doubleheader-specific clause that Majors/AAA still carry.) Cite: D8 Suppl. Intermediate Baseball, "Local Rules That May Differ," rev. 2025-03-22.
- **Cross-district (D7/D8 Teen Agreement):** For games crossing District 7/8, a different game-ending standard applies instead: no new inning within 15 minutes of a posted field-reservation end time, or within 30 minutes of the next scheduled game's start time. Cite: D7-D8-Intermediates-and-Juniors-Rules-Options.pdf, 2025.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### D3. Weekday curfew

- **D8 Interleague:** Sunday through Thursday while school is in session: no new inning after **9:15 pm**, no new batter after **9:30 pm** — CONFIRMED. Later than Majors/AAA's 8:30/8:45. The cross-district D7/D8 agreement uses the same 9:15/9:30 curfew.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### D4. Mercy / run-ahead rule

- **D8 Interleague:** **Shifted one inning later than Majors/AAA**, because it's a 7-inning game — CONFIRMED. 15+ after 4 (3½ if home ahead), 10+ after 5 (4½ if home ahead), 8+ after 6 (5½ if home ahead). Cite: D8 Suppl. Intermediate Baseball §8, rev. 2025-03-22. The cross-district D7/D8 agreement adopts the same tiers (citing LL Rule 4.10(e)).
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### D5. Runs-per-half-inning cap

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** No cap — CONFIRMED, same as Majors, unlike AAA's 5-run cap. Confirmed by absence: the D8 Intermediate supplement's "Local Rules That May Differ" section has only 5 provisions (time limits, curfew, minimum play, pitching limits, post-season) — no run-limit section, unlike AAA's supplement which has an explicit §6 "RUN LIMITS PER INNING." The mercy rule (§8) is the only run-differential provision at this tier.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### D6. Dropped third strike

- **LL Rulebook:** Standard Rule 6.09(b), always in effect — CONFIRMED, no D8 override (unlike AAA, which turns it off).
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### D7. Stealing / leadoffs

- **LL Rulebook:** **Genuine rules flip from Majors/AAA — CONFIRMED. Full leadoffs are allowed.** Runners may leave their base at any time, not just once the pitch reaches the batter. Cite: LL Rule 7.13 — the "Little League (Major) and Minor League" no-leadoff restriction does not apply above that tier.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play; genuinely new mechanic for any NCLL coach/player moving up from Majors/AAA, since neither of NCLL's current divisions has real leadoffs.

### D8. Bunting

- **LL Rulebook:** Permitted, same as Majors/AAA — not flagged as a divergence by Tower.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### D9. Batter's-box rule

- **D8 Interleague:** Rules (a)–(h) + penalty — identical to Majors/AAA. Cite: D8 Suppl. Intermediate Baseball §7, rev. 2025-03-22.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### D10. On-deck / bat safety

- **LL Rulebook:** **Genuine rules flip from Majors/AAA — CONFIRMED. The on-deck position is permitted** (Majors/AAA ban it entirely). Cite: LL Rule 1.08 NOTE 1 + A.R., exception clause: "the on-deck position is permitted in Intermediate (50-70) Division, Junior, and Senior League." A helmet is required in the on-deck circle.
- **D8 Interleague:** No bat in the dugout; practice swings only in the on-deck circle or batter's box, under coach direction; the on-deck batter should stand on the same side as the current batter. Cite: D8 Suppl. Intermediate Baseball §5 (Bat Safety), rev. 2025-03-22.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.
- **Notes:** This is the biggest single mechanical adjustment for any NCLL player/coach moving up from Majors/AAA to Intermediate — an entire on-deck routine that doesn't exist below this tier.

### D11. Courtesy runner

- **D8 Interleague:** Same as Majors/AAA — courtesy runner (last out) for the catcher and/or pitcher of record with two outs, part of the identical common-rules block. CONFIRMED.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### D12. Infield fly

- **LL Rulebook:** Standard Rule 2.00/6.05(e), not restated by D8 — same as Majors (not AAA's narrower local version).
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### D13. Bat throwing

- **NCLL:** N/A — this is an NCLL-invented local rule specific to in-house Majors/AAA play; no in-house Intermediate team exists to apply it to. If the All-Star team is ever fielded, the board should decide whether to extend the same warning-then-removal mechanic.

### D14. Headfirst sliding

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — the ban lifts at Intermediate.** A direct rule reading, not an inference: Rule 7.08(a)(4) verbatim scopes the headfirst-while-advancing out to "Little League (Major) and below only" — that scoping means the rule does not reach Intermediate (50-70) and above. A runner may slide headfirst while advancing at this tier.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.
- **Notes:** Consistent with the tier's general pattern of restrictions loosening (leadoffs, balks, on-deck all flip here too).

### D15. Malicious contact

- **LL Rulebook:** Same rule as Majors/AAA (league-wide, umpire's-judgment standard) — not tier-dependent.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

## F. Game operations

### F1. Dugout assignments

- **D8 Interleague:** Home = 3rd base dugout — same as Majors/AAA, common-rules block.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### F2. Umpiring

- **D8 Interleague:** If a D8 umpire isn't available, home supplies plate, visitor supplies field — same as Majors/AAA, common-rules block.
- **Cross-district (D7/D8 Teen Agreement):** **Differs** — the home team provides BOTH umpires (plate and base), not just the fallback plate umpire. Cite: D7-D8-Intermediates-and-Juniors-Rules-Options.pdf, 2025.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### F3. Scorekeeping

- **D8 Interleague:** Non-coach scorekeeper, home team's book official — same as Majors/AAA, common-rules block.
- **Cross-district (D7/D8 Teen Agreement):** Home team provides the official scorekeeper; either team may cover if the other can't. Cite: D7-D8-Intermediates-and-Juniors-Rules-Options.pdf, 2025.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### F4. Adult supervision

- **D8 Interleague:** Background-checked adult in the dugout at all times — same as Majors/AAA, common-rules block.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.

### F5. Game balls

- **D8 Interleague:** Partially resolved — D8 Suppl. Intermediate Baseball §2 specifies only the ball type ("a Little League approved Senior Division baseball," see B3) and, unlike the AAA/Majors supplements, omits the "home team provides a minimum of 2 new game balls" provisioning sentence. D8 genuinely doesn't state who supplies balls at this tier. Since Intermediate only ever plays tournament games for NCLL (see header note), LL Tournament Rules & Guidelines govern ball provision in that context (host league / higher seed), not the D8 supplement.
- **NCLL:** N/A for in-house play.
- **Notes:** Not a board decision — this is a genuine gap between the D8 supplement and the tournament rulebook, resolved by knowing which rulebook governs in which context rather than by an NCLL choice.

### F6. Score & pitch-count reporting

(roster size targets, standings, reporting mechanisms) don't apply — there's no in-house
activity to set policy for. Tower reviewed the doc 2026-09-03, confirmed every LL/D8
citation against the OCR and supplement text (nothing to correct), and closed all 4 items
flagged in the initial draft: D5 (no run cap, confirmed by absence of a run-limit section in

NCLL), and G10 (the 8-regular-season-games tournament requirement doesn't require those
games to be in the tournament team's own division — AAA/Majors games count, confirmed at
the rulebook level; exact Intermediate tournament-team age band/pool definition still needs
the LL Tournament Rules & Guidelines edition, not the base rulebook).

What's genuinely still open:

1. **Whether NCLL wants to revisit running a real in-house Intermediate division at all.**
   Not a documentation gap — a genuine strategic question (facility/coach availability,
   whether skipping straight to Juniors' 90-ft diamond serves 13-year-olds as well as a
