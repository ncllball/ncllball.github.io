# NCLL Baseball — Senior Division Rules

> Draft. Season: 2026. Last edited: 2026-09-03. Tower's LL Rulebook + D8 layer delivered in
> full and applied below with citations. Like Juniors, **NCLL actually runs Seniors** as a
> real regular-season division (league age 15–16, full 90-ft diamond, its own draft) — not
> just an All-Star team. Senior has **four rules that exist nowhere else in this rule set**:
> batting nine is allowed (every other division is CBO-only), a Designated Hitter is
> available, mandatory play adds a required at-bat on top of the 9 defensive outs, and bats
> must be BBCOR (USABat isn't sufficient here, unlike Intermediate/Junior). See B4, D-section,
> and E1/E2 below.
> Authority order: **LL Official Rulebook** → **D8 interleague supplement** → **NCLL choice**.
> **Division of labor on this doc:** Tower Claude owns the **LL Rulebook** and **D8
> Interleague** rows, with citations. The NCLL session's job is the **NCLL** row + Notes,
> sourced from the repo, ncllball.com, the Outline wiki, the recovered policy archive, and
> board minutes — same method as baseball-majors-rules.md/baseball-aaa-rules.md/baseball-junior-rules.md.

## A. Division identity

### A1. Age eligibility (league age)

- **LL Rulebook:** LL Regulation V governs age-eligible player selection into the division.
- **D8 Interleague:** League age 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball, "Official Rules Specific to..." §1 (+ LL Regulation V), rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** 15s and 16s are the core. Registration age band shown is 15–16, but per the same page's footnote, players as young as 13 are technically eligible.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Registration/registration-main.html (age-matrix table, footnote 8 — "Baseball Seniors (15-16) is mainly for 15's & 16's, though as young as 13's are eligible.").

### A2. Purpose / what this division develops

- **LL Rulebook:** Senior League is LL's oldest full-size-diamond division — same 90-ft/60'6" dimensions as Junior, but with additional rule flexibility (batting nine, DH) that only becomes available at this tier.
- **NCLL:** Per `_Programs/baseball-hub.html`'s own framing: "The Senior Division is built for league-age 15-16 players who want Little League reps alongside high school baseball." Explicit focus: "maintain varsity-level conditioning, keep arms on safe workloads, and align with high school timelines." It's the division built to coexist with high school ball, not replace it.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html (Senior Division card, panel-intro and "Ballpark Dimensions" panel).

### A3. Participation in two divisions

- **LL Rulebook:** Same Reg I NOTE 2 / dual-roster basis as baseball-majors-rules.md A3 — a player must be Major-Division-eligible or above to dual-roster (trivially true at Senior).
- **NCLL:** No source found describing NCLL dual-rostering into/out of Seniors. Given the age gap from Majors/AAA, this isn't a practical overlap case the way A3 is for Majors↔AAA.
- **Notes:** Not found stated anywhere; likely doesn't come up given the age structure.

## B. Field & equipment

### B1. Base path distance

- **LL Rulebook:** 90 feet — same as Junior. Cite: LL Rule 1.04 field diagram, "90-ft infield square."
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook. Confirmed live on _Programs/baseball-hub.html ("Seniors play on a full 90' field... matches high school mound, base paths, and fence dimensions").

### B2. Pitching distance & mound type

- **D8 Interleague:** 60 feet 6 inches — CONFIRMED, same as Junior. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball §3.
- **NCLL:** Per D8/rulebook.

### B3. Ball standard

- **D8 Interleague:** LL-approved Senior Division baseball — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball §2, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B4. Bat standard — Senior-unique #1

- **LL Rulebook:** **BBCOR-only, max 36", drop -3 (a 33" bat must weigh ≥30 oz) — the strictest bat standard in the whole rule set, and the only division where plain USABat isn't sufficient** (USABat works at Intermediate/Junior; here it doesn't). Wood bats are always fine. Cite: LL Rule 1.10, Senior League.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.
- **Notes:** The most important equipment flag in this doc — a bat that's perfectly legal at Intermediate or Junior (USABat, no BBCOR needed) is illegal at Senior. Families moving a player up need to be told this explicitly, probably more urgently than any other equipment change in the whole division-rules series.

### B5. Home-run distance / outfield line

- **D8 Interleague:** 300 ft cones on a non-fenced field — CONFIRMED, same as Junior. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball §4, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B6. Helmets / protective equipment

- **LL Rulebook:** Same NOCSAE/no-repaint/decal rules as every other division (rulebook-wide).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

## C. Pitching

### C1. Pitching format

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** Full kid pitch — unchanged at every division level.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### C3. Pitch-count limits & required rest

- **D8 Interleague:** 70 pitches before May 1 (same as Intermediate/Junior), then the standard Reg VI daily-max chart by league age — CONFIRMED. Catcher restriction: catches more than 3 innings can't pitch that day; catches 1 pitch in the 4th inning can't pitch that day. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball, "Local Rules That May Differ," rev. 2024-03-22. Note: the D7/D8/D9 cross-district agreement's closing note only mentions the 9-out mandatory-play rule, not the 70-pitch pre-May-1 limit — but the D8 Senior supplement's own §4 carries that limit, so it applies to D8 teams regardless of which agreement governs a given game.
- **NCLL:** Reporting baseline resolved by Reg VI(e)–(g), same as every other division (see baseball-majors-rules.md C3) — only the local workflow is open, same open-item pattern as baseball-aaa-rules.md/baseball-junior-rules.md C3.
- **Notes:** » NCLL DECISION (narrowed): the rulebook baseline is settled; only Seniors' specific recording workflow is open.

### C4. Catcher → pitcher restriction

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** Same mechanic as every division — covered under C3 above.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook/D8.

### C5. Walks / HBP / balks

- **LL Rulebook:** Balks are in effect at Senior, same as Intermediate/Junior (the rulebook's "no balk" exception only covers Major/Minor). Cite: LL Rules 2.00 (BALK), 8.05.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### C6. Mound visits *(new 2026 rule, no equivalent row above)*

- **LL Rulebook:** **2026 rule clarification, applies at this tier.** Rule 8.06(b) Note (new for 2026): in the Intermediate (50/70), Junior, and Senior divisions, the number of remaining mound visits does not prevent a pitcher who was removed from the mound from returning as pitcher later in the game. Cite: 2026 Rulebook Significant Updates.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook — applies to Seniors since it's a real, live NCLL division.
- **Notes:** A 2026-specific change — flag it for coaches this season, since a coach relying on an older understanding of mound-visit limits could otherwise assume a pitcher removed from the mound is done for the game.

## D. Game play

### D1. Innings for a complete game

- **D8 Interleague:** 7 innings — CONFIRMED, same as Intermediate/Junior. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball §6, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D2. Time limits

- **D8 Interleague:** No new inning after 2:15, no new batter after 2:30, reverts to the last completed inning, ties allowed — CONFIRMED, same as Junior. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball, "Local Rules That May Differ," rev. 2024-03-22.
- **Cross-district (D7/D8/D9 Senior Interleague Agreement):** No new inning within 15 minutes of a posted field-reservation end time, or within 30 minutes of the next scheduled game's start time — same shape as the Intermediate/Junior cross-district agreement.
- **NCLL:** Per D8 for D8-only games; per the cross-district agreement for cross-district games.

### D3. Weekday curfew

- **D8 Interleague:** Sunday through Thursday while school is in session: no new inning after 9:15 pm, no new batter after 9:30 pm — CONFIRMED, same as Intermediate/Junior.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D4. Mercy / run-ahead rule

- **D8 Interleague:** Shifted one inning later than Majors/AAA (7-inning game) — CONFIRMED, same tiers as Intermediate/Junior: 15+ after 4 (3½ if home ahead), 10+ after 5 (4½ if home ahead), 8+ after 6 (5½ if home ahead). Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball §8, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D5. Runs-per-half-inning cap

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** No cap — CONFIRMED by absence, same as Majors/Intermediate/Junior. The D8 Senior supplement has no run-limit provision.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook/D8.

### D6. Dropped third strike

- **LL Rulebook:** Standard Rule 6.09(b), always in effect — CONFIRMED, no D8 override, same as Majors/Intermediate/Junior (not AAA).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D7. Stealing / leadoffs

- **LL Rulebook:** Full leadoffs allowed, same as Intermediate/Junior. Cite: LL Rule 7.13 (the Major/Minor no-leadoff restriction doesn't reach Senior).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D8. Bunting

- **LL Rulebook:** Permitted, unchanged from every division.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D9. Batter's-box rule

- **D8 Interleague:** Rules (a)–(h) + penalty — identical to every other division. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball §7, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D10. On-deck / bat safety

- **LL Rulebook:** On-deck is permitted, helmet required — same as Intermediate/Junior. Cite: LL Rule 1.08 EXCEPTION.
- **D8 Interleague:** No bat in the dugout; practice swings only in the on-deck circle or batter's box, under coach direction; the on-deck batter stands on the same side as the current batter. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball §5 (Bat Safety), rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook/D8.

### D11. Courtesy runner

- **D8 Interleague:** Same as every division — courtesy runner (last out) for the catcher and/or pitcher of record with two outs, common-rules block.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D12. Infield fly

- **LL Rulebook:** Standard Rule 2.00/6.05(e), not restated by D8 — same as Majors/Intermediate/Junior (not AAA's narrower local version).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D13. Bat throwing

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not found documented for Seniors specifically. Same open-item pattern as baseball-junior-rules.md D13 — Majors/AAA's warning-then-removal local rule is NCLL-invented, extending it to Seniors (and, in a co-league year, getting the partner league(s) to agree — see G10) is a genuine board decision.

### D14. Headfirst sliding

- **LL Rulebook:** Permitted at Senior — Rule 7.08(a)(4)'s headfirst-while-advancing out is scoped "Little League (Major) and below only," same as Intermediate/Junior.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D15. Malicious contact

- **LL Rulebook:** Same rule as every division (league-wide, umpire's-judgment standard).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

## E. Roster & lineup

### E1. Batting order — Senior-unique #2 and #3 (batting nine + DH)

- **LL Rulebook:** **The only division where a traditional 9-player batting order is allowed at all.** D8 common rule §5: "Batting nine is not allowed, except in the Seniors Baseball division." A Senior team may run CBO (every other division's only option) OR a traditional bat-nine lineup with Rule 3.03 substitution/re-entry — the team/league picks. **If a team bats nine, a Designated Hitter becomes available** (LL Rule 3.03 Senior League DH rule): the DH bats throughout for one player in the regular lineup, may re-enter once in the same batting slot, and only a player NOT in the batting order can be the DH. Under CBO, there's no DH, since everyone already bats.
- **D8 Interleague:** Default is free substitution + CBO (same as every other division); if a team elects to bat nine instead, Rule 3.03's re-entry rules govern in place of the free-sub default.
- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not found documented anywhere — the single most consequential open item in this doc. Does NCLL's Senior team run CBO (matching every other NCLL division) or a traditional bat-nine lineup with DH? This is a real, board-level choice with no default to fall back on, unlike most other divisions' rows.
- **Notes:** » NCLL DECISION: CBO vs. batting nine (+ DH availability) for Seniors — genuinely undecided by any source found. This is the top-priority item in Open board decisions below.

### E2. Substitution

- **D8 Interleague:** Free substitution under CBO (D8 default); if the team bats nine, Rule 3.03 governs re-entry instead. Same conditional structure as E1.
- **NCLL:** Follows directly from whichever E1 choice NCLL makes — not a separate decision.

### E3. Minimum / mandatory play — Senior-unique #4

- **D8 Interleague:** **9 defensive outs (not necessarily consecutive) PLUS one at-bat for each player — the only division where the D8 supplement requires the at-bat explicitly**, rather than relying on CBO to cover it automatically. CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball, "Local Rules That May Differ" §3: "nine defensive outs (does not have to be consecutive) and one at-bat for each player." Like Intermediate/Junior, omits the "≥3 at infield" line and the Reg IV(i) board-penalty paragraph.
- **NCLL:** Per D8. This requirement matters most if NCLL's Senior team bats nine (see E1) — under CBO everyone bats automatically, so the explicit at-bat requirement is redundant; under a bat-nine lineup, a manager benching players for part of the game needs to actively make sure everyone still gets their at-bat.
- **Notes:** Directly connects to the open E1 CBO-vs-bat-nine decision — this row's practical stakes depend on how that's resolved.

### E4. Minimum players to start

- **D8 Interleague:** 8-player minimum, 9th slot skipped — common-rules block, same as every division.
- **Cross-district (D7/D8/D9 Senior Interleague Agreement):** No borrowing a fielder from the opponent; a team with only 8 present plays defense with 8.
- **NCLL:** Per D8 for D8-only games; per the cross-district agreement for cross-district games.

### E5. Late-arriving players

- **D8 Interleague:** Same as every division — bottom of the order (under CBO; under a bat-nine lineup, standard substitution rules would apply instead).
- **NCLL:** Per D8, subject to the open E1 CBO-vs-bat-nine decision.

## G. Season structure

### G1. Team formation / draft

- **LL Rulebook:** Same Regulation V basis as every division.
- **NCLL:** NCLL runs its own Senior Division draft — tryouts pace players into a snake draft; managers evaluate hitting, defense, and pitching; every athlete lands on a roster with balanced pitching depth. Same structure as Juniors (baseball-hub.html uses near-identical language for both cards).
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html (Senior Division card, "Assessments & Draft" panel).

### G2. Assessments / tryouts

- **NCLL:** Required for Senior-division players, same as AAA/Majors/Juniors (registration eligibility note applies league-wide to "AAA, Majors, or Junior division players" — Seniors isn't named in that specific sentence, worth flagging as a small gap, though baseball-hub.html's own Senior card confirms tryouts happen).
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html (Senior Division card, "Assessments & Draft" panel). ⚠ registration-main.html's eligibility-note sentence names AAA/Majors/Junior explicitly but not Senior — likely an oversight in that copy rather than a real policy gap, since baseball-hub.html clearly describes Senior tryouts, but worth a quick confirm.

### G3. Playing up / down

- **LL Rulebook:** League age 13 (and technically 12–14, per A1) may participate in Seniors per D8's band; NCLL's practice narrows the core to 15–16.
- **NCLL:** No formal approval gate beyond the standard draft/tryout process documented — same pattern as Juniors' G3.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Registration/registration-main.html footnote 8 (same source as A1).

### G4. Roster size target

- **LL Rulebook:** Regulation III(a) — 10–15 players, with the same exception clause as Juniors: up to **20 players** if NCLL fields only one regular-season Senior team.
- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not found stated anywhere for Seniors specifically — same open-item pattern as baseball-aaa-rules.md/baseball-junior-rules.md G4.
- **Notes:** » NCLL DECISION (narrowed): the outer bound is settled by rule; only the actual target is open.

### G5. Practices / start dates

- **NCLL:** **2–3 events per week** (same as Juniors), specifically **Monday/Wednesday games with Sunday practice**. Fields: Greenlake or Lower Woodland #1 primarily, with occasional sessions at Bitter Lake or Magnuson. Registration stays open longer than other divisions to accommodate high-school baseball cut timelines. 90-ft field access is limited, same constraint as Juniors.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html (Senior Division card — "Schedule: Mon/Wed games; Sun practice," "Fields: Greenlake or Lower Woodland #1," "High School Coordination" panel) + _Registration/registration-main.html (age-matrix table, footnote 9).

### G6. Schedule type

- **NCLL:** NCLL fields its own Senior team via its own draft (see G1) and plays a District 8 schedule against individual opponent towns — per baseball-hub.html: "Senior teams compete across District 8, keeping travel local while elevating the level of play. Opponents include Ballard, Queen Anne, Magnolia, Richmond, Northwest, and Northeast Seattle. Regular season feeds directly into District tournament play." Identical opponent list to Juniors.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html (Senior Division card, "District Competition" panel). Per the same co-leaguing clarification established in baseball-junior-rules.md G6/G10: this describes the regular season specifically, which is NCLL's own independent team — co-leaguing (if it applies here) is an All-Star-team concept, not a regular-season one (see G10). G10 now has NCLL's specific confirmed All-Star co-league partners (RUG + Northwest Seattle) — whether the regular-season team here is the same partnership or a genuinely separate arrangement isn't confirmed; see G10's cross-check flag.

### G7. Player pool / call-ups

- **LL Rulebook:** Same general mid-season player-agent movement mechanism as baseball-junior-rules.md G7 (Reg III(d)/IV).
- **NCLL:** No Senior-specific call-up or pool-player program found documented. Same open-item pattern as baseball-junior-rules.md G7 — plausible but undocumented, and would need the co-league partner's agreement in a co-league year for the All-Star team specifically (not the regular season, which NCLL runs independently — see G6).

### G8. Standings

- **NCLL:** Standings/seeding appear to be tracked — baseball-hub.html states "regular season feeds directly into District tournament play," which implies some seeding mechanism, same inference pattern as baseball-junior-rules.md G8. Not documented in GameChanger-specific detail the way baseball-majors-rules.md G8 is for Majors.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html ("District Competition" panel).

### G9. Post-season

- **D8 Interleague:** End-of-year "World Cup"-style tournament — pools, top 2 per pool advance to single-elimination, seeding set about one month out. **Not wood-bat** — the wood-bat requirement is Junior-specific only. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Regular-season standings feed District tournament seeding (see G8/G6). No NCLL-internal Senior post-season tournament found documented (same as Juniors — post-season runs through the D8/All-Star track).

### G10. All-Star / tournament team

- **LL Rulebook:** Same tournament-player eligibility baseline as baseball-majors-rules.md G10, with Senior-specific detail: minimum regular-season participation is **60% of the regular season, 6 games minimum for Seniors** specifically (a different framing than the flat "8 games" other divisions use — worth noting as Senior's own stated threshold rather than assuming the same 8-game rule applies verbatim). High school baseball players (and those with approved medical waivers) are exceptions to the minimum-play requirement.
- **NCLL:** League age 15–16 Baseball Seniors All-Star team. **Marked both `[Co-league]` and `[Pending]`** on `_Programs/allstars-main.html` — same dual-tag pattern as Baseball Juniors (co-leaguing is possible per LL rules allowing up to 3 leagues to combine when one league lacks enough players or a qualified coach; see baseball-junior-rules.md G10 for the full mechanism, confirmed by the user to be almost exclusively an All-Star-team tool, rarely used for the regular season). **NCLL's specific Senior co-league partners, confirmed 2026-09-03: RUG and Northwest Seattle** — verbatim from the 2026 D8 tournament brackets: "Seniors (no District Tournament. RUG / North Central / Northwest gets a BYE straight to the WA State Tournament)" ("North Central" = NCLL). Unlike every other division's tournament path, **there is no D8 Senior District tournament at all** — the RUG/NCLL/Northwest co-op gets a direct bye to the Washington State Tournament. All three partners are fellow D8 CITY-group leagues (see baseball-majors-rules.md G9), not a suburban North-End partnership. District 8 Tournament begins **June 29** (2026 timeline) at Shorewood HS for the age-matrix/timeline listing generally, but per the bracket note above, Seniors specifically skips straight to State rather than playing a District bracket. State advancement is listed TBD for 2026; unlike Majors/Juniors, Seniors is NOT named among the 2026 divisions advancing to Regionals (only "Majors (12's) and Juniors" are named) — so even with the direct bye, a Senior State win doesn't advance further in 2026.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/allstars-main.html (eligibility section — "Minimum Regular Season Game Participation... 6 games minimum for Seniors"; age-matrix `[Co-league]` tag; timeline "June 29: Baseball Seniors District 8 All-Star Tournament Begins [Pending]"; tournament-details table "Seniors [Pending] · Jun 29+ · Shorewood HS"; Regionals footnote naming only Majors/Juniors for 2026) + the 2026 D8 tournament brackets (RUG/North Central/Northwest bye citation, confirmed 2026-09-03). Same coaches'-committee selection process as baseball-majors-rules.md G10 documents league-wide, no Senior-specific variation found. **Cross-check flag:** it isn't confirmed whether NCLL's *regular-season* Senior team (see G1/G6, described there as NCLL's own independent draft/roster) is actually this same RUG+Northwest partnership year-round, or whether the partnership forms only for the All-Star tournament specifically, the same City-co-op-is-an-All-Star-thing pattern established for Juniors. Worth confirming directly rather than assuming either way.
