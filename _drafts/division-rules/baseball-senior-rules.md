## A. Division identity

### A1. Age eligibility (league age)

- **LL Rulebook:** **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit, Tower-verified) — "Regulation V" was the wrong citation.** Regulation V is "Selection of Players" (draft methods), not age eligibility. Regulation IV(a), Senior League: "Any player with amateur status who will attain the age of 13 years before September 1, 2026, and who will not attain the age of 17 years on or before August 31, 2026, shall be eligible to compete." NOTE under that clause: "The local league may allow 12-year-olds to participate in or dual roster in the Senior League Division. Players must be assessed by the league as capable of participation at that level." Cite: Regulation IV(a), `2026-little-league-baseball.md` line 1011.
- **D8 Interleague:** League age 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball, "Official Rules Specific to..." §1, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** 15s and 16s are the core. Registration age band shown is 15–16, but per the same page's footnote, players as young as 13 are technically eligible.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Registration/registration-main.html (age-matrix table, footnote 8 — "Baseball Seniors (15-16) is mainly for 15's & 16's, though as young as 13's are eligible.").

### A2. Purpose / what this division develops

- **LL Rulebook:** **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit) — batting nine isn't itself a national Senior-only feature; the DH is.** Senior League is LL's oldest full-size-diamond division — same 90-ft/60'6" dimensions as Junior. Rule 4.04's continuous-batting-order option is mandatory only for Tee Ball and Minor League; every other division — Majors, Intermediate, Junior, and Senior alike — may adopt CBO or run a traditional batting order as a local choice. What D8's own interleague common rules restrict to Senior specifically is batting nine *for D8 games* (see E1) — that's a District 8 local restriction, not an LL Rulebook fact about Senior uniquely being allowed a real batting order. Senior's actual national-rulebook-unique feature is the **Designated Hitter** (Rule 3.03, Senior League Designated Hitter Rule) — no other division has a DH option at all, batting nine or not. Cite: Rule 4.04 NOTE 1, `2026-little-league-baseball.md` line 2252; Rule 3.03, line 2142.
- **NCLL:** Per `_Programs/baseball-hub.html`'s own framing: "The Senior Division is built for league-age 15-16 players who want Little League reps alongside high school baseball." Explicit focus: "maintain varsity-level conditioning, keep arms on safe workloads, and align with high school timelines." It's the division built to coexist with high school ball, not replace it.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html (Senior Division card, panel-intro and "Ballpark Dimensions" panel).

### A3. Participation in two divisions

- **LL Rulebook:** Same Reg I NOTE 2 / dual-roster basis as baseball-majors-rules.md A3 — a player must be Major-Division-eligible or above to dual-roster (trivially true at Senior).
- **NCLL:** No source found describing NCLL dual-rostering into/out of Seniors. Given the age gap from Majors/AAA, this isn't a practical overlap case the way A3 is for Majors↔AAA.
- **Notes:** Not found stated anywhere; likely doesn't come up given the age structure.

## B. Field & equipment

### B1. Base path distance

- **LL Rulebook:** 90 feet — same as Junior. Cite: LL Rule 1.04 field diagram, "90-ft infield square."
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook. Confirmed live on _Programs/baseball-hub.html ("Seniors play on a full 90' field... matches high school mound, base paths, and fence dimensions").

### B2. Pitching distance & mound type

- **D8 Interleague:** 60 feet 6 inches — CONFIRMED, same as Junior. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball §3.
- **NCLL:** Per D8/rulebook.

### B3. Ball standard

- **D8 Interleague:** LL-approved Senior Division baseball — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball §2, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B4. Bat standard — Senior-unique #1

- **LL Rulebook:** **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit) — the BBCOR requirement doesn't apply to every bat at Senior; solid one-piece wood bats are specifically exempt from it.** Max 36" length, drop -3 (a 33" bat must weigh ≥30 oz) — the strictest non-wood bat standard in the whole rule set, and the only division where plain USABat isn't sufficient (USABat works at Intermediate/Junior; here it doesn't). "All bats not made of a single piece of wood shall meet the Batted Ball Coefficient of Restitution (BBCOR) performance standard" — so a solid one-piece wood bat needs neither USABat nor BBCOR certification, only the length/weight spec. Cite: Rule 1.10, Senior League, `2026-little-league-baseball.md` line 1681.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.
- **Notes:** The most important equipment flag in this doc — a bat that's perfectly legal at Intermediate or Junior (USABat, no BBCOR needed) is illegal at Senior. Families moving a player up need to be told this explicitly, probably more urgently than any other equipment change in the whole division-rules series.

- **D8 Interleague:** 300 ft cones on a non-fenced field — CONFIRMED, same as Junior. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball §4, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B6. Helmets / protective equipment

- **LL Rulebook:** Same NOCSAE/no-repaint/decal rules as every other division (rulebook-wide).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

## C. Pitching

### C1. Pitching format

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** Full kid pitch — unchanged at every division level.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### C3. Pitch-count limits & required rest

- **LL Rulebook:** **ADDED 2026-09-04 (rulebook-verify pass) — this row was previously missing entirely; Senior's LA 15–16 rest table genuinely differs from the ≤14 table every other division doc in this project cites, so it can't just be inherited from baseball-majors-rules.md C3.** Daily max for league age 13–16 is **95 pitches** (Regulation VI(c) — the same table gives 85 for LA 11–12, 75 for LA 9–10, 50 for LA 6–8). Pitchers **league age 15–16** follow a separate, higher rest table than the standard ≤14 table: 76+ pitches → 4 calendar days rest; 61–75 → 3 days; 46–60 → 2 days; 31–45 → 1 day; 1–30 → 0 days (Regulation VI(d)). NOTE 1 under both tables: "Under no circumstance shall a player pitch in three (3) consecutive days." A 14-year-old rostered on a Senior team still uses the standard ≤14 table (66+→4 / 51–65→3 / 36–50→2 / 21–35→1 / 1–20→0), not the 15–16 table — the table that applies is set by the individual pitcher's own league age, not the division. Cite: Regulation VI(c) and VI(d), `2026-little-league-baseball.md` lines 1233–1241 (daily max) and 1264–1294 (rest tables, both the ≤14 and 15–16 versions).
- **D8 Interleague:** 70 pitches before May 1 (same as Intermediate/Junior), then the standard Reg VI daily-max chart by league age — CONFIRMED. Catcher restriction: catches more than 3 innings can't pitch that day; catches 1 pitch in the 4th inning can't pitch that day. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball, "Local Rules That May Differ," rev. 2024-03-22. Note: the D7/D8/D9 cross-district agreement's closing note only mentions the 9-out mandatory-play rule, not the 70-pitch pre-May-1 limit — but the D8 Senior supplement's own §4 carries that limit, so it applies to D8 teams regardless of which agreement governs a given game.
- **NCLL:** Reporting baseline resolved by Reg VI(e)–(g), same as every other division (see baseball-majors-rules.md C3) — only the local workflow is open, same open-item pattern as baseball-aaa-rules.md/baseball-junior-rules.md C3.
- **Notes:** » NCLL DECISION (narrowed): the rulebook baseline is settled; only Seniors' specific recording workflow is open.

### C4. Catcher → pitcher restriction

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit) — same fix as baseball-junior-rules.md C4: age-specific thresholds, not a flat "4+ innings."** "A player who played the position of catcher for three (3) innings or less, moves to the pitcher position, and delivers 21 pitches or more (15- and 16-year-olds: 31 pitches or more) in the same day, may not return to the catcher position on that calendar day. EXCEPTION: If the pitcher reaches the 20-pitch limit (15- and 16-year-olds: 30-pitch limit) while facing a batter, the pitcher may continue to pitch, and maintain their eligibility to return to the catcher position, until [the at-bat resolves]." Which threshold applies is set by the individual player's own league age, not the division: NCLL's core 15–16 registration band (see A1) means the 31-pitch/30-pitch figures govern most Senior players, but a younger dual-rostered 13- or 14-year-old (see A1) would still use the 21/20 figures. Separately: "The catcher receiving one pitch to a batter in the fourth inning constitutes having caught four (4) innings." Cite: Regulation VI(a), `2026-little-league-baseball.md` lines 1225 and 1227.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook/D8.

### C5. Walks / HBP / balks

- **LL Rulebook:** Balks are in effect at Senior, same as Intermediate/Junior (the rulebook's "no balk" exception only covers Major/Minor). Cite: LL Rules 2.00 (BALK), 8.05.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### C6. Mound visits *(new 2026 rule, no equivalent row above)*

- **LL Rulebook:** **2026 rule clarification, applies at this tier.** Rule 8.06(b) Note (new for 2026): in the Intermediate (50/70), Junior, and Senior divisions, the number of remaining mound visits does not prevent a pitcher who was removed from the mound from returning as pitcher later in the game. Cite: 2026 Rulebook Significant Updates.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook — applies to Seniors since it's a real, live NCLL division.
- **Notes:** A 2026-specific change — flag it for coaches this season, since a coach relying on an older understanding of mound-visit limits could otherwise assume a pitcher removed from the mound is done for the game.

- **LL Rulebook:** **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit) — same fix as baseball-majors-rules.md D15.** There is no standalone "malicious contact" rule; Rule 7.08(a)(3) is an OUT call for a runner who fails to slide or attempt to get around a fielder waiting to field a thrown ball and tag them, with its own A.R. explicitly stating "There is no 'must slide rule.'" ## E. Roster & lineup

### E1. Batting order — Senior-unique #2 and #3 (batting nine + DH)

- **LL Rulebook:** **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit) — a traditional 9-player order isn't nationally Senior-exclusive; that's D8's own local restriction (see D8 Interleague row).** Rule 4.04: continuous batting order is mandatory only for Tee Ball and Minor League; Majors, Intermediate, Junior, and Senior may all adopt CBO or a traditional order as a local choice. **What genuinely is Senior-unique nationally is the Designated Hitter** (LL Rule 3.03 Senior League Designated Hitter Rule): available only when a team runs a traditional (non-CBO) batting order — the DH bats throughout for one player in the regular lineup, may re-enter once in the same batting slot, and only a player NOT in the batting order can be the DH. Under CBO, there's no DH, since everyone already bats.
- **D8 Interleague:** **This is where the "Senior-only" batting-nine restriction actually comes from** — D8 common rule §5: "Batting nine is not allowed, except in the Seniors Baseball division." For D8 interleague games specifically, every other division must use CBO; only Senior may elect a traditional bat-nine lineup with Rule 3.03 substitution/re-entry instead of D8's free-sub default.
- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not found documented anywhere — the single most consequential open item in this doc. Does NCLL's Senior team run CBO (matching every other NCLL division) or a traditional bat-nine lineup with DH? This is a real, board-level choice with no default to fall back on, unlike most other divisions' rows.
- **Notes:** » NCLL DECISION: CBO vs. batting nine (+ DH availability) for Seniors — genuinely undecided by any source found. This is the top-priority item in Open board decisions below.

### E2. Substitution

- **D8 Interleague:** Free substitution under CBO (D8 default); if the team bats nine, Rule 3.03 governs re-entry instead. Same conditional structure as E1.
- **NCLL:** Follows directly from whichever E1 choice NCLL makes — not a separate decision.

### E3. Minimum / mandatory play — Senior-unique #4

- **LL Rulebook:** **ADDED 2026-09-04 (rulebook-verify pass) — this row was previously missing.** Regulation IV(i) NOTE 6: **"Mandatory Play Requirements do not apply for the Senior League Division."** LL itself drops the mandatory-play floor entirely at Senior League — the standard 6-defensive-outs-plus-1-at-bat requirement that every other division doc in this project cites (see baseball-majors-rules.md E3) simply doesn't exist at this level under the LL Rulebook. This makes the D8 supplement's row below **D8 adding a floor back in that LL itself doesn't require**, not D8 modifying an existing LL number — worth stating explicitly so the provenance is clear, since every other division's D8 row is a *modification* of a real LL baseline, not a from-scratch addition. Cite: Regulation IV(i) NOTE 6, `2026-little-league-baseball.md` line 1079.
- **D8 Interleague:** **9 defensive outs (not necessarily consecutive) PLUS one at-bat for each player — the only division where the D8 supplement requires the at-bat explicitly**, rather than relying on CBO to cover it automatically. CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Senior Baseball, "Local Rules That May Differ" §3: "nine defensive outs (does not have to be consecutive) and one at-bat for each player." Like Intermediate/Junior, omits the "≥3 at infield" line and the Reg IV(i) board-penalty paragraph.
- **NCLL:** Per D8. This requirement matters most if NCLL's Senior team bats nine (see E1) — under CBO everyone bats automatically, so the explicit at-bat requirement is redundant; under a bat-nine lineup, a manager benching players for part of the game needs to actively make sure everyone still gets their at-bat.
- **Notes:** Directly connects to the open E1 CBO-vs-bat-nine decision — this row's practical stakes depend on how that's resolved.

### E4. Minimum players to start

- **D8 Interleague:** 8-player minimum, 9th slot skipped — common-rules block, same as every division.
- **Cross-district (D7/D8/D9 Senior Interleague Agreement):** No borrowing a fielder from the opponent; a team with only 8 present plays defense with 8.
- **NCLL:** Per D8 for D8-only games; per the cross-district agreement for cross-district games.

### E5. Late-arriving players

- **D8 Interleague:** Same as every division — bottom of the order (under CBO; under a bat-nine lineup, standard substitution rules would apply instead).
- **NCLL:** Per D8, subject to the open E1 CBO-vs-bat-nine decision.

## G. Season structure

### G1. Team formation / draft

- **LL Rulebook:** Same Regulation V basis as every division.
- **NCLL:** NCLL runs its own Senior Division draft — tryouts pace players into a snake draft; managers evaluate hitting, defense, and pitching; every athlete lands on a roster with balanced pitching depth. Same structure as Juniors (baseball-hub.html uses near-identical language for both cards).
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html (Senior Division card, "Assessments & Draft" panel).

### G2. Assessments / tryouts

- **NCLL:** Required for Senior-division players, same as AAA/Majors/Juniors (registration eligibility note applies league-wide to "AAA, Majors, or Junior division players" — Seniors isn't named in that specific sentence, worth flagging as a small gap, though baseball-hub.html's own Senior card confirms tryouts happen).
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html (Senior Division card, "Assessments & Draft" panel). ⚠ registration-main.html's eligibility-note sentence names AAA/Majors/Junior explicitly but not Senior — likely an oversight in that copy rather than a real policy gap, since baseball-hub.html clearly describes Senior tryouts, but worth a quick confirm.

- **NCLL:** No formal approval gate beyond the standard draft/tryout process documented — same pattern as Juniors' G3.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Registration/registration-main.html footnote 8 (same source as A1).

### G4. Roster size target

- **LL Rulebook:** **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit) — same fix as baseball-junior-rules.md G4: this range isn't universal.** Regulation III(a) — 10–15 players, with the same exception clause as Juniors: up to **20 players** if NCLL fields only one regular-season Senior team. This range applies to Junior/Senior/Intermediate/Major — Tee Ball and Minor League have no national minimum or maximum at all ("There will be no minimum or maximum established at the Tee Ball and Minor League levels"). Cite: Regulation III(a), `2026-little-league-baseball.md` lines 945 and 947.
