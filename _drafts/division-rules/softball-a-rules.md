## Softball-wide LL Rulebook facts *(same across every softball division doc — differs from baseball)*

| Aspect | Rule | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Base paths | 60 ft at every level — never changes to 70/90 the way baseball does | LL Rule 1.04 (60-ft infield square; Tee Ball option 50 — n/a to NCLL, no softball Tee Ball) |
| Pitching motion | Underhand / windmill from an 8-ft-radius circle; no more than 2 arm revolutions | LL Rule 8.01 |
| Pitching distance | Minor (A/AA/AAA) 35 ft · Majors 40 ft · Juniors 43 ft | LL Rule 1.07 NOTE 3–4 (D8 matches) |
| Leadoffs | None at any level. Majors/Juniors: runner may not leave base until the pitch is released. Minor (A/AA/AAA): not until the pitch is batted or reaches the batter. LL lets a league adopt the stricter Minor timing for Majors too — NCLL should state which it uses (see softball-majors-rules.md D7) | LL Rule 7.13 |
| Look-back rule *(no baseball equivalent)* | When a runner is off base after a pitch or a completed at-bat, and the pitcher has the ball within the 8-ft circle and is not making a play (a fake throw counts as a play), the runner must immediately commit to advancing or returning — no stopping, no standing off the base. **PENALTY:** ball dead, "No Pitch," runner is OUT. A.R.: if the runner commits, then stops again before reaching a base with no play made, still out. The 8-ft circle must be marked. Responsibility is removed once the pitcher makes a play on a runner. **D8 A/AA go further still — no leaving at all until the ball crosses the plate** (see D7a) | LL Rule 7.13 NOTE 1–2 + 7.08(a)(5) |
| Headfirst slide while advancing | **Out** at Tee Ball, Minor (A/AA/AAA), and Majors. **Permitted at Juniors.** Sliding back INTO a base headfirst is always legal at every level. A.R.: "There is no 'must slide' rule" — a runner is never required to slide | LL Rule 7.08(a)(4) |
| Balk | **There is no balk in softball at any level — this never changes by division**, unlike baseball. An illegal pitch (crow-hop, illegal delivery, quick pitch) is simply a ball on the batter | LL Rule 8.05 PENALTY ("All Divisions: the pitch shall be called a ball") |
| Illegal pitch / delivery *(applies AAA and up — coach/machine pitch at A/AA isn't subject to 8.01 except AA's player-pitcher portion)* | Underhand, hand below the hip; both feet within the 24″ pitcher's plate at the start; pivot foot on top of the plate; no crow-hop (pushing off from anywhere but the plate); **leaping IS legal** (both feet may leave the ground if they stay within the 24″ width and the pivot foot isn't replanted — this reverses the old ban, coaches from older softball won't know it); ≤2 arm revolutions in the windmill; one step with the non-pivot foot, forward and toward the batter, at release; no stop/reversal after the hands separate. Penalty → ball on the batter | LL Rule 8.01 (crow-hop 8.01(r), leaping 8.01(s), revolutions 8.01(o), step 8.01(h)) → LL Rule 8.05 |
| Dropped ball / "no pitch" | If the ball slips from the pitcher's hand before/during delivery, a ball is declared and the ball stays live — runners advance at their own risk. Not a do-over | LL Rule 8.07(a) |
| Ball | LL: 12″ Majors/Juniors, 11″ Minor. D8 City: A = 10″ Level-1 safety, AA/AAA = 11″, Majors/Juniors = 12″ | LL Rule 1.09 |
| Bat | One softball standard for all levels (no USABat/BBCOR split like baseball); non-wood must carry the current certification mark; ≤34″, 2¼″ barrel | LL Rule 1.10 |
| Batting helmets | League provides 6 (7 for Juniors), NOCSAE-approved. Batter, all runners, and (Juniors) the on-deck batter must wear one. Face guards/C-flaps permitted, **not required by LL** — a local/NCLL choice, not a rulebook mandate. Stickers/decals permitted ("not excessive or offensive," 2025 change); no re-painting, no tape | LL Rule 1.16 |
| Catcher's equipment | Mask with throat protector (or helmet-style mask with built-in throat guard), chest protector, shin guards, and — male catchers — a cup. Required for ANY player catching, including warm-ups. **LL has no defensive/fielding-mask requirement for other positions** — Rule 1.14 just requires a glove; any infield/pitcher mask policy is NCLL/local, not an LL rule (corrected 2026-09-03 — an earlier pass conflated this with USA Softball/travel-ball rules) | LL Rule 1.17 (catcher) / Rule 1.14 (glove, no mask mandate) |
| On-deck batter | Not permitted in Minor or Majors; Juniors only (helmet, on-deck circle) | LL Rule 1.08 NOTE 1 |
| Dropped 3rd strike | Majors & Juniors: batter becomes a runner on an uncaught 3rd strike if 1B unoccupied or 2 outs. A/AA/AAA: batter is out, caught or not | LL Rule 6.09(b) |
| Infield fly | Applies at Majors & Juniors (LL standard). D8 modifies below (A/AA: off; AAA: lenient) | LL Rule 2.00 / 6.05 |
| DP / Flex | Rule 3.03 DP and Senior DP/Flex cannot be used with a Continuous Batting Order. D8 mandates CBO at every level → DP/Flex is unavailable in all D8 interleague softball | LL Rule 3.03(j) |
| Substitution / re-entry | D8's mandated CBO + free substitution overrides LL's traditional-order re-entry restrictions — everyone bats, defense is free | LL Rule 3.03 |
| Courtesy runner | Local League Option (D8 adopts). With 2 outs, a courtesy runner for the pitcher and/or catcher of record; under CBO, the runner is the last player to make an out. **2026 change:** with the pitcher AND catcher of record both on base with 2 outs, the offense may courtesy-run for both at once | LL Rule 7.14(b) + 3.04 |
| Pitching limits (LL baseline) | Minors/Majors: max 12 innings/day; 7+ innings → 1 mandatory day of rest; one pitch = one inning. Junior/Senior: no LL restriction (local board/district may add — D8 does) | LL Regulation VI(b) |
| Mandatory play | LL Regulation IV; D8 modifies (see per-division) | — |

## B. Field & equipment

### B1. Base path distance

- **LL Rulebook:** **60 feet — CONFIRMED**, same at every softball level (never 70/90 the way baseball graduates). Cite: LL Rule 1.04 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### B2. Pitching distance & mound type

- **LL Rulebook:** **35 feet**, the Minor Softball (A/AA/AAA) distance — CONFIRMED. Cite: LL Rule 1.07 NOTE 3–4 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Machine or coach positioned "approximately 35 ft (simulating later player-pitch distance of 35–40 ft)."
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/a-division.html` ("Placement," under Pitching & Catching — see source-quality flag in header note).

### B3. Ball standard

- **D8 Interleague:** **10″ Level-1 safety ball** — the softball-specific safety-ball step below AA/AAA's 11″ standard ball. Cite: D8 City A softball doc — Tower, 2026-09-03. This is a genuine, meaningful equipment divergence from baseball's A Division (RIF5) worth noting side-by-side: both entry divisions use a safety ball, but the softball and baseball products differ.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B4. Bat standard

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03.** LL Rule 1.10 — **one softball bat standard applies at every level**, unlike baseball's USABat/BBCOR split by division. Non-wood bats must carry the current softball certification mark; ≤34″, 2¼″ barrel.
- **NCLL:** Not independently restated for A specifically on the recovered page.

### B5. Home-run distance / outfield line

- **NCLL:** n/a — not a meaningful concept at this level.

### B6. Helmets / protective equipment

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03.** Batting helmets: league provides 6, NOCSAE-approved; batter and all runners must wear one (Rule 1.16). Catcher's gear: mask w/ throat protector, chest protector, shin guards, cup for male catchers, required any time a player is catching including warm-ups (Rule 1.17). **Correction, applies to every softball doc in this project:** LL has **no rule requiring a defensive/fielding face mask** for any other position — Rule 1.14 only requires a glove. Face masks on batting helmets and infield/pitcher masks are entirely NCLL/local choices, not LL mandates — an earlier pass in this project's own docs implied otherwise in places by citing them near "LL Rulebook," which this correction fixes.
- **NCLL:** Face masks on batting helmets "strongly encouraged early to normalize safety" (an NCLL choice, not an LL requirement). Infield defensive masks optional but supported, "especially 1B & pitcher spots" (also NCLL, not LL). Typical glove size 9″–11″; discourage oversized traps.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/a-division.html` (Equipment section).

## C. Pitching

### C1. Pitching format — the core A Division mechanic

- **LL Rulebook:** Same underhand/windmill motion rule applies league-wide (see softball-wide facts table) — not division-specific.
- **NCLL:** **Coach pitch (or machine) only** — no player pitcher at this level. Offensive-team coach or approved volunteer operates a pitching machine (if used) or delivers soft coach pitches, "not strikeout focus." **Live-page detail not previously seen at baseball A:** the coach may pitch from within 1 ft of the circle rather than a fixed rubber, and coaches may wear a glove — a batted ball hitting the coach pitcher is incidental, ball stays live. The player at the pitcher position stays fully inside the circle until the ball is in play (may pick a side).
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/a-division.html` ("Pitching Machine Operation," "Placement," under Pitching & Catching).

### C2. Live-pitch limits / at-bat structure

- **NCLL:** **7 live pitches per batter, then out.** A foul ball on/after pitch 7 buys another pitch, unless it's caught for an out. No tee-ball fallback after 7 pitches — the batter is simply out. **No walks; no HBP advancement** — if a coach pitch hits the batter, the batter does NOT take first (a real divergence from baseball's A Division, which has no HBP concept at all since it's machine-only).
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/a-division.html` (Hitting section) — this specific 7-pitch structure with its foul-ball exception isn't fully spelled out on the recovered page in this much detail; ⚠ confirm the exact mechanic with the VP of Softball before treating as final, given the source-quality flag.

### C3–C5. Pitch counts, catcher restrictions, walks/balks

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — no balk exists in softball at any level, ever** (unlike baseball, where the balk rule flips on above Majors/AAA). An illegal pitch is simply called a ball on the batter. Cite: LL Rule 8.05 PENALTY — Tower, 2026-09-03. n/a in practice at A regardless, since there's no live pitcher subject to a delivery-illegality call in coach/machine pitch.
- **NCLL:** n/a — no player pitcher, no pitch-count or catcher-restriction concept at this level.

- **NCLL:** n/a as a separate mercy threshold — see D5's inning cap, which functions as the de facto control at this level.

### D5. Runs-per-half-inning cap

- **NCLL:** **"5-run or full lineup bat-around" guideline, agreed pre-game between managers** — softer/negotiable framing than the hard caps at AA/AAA/Majors.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/a-division.html`.

### D6. Dropped third strike

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — the LL default at Minor Softball is OFF**, same scoping baseball's Minor League gets: Rule 6.09(b) is limited to Majors & Juniors only. Cite: LL Rule 6.09(b) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** n/a in practice — see C2, there's no traditional strikeout/pitch-count structure the way full kid-pitch divisions have (a batter is out after 7 pitches, not on a called/swinging third strike against a live defensive catcher play).

### D7. Stealing / leadoffs

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — no leadoffs at any softball level**; at Minor Softball (which A sits within), a runner may not leave until the pitch is batted or reaches the batter. Cite: LL Rule 7.13 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** **No stealing or leading off** — explicitly a developmental-focus choice, matching the LL default exactly (not a local tightening).
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/a-division.html` (Base Running).

### D7a. Look-back rule *(softball-specific, no baseball equivalent — see shared facts table for the full citation)*

- **LL Rulebook:** Applies league-wide in principle (Rule 7.13 NOTE 1–2 + 7.08(a)(5)) — but **D8's A/AA rule is stricter and supersedes it in practice**: a runner may not leave the base at all until the ball crosses the plate (see D7), so the look-back scenario (runner already off base, pitcher holding the ball in the circle) is less likely to arise at A than at AAA and up, where leadoffs happen closer to the pitch itself.
- **NCLL:** Not independently addressed on the recovered page; the underlying LL rule would still govern if a runner is ever caught off base with the pitcher holding the ball unengaged.

### D8. Bunting

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not addressed for A Softball specifically — plausible n/a given no true kid-pitch mechanic exists yet, but not stated.

### D9. Batter's-box rule

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated for A Softball.

### D10. On-deck / bat safety

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — not permitted at Minor Softball or Majors, Juniors-only.** Cite: LL Rule 1.08 NOTE 1 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Not independently restated for A specifically on the recovered page, but the rulebook rule applies regardless.

### D11. Courtesy runner

- **LL Rulebook:** A Local League Option (Rule 7.14(b) + 3.04) that D8 adopts at the divisions where it plays interleague — since A is confirmed entirely in-house (see G6), whether NCLL extends this option to A's own in-house games is purely NCLL's call, not something D8 mandates either way. Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated for A Softball.

### D12. Infield fly

- **D8 Interleague:** **OFF at A** (and AA) — D8 modifies the LL standard downward at these two lower divisions. Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03 (softball-wide facts table).
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D13. Bat throwing

- **NCLL:** "One warning; repeat → sit for the half-inning" — a **different penalty structure than baseball's warning-then-removal-from-the-game** mechanic. Worth flagging clearly: softball's version is a half-inning benching, not ejection.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/a-division.html` (Hitting).

### D14. Headfirst sliding

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — OUT at Minor Softball (A sits within this tier).** A runner who slides headfirst while advancing is out; sliding headfirst back INTO a base (returning) is always legal. A.R.: "There is no 'must slide' rule" — a runner is never required to slide at all. Cite: LL Rule 7.08(a)(4) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook — not independently restated on the recovered page.

### D15. Malicious contact

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated for A Softball specifically.

### D16. Advancing on base hits / overthrows *(A-Division-specific row, no equivalent numbering above)*
