## B. Field & equipment

### B1. Base path distance

- **LL Rulebook:** **60 feet — CONFIRMED**, same as every softball level (softball's base paths never graduate to 70/90 the way baseball's do).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### B2. Pitching distance & mound type

- **LL Rulebook:** **40 feet — CONFIRMED**, the Majors Softball standard (a step up from AAA's 35 ft). Cite: LL Rule 1.07 NOTE 3–4 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` ("Distance," under Pitching — "40 ft rubber (Majors Softball standard)").

### B3. Ball standard

- **D8 Interleague:** **12″ ball** — "12\" Little League softballs (yellow, raised seams)." Cite: D8 City Majors softball doc — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per D8. **Home team provides 2 balls to the plate meeting** — the first explicit ball-provisioning statement anywhere in the softball ladder (A/AA/AAA all leave this ⚠ open).
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` ("Game Rules / Structure" section — "12\" Little League softballs... home team provides 2 to the plate meeting").

### B4. Bat standard

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03.** LL Rule 1.10 — one softball bat standard at every level; ≤34″, 2¼″ barrel, current certification mark (USA/ASA/USSSA per the recovered page's own language, "verify pre-season guidance"). **2026 change, propagated 2026-09-04 (rulebook-verify pass):** pine tar or any other similar adhesive substance is now permitted on the bat handle/grip at all levels (Rule 1.10 Note 2) — reverses the prior prohibition; may not be applied to the barrel/taper or cover the BPF mark, penalty for violation. Thumb protectors are permitted; choke-knobs and choke-up assists are not (Rule 1.10 A.R. 2). Cite: `2026-little-league-softball.md` "2026 Significant Updates" (lines 59–61) and the full Rule 1.10 text (lines 1594–1602).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### B5. Home-run distance / outfield line

- **D8 Interleague:** **200 ft** — CONFIRMED via Tower, the first explicit home-run line anywhere in the softball ladder (A/AA/AAA don't have a fixed HR-cone concept at all — see those docs' B5 rows).
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B6. Helmets / protective equipment

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03.** Batting helmets NOCSAE-approved, league-provided, required for batter/runners (Rule 1.16). Catcher's gear required any time catching (Rule 1.17). **Correction, project-wide:** LL has no rule requiring a defensive/fielding mask at any position but catcher — Rule 1.14 only requires a glove. "Some interleague opponents may require them" is a real possibility (opponent-local policy), but not an LL-level mandate.
- **NCLL:** Face masks on batting helmets "strongly encouraged / often required in interleague" (local/opponent policy). Pitcher defensive masks recommended (also local, not LL).
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` (Equipment).

## C. Pitching

### C1. Pitching format

- **LL Rulebook:** Same underhand/windmill motion; **40-ft distance** (see B2) — not division-specific beyond the distance.
- **NCLL:** Kid pitch, full season. Encourage a rotation of at least 3–4 pitchers across the season. Core mechanical focus: command and change of speeds (change-up introduction). Limit early pursuit of advanced spin pitches unless a player demonstrates stable core mechanics.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` ("Innings & Usage," "Mechanics & Development," under Pitching).

### C2. Illegal pitch enforcement

- **NCLL:** Umpires enforce obvious replant/leap violations; early season, an instructional warning unless advantage is gained — same standard as AAA's.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` ("Illegal Pitch").

### C3. Pitch-count limits & required rest

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — innings-pitched, not pitch counts.** Majors: max 12 innings/day; 7+ innings in a day → 1 mandatory rest day; one pitch thrown = one inning charged. Cite: LL Regulation VI(b) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **D8 Interleague:** **≤3 innings pitched per game through April 30** — same early-season D8 workload cap as AAA. Cite: D8 City Majors softball doc — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** No pitch counts; monitor fatigue and mechanics (arm circle shortening, loss of control, posture breakdown) directly.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` ("Innings & Usage").

### C4. Catcher → pitcher restriction

- **NCLL:** "Avoid full game catching + heavy circle workload in same day when possible" — same guidance-framed (not numeric-threshold) approach as AAA's C4.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` ("Catcher Usage / Battery Management").

### C5. Walks / HBP / balks

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — no balk exists in softball at any level, ever.** An illegal pitch is simply a ball on the batter. Cite: LL Rule 8.05 PENALTY — Tower, 2026-09-03. Full Rule 8.01 delivery mechanics apply at Majors.
- **NCLL:** Standard 4-ball walk applies (4/3 count, foul with 2 strikes extends the at-bat — see E1). No walk-continuation restriction exists at Majors — that's specifically an AAA rule (see softball-aaa-rules.md D7); at Majors, a walked runner may advance normally, subject only to the standard leadoff/steal timing (see D7 below).

## D. Game play

### D1. Innings for a complete game

- **NCLL:** **6 innings regulation.**
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` ("Game Rules / Structure").

### D2. Time limits

- **NCLL:** **Time guideline ~2:00; no new inning after 1:50** (local field-slot dependent). Full explicit statement per Tower's handoff: **2:15/2:30** structure — no new inning after 2:15, no new batter after 2:30 (reconciling the recovered page's softer "~2:00" guidance with Tower's more precise D8 citation; the D8 figure governs for interleague play).
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` ("Game Rules / Structure") + Tower, 2026-09-03 (D8 City Majors softball doc, precise 2:15/2:30 citation).

### D3. Weekday curfew

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated for Majors Softball (unlike baseball Majors, which has an explicit weekday curfew clause).

### D4. Mercy / run-ahead rule

- **NCLL:** **Through April 30:** no separate mercy threshold beyond the 5-run inning cap (see D5). **After April 30:** **15 after 3 (2½ if home ahead) / 10 after 4 (3½) / 8 after 5 (4½)** — CONFIRMED via Tower. This split (a stricter early-season run cap giving way to a tiered mercy rule after April 30) is a genuinely Majors-specific structure not found at any lower softball division.
- **Notes:** Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03 (D8 City Majors softball doc).

### D5. Runs-per-half-inning cap

- **NCLL:** **Through April 30: 5-run cap per half-inning** (HR/ground-rule-double exempt — all driven-in runs score, matching baseball's own AAA/Majors exemption language exactly), **6th inning and later = no cap, one time through the order.** **After April 30:** the tiered mercy rule (see D4) replaces this cap.
- **Notes:** Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03 + `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` ("Run cap: 5 per half inning except final declared inning (open)").

### D6. Dropped third strike

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — applies at Majors, LL standard, unmodified.** "The batter becomes a runner on an uncaught 3rd strike if 1B is unoccupied or there are 2 outs" — a real divergence from every division below Majors (A/AA/AAA all have this OFF). Cite: LL Rule 6.09(b) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook — not independently restated on the recovered page, but the rulebook rule applies regardless. Worth flagging clearly to families/coaches moving up from AAA, since this is genuinely new territory (no lower softball division has it).

### D7. Stealing / leadoffs

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — the default is "on release"** (the softball-wide Majors/Juniors standard, distinct from the stricter Minor-level "batted or reaches the batter" default) — **but Rule 7.13 explicitly permits a league to adopt the stricter Minor timing for Majors too, and this is genuinely NCLL's own choice to state, not settled by the rulebook alone.** Cite: LL Rule 7.13 — Tower, 2026-09-03. (Contrast AAA/AA, where the recovered pages' "on release" claims may actually be errors relative to the Minor-level default those divisions are supposed to use — see softball-aaa-rules.md D7 — this is the reverse situation: Majors legitimately could use either timing, and NCLL simply needs to say which.)
- **NCLL:** Steals of any base permitted on release; confirm interleague stealing-home allowances pre-game (varies by opponent). Encourage aggressive but smart reads on passed balls/wild pitches. Not confirmed whether NCLL has ever considered adopting the stricter Minor timing instead — see Open board decisions.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` ("Leaving the Base," "Stealing," under Base Running).

### D8. Bunting

- **NCLL:** Not restricted — "situational awareness (RISP, advancing runners)" listed as an offensive skill goal, implying bunting/small-ball is a live tactic at this level (unlike AAA's "use sparingly" framing).

### D9. Batter's-box rule

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated for Majors Softball specifically.

### D10. On-deck / bat safety

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — NOT permitted at Majors** (the Juniors-only exception doesn't reach down to Majors). Cite: LL Rule 1.08 NOTE 1 — Tower, 2026-09-03. Bat in hand only when actually up to bat.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D11. Courtesy runner

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — LL Rule 7.14(b) + 3.04**, a Local League Option D8 adopts. With 2 outs, courtesy-runs for the pitcher and/or catcher of record; under CBO, the runner is the last player put out. **2026 change:** may courtesy-run for both pitcher AND catcher at once if both are on base with 2 outs. Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D12. Infield fly

- **LL Rulebook:** **Applies — LL standard, unmodified by D8 at Majors** (unlike A/AA where it's off, and AAA where it's lenient). Cite: LL Rule 2.00/6.05 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D13. Bat throwing

- **NCLL:** "One team warning then possible out for repeat" — same structure as AAA's.

### D14. Headfirst sliding

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — OUT at Majors** (Juniors is the only softball division where this flips permitted). A.R.: no "must slide" rule. Cite: LL Rule 7.08(a)(4) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Feet-first on advances; head-first only on returns — matches the rulebook exactly, and the same rule as AAA (see softball-aaa-rules.md D14). Hook and pop-up slides for scoring plays introduced later in the season.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/majors.html` ("Sliding," under Base Running).

### D15. Malicious contact

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated as its own rule for Majors Softball specifically.
