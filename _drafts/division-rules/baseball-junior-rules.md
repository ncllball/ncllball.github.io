## A. Division identity

### A1. Age eligibility (league age)

- **LL Rulebook:** LL Regulation V governs age-eligible player selection into the division.
- **D8 Interleague:** League age 12, 13, 14; league age 15 may participate with District Administrator approval — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Junior Baseball, "Official Rules Specific to..." §1 (+ LL Regulation V), rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** 13s and 14s are the core. 12s CAN join Juniors, but it's not advised, since they'd forgo a year of Majors eligibility. No NCLL practice found for accepting a 15-year-old under the D8/DA-approval option.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Registration/registration-main.html (age-matrix table, footnote 7 — "Baseball Juniors (13-14) is for 13's & 14's. While 12's can join, it's not advised as they'd forego a year of Majors.").

### A2. Purpose / what this division develops

- **LL Rulebook:** Junior League is LL's full-size-diamond division — 90-foot bases, 60'6" mound, matching the standard baseball field every level above it uses. Balks, full leadoffs, and on-deck are all in effect, same as high school ball.
- **NCLL:** Per `_Programs/baseball-hub.html`'s own framing: "The Junior Division is built for league-age 13-14 players, mirroring prep baseball dimensions and pace... elevate varsity-level mechanics, strategy, and leadership while competing across District 8." It's the division where NCLL players transition to true high-school-scale baseball before Seniors.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html (Junior Division card, panel-intro and "Ballpark Dimensions & Mechanics" panel).

### A3. Participation in two divisions

- **LL Rulebook:** Same Reg I NOTE 2 / dual-roster basis as baseball-majors-rules.md A3 (Reg I/IV, per LL's "dual roster" guidance — see baseball-majors-rules.md A3 for the full condition list). A player must be Major-Division-eligible or above to dual-roster, so a Junior-age player dual-rostering downward into Majors isn't the applicable case; a Majors-eligible 12 playing Juniors as well as Majors would be.
- **NCLL:** No source found describing any NCLL dual-rostering into/out of Juniors (unlike the Majors↔AAA Call-Up program). Given Juniors draws primarily 13–14s who've aged out of Majors eligibility, the practical overlap is narrow (mainly a 12 choosing Juniors over Majors — see A1 — not a dual-roster case).
- **Notes:** Not found stated anywhere; likely just doesn't come up given the age-eligibility structure, not a documented NCLL policy either way.

## B. Field & equipment

### B1. Base path distance

- **LL Rulebook:** 90 feet for Junior/Senior League — CONFIRMED. Cite: LL Rule 1.04 field diagram.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook — 90-ft diamond, matching high school dimensions. Confirmed live on _Programs/baseball-hub.html ("Junior teams play on a full 90' diamond, matching high school rules").

### B2. Pitching distance & mound type

- **D8 Interleague:** 60 feet 6 inches (full distance) — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Junior Baseball §3 + LL Rule 1.04.
- **NCLL:** Per D8/rulebook. Confirmed live on _Programs/baseball-hub.html ("Pitchers work from 60'6\" with varsity-style pitch counts").

### B3. Ball standard

- **D8 Interleague:** LL-approved Senior Division baseball — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Junior Baseball §2, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B4. Bat standard

- **LL Rulebook:** **Genuine rules flip from Majors/AAA — CONFIRMED, same as Intermediate.** Bats meeting USABat OR BBCOR are both permitted; max length 34" (vs. 33" at Majors/AAA). Cite: LL Rule 1.10 NOTE 2 (Intermediate/Junior subsection) — names "Intermediate (50-70) Division and Junior League Division" for BBCOR eligibility. **2026 equipment changes, propagated 2026-09-04 (rulebook-verify pass):** pine tar or any other similar adhesive substance is now permitted on the bat handle/grip at all levels (Rule 1.10 NOTE 2, a *different* NOTE 2 — the general "grip" subsection, not the BBCOR one just cited) — reverses the prior prohibition; may not be applied to the barrel/taper or cover the USA Baseball logo **or the BBCOR mark specifically called out for Intermediate/Junior/Senior**, penalty for violation. Thumb protectors are permitted; choke-knobs and choke-up assists are not (Rule 1.10 A.R. 2). Rule 1.11(a)(3): the pitcher's undershirt-sleeve rule no longer singles out neoprene — any sleeve a pitcher wears, exposed or not, must be one solid color and not white or gray. Cite: `2026-little-league-baseball.md` "2026 Significant Updates" (lines 59–63); full text — pine tar NOTE 2 (line 1687), A.R. 2 (line 1697), Rule 1.11(a)(3) (line 1711).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.
- **Notes:** Same coach/family flag as baseball-intermediate-rules.md B4 — a bat illegal at Majors/AAA becomes legal here, worth calling out explicitly the first year a player moves up.

### B5. Home-run distance / outfield line

- **D8 Interleague:** 300 ft cones on a non-fenced field — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Junior Baseball §4, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B6. Helmets / protective equipment

- **LL Rulebook:** Same NOCSAE/no-repaint/decal rules as every other division (rulebook-wide, not division-specific).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

## C. Pitching

### C1. Pitching format

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** Full kid pitch — unchanged at every division level.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### C3. Pitch-count limits & required rest

- **D8 Interleague:** 70 pitches before May 1 (same as Intermediate; higher than Majors/AAA's 50), then the standard Reg VI daily-max chart by league age — CONFIRMED. Catcher restriction: catches more than 3 innings can't pitch that day; catches 1 pitch in the 4th inning can't pitch that day; a catcher planning to pitch should catch a maximum of 3 innings. Cite: D8 Suppl. Junior Baseball, "Local Rules That May Differ," rev. 2024-03-22.
- **Reporting baseline RESOLVED** — same answer as baseball-aaa-rules.md C3: Regulation VI(e)–(g) requires the league to designate the scorekeeper or another game official as the official pitch-count recorder, who must give the current count to either manager on request and notify the UIC when a pitcher hits the max. The D8 Junior supplement adds nothing on top of this.
- **NCLL:** ⚠ Narrowed — only the local tooling/workflow is NCLL's to set (who the recorder is, GameChanger vs. paper, retention), same as baseball-aaa-rules.md C3. **Co-league wrinkle:** if the Junior team is co-leagued in a given year (see G10), the partner league(s) would need to use a shared recording method — not addressed anywhere, since it depends on which partner league and how the co-league agreement is structured that year.
- **Notes:** » NCLL DECISION (narrowed): the rulebook baseline is settled; only the local workflow (and, in a co-league year, the shared-method agreement with the partner league) remains open.

### C4. Catcher → pitcher restriction

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** Same mechanic as every division (catch 4+ innings ⇒ can't pitch that day) — covered under C3 above.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook/D8.

### C5. Walks / HBP / balks

- **LL Rulebook:** **Genuine rules flip from Majors/AAA — CONFIRMED. Balks are in effect at Junior**, same as Intermediate. An illegal pitch with runners on base is a balk; runners advance one base. Cite: LL Rules 2.00 (BALK), 8.05 — "[a balk in Intermediate (50-70) Division/Junior/Senior League]."
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.
- **Notes:** Same coach-facing flag as baseball-intermediate-rules.md C5 — genuinely new territory for a player/coach moving up from Majors/AAA, since neither of those divisions has balks.

### C6. Mound visits *(new 2026 rule, no equivalent row above)*

- **LL Rulebook:** **2026 rule clarification, applies at this tier.** Rule 8.06(b) Note (new for 2026): in the Intermediate (50/70), Junior, and Senior divisions, the number of remaining mound visits does not prevent a pitcher who was removed from the mound from returning as pitcher later in the game. Cite: 2026 Rulebook Significant Updates.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook — applies to Juniors since it's a real, live NCLL division (unlike Intermediate).
- **Notes:** A 2026-specific change — flag it for coaches this season, since a coach relying on an older understanding of mound-visit limits could otherwise assume a pitcher removed from the mound is done for the game.

## D. Game play

### D1. Innings for a complete game

- **D8 Interleague:** 7 innings — CONFIRMED (vs. 6 at Majors/AAA). Cite: D8 Suppl. Junior Baseball §6, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D2. Time limits

- **D8 Interleague:** No new inning after 2:15, no new batter after 2:30, reverts to the last completed inning, ties allowed (no doubleheader clause in this supplement) — CONFIRMED. Cite: D8 Suppl. Junior Baseball, "Local Rules That May Differ," rev. 2024-03-22.
- **Cross-district (D7/D8 Teen Agreement):** For games crossing District 7/8, a different standard applies: no new inning within 15 minutes of a posted field-reservation end time, or within 30 minutes of the next scheduled game's start time. Cite: D7-D8-Intermediates-and-Juniors-Rules-Options.pdf, 2025.
- **NCLL:** Per D8 for D8-only games; per the cross-district agreement for D7↔D8 games.

### D3. Weekday curfew

- **D8 Interleague:** Sunday through Thursday while school is in session: no new inning after 9:15 pm, no new batter after 9:30 pm — CONFIRMED, same as Intermediate. The cross-district agreement uses the same curfew.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D4. Mercy / run-ahead rule

- **D8 Interleague:** Shifted one inning later than Majors/AAA (7-inning game) — CONFIRMED, same tiers as Intermediate: 15+ after 4 (3½ if home ahead), 10+ after 5 (4½ if home ahead), 8+ after 6 (5½ if home ahead). Cite: D8 Suppl. Junior Baseball §8, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D5. Runs-per-half-inning cap

- **LL Rulebook / D8 Interleague:** No cap — CONFIRMED, same as Majors/Intermediate. Confirmed by absence: the D8 Junior supplement has no run-limit provision.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook/D8.

### D6. Dropped third strike

- **LL Rulebook:** Standard Rule 6.09(b), always in effect — CONFIRMED, no D8 override (same as Majors; unlike AAA).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D7. Stealing / leadoffs

- **LL Rulebook:** **Genuine rules flip from Majors/AAA — CONFIRMED. Full leadoffs are allowed**, same as Intermediate. Runners may leave their base at any time. Cite: LL Rule 7.13 (the Major/Minor no-leadoff restriction doesn't reach Junior).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D8. Bunting

- **LL Rulebook:** Permitted, unchanged from Majors/AAA.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D9. Batter's-box rule

- **D8 Interleague:** Rules (a)–(h) + penalty — identical to every other division. Cite: D8 Suppl. Junior Baseball §7, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D10. On-deck / bat safety

- **LL Rulebook:** **Genuine rules flip from Majors/AAA — CONFIRMED. On-deck is permitted**, same as Intermediate, helmet required.
- **D8 Interleague:** No bat in the dugout; practice swings only in the on-deck circle or batter's box, under coach direction; the on-deck batter stands on the same side as the current batter. Cite: D8 Suppl. Junior Baseball §5 (Bat Safety), rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook/D8.

### D11. Courtesy runner

- **D8 Interleague:** Same as every division — courtesy runner (last out) for the catcher and/or pitcher of record with two outs, common-rules block. CONFIRMED.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D12. Infield fly

- **LL Rulebook:** Standard Rule 2.00/6.05(e), not restated by D8 — same as Majors/Intermediate (not AAA's narrower local version).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D13. Bat throwing

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not found documented for Juniors specifically. Majors/AAA both have a warning-then-removal local rule (see baseball-majors-rules.md D13); it's an NCLL-invented rule (no LL/D8 basis), so extending it to Juniors is a genuine board decision — and since Juniors may be co-leagued in a given year (see G10), the co-league partner would also need to agree to it, not just NCLL's own board.

### D14. Headfirst sliding

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED, permitted at Junior** — Rule 7.08(a)(4)'s headfirst-while-advancing out is scoped "Little League (Major) and below only" and doesn't reach this tier, same as Intermediate.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D15. Malicious contact

- **LL Rulebook:** Same rule as every division (league-wide, umpire's-judgment standard) — not tier-dependent.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

## G. Season structure

### G1. Team formation / draft

- **LL Rulebook:** Same Regulation V basis as every division.
- **NCLL:** NCLL runs its own Junior Division draft — a snake draft, run so "parity" stays "front and center." All athletes attend tryouts; tryouts slot players so managers can balance pitching and key positions; every player who tries out lands on a roster.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html (Junior Division card, "Assessments & Draft" panel: "All athletes attend tryouts; every player lands on a roster via the Junior Division draft... Snake draft keeps parity front and center."). This is NCLL's OWN draft producing NCLL's OWN roster — worth noting since it argues against a literal shared-roster arrangement with a partner town (see G6 for the "co-league" ambiguity this raises).

### G2. Assessments / tryouts

- **NCLL:** Required for all Junior-division players (also required for AAA/Majors, both sports) per the registration page's eligibility note. Distinct from the AAA/Majors 5-Tool Assessment event — Juniors' tryouts are described on baseball-hub.html as slotting players for the draft, not detailed further (station list, date, location not found).
- **Notes:** Cite: _Registration/registration-main.html ("Required for all AAA, Majors, or Junior division players... regardless of previous division") + _Programs/baseball-hub.html.

### G3. Playing up / down

- **LL Rulebook:** A league-age-12 MAY play Juniors instead of Majors, at NCLL's discretion (see A1) — this is the main "playing up" question at this tier, framed as a choice rather than a promotion.
- **NCLL:** A 12 can join Juniors, but NCLL advises against it since it costs the player a year of Majors eligibility. No formal approval gate beyond the standard draft/tryout process is documented.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Registration/registration-main.html footnote 7 (same source as A1).

### G4. Roster size target

- **LL Rulebook:** Regulation III(a): "No team may have more than 15 players nor less than 10" — same range as every other division. **Junior-specific exception:** a league may roster up to **20 players** if it's fielding only one regular-season Junior team. So NCLL's real bound is 10–15, or up to 20 if NCLL runs just a single Junior team in a given year.
- **NCLL:** ⚠ The specific target within that range isn't documented anywhere found for Juniors (Majors' 11–13 target has no independent Juniors equivalent). Given NCLL appears to field at least one Junior team against a District 8 schedule (see G6), the 20-player single-team exception may be the relevant bound rather than the standard 10–15 — worth confirming how many Junior teams NCLL actually fields before setting a target.
- **Notes:** » NCLL DECISION (narrowed): the outer bound is settled by rule (10–15, or up to 20 for a single team); only the actual target NCLL wants within that range is open.

### G5. Practices / start dates

- **NCLL:** Practices/games run **2–3 events per week** (fewer than AAA/Majors' 3–4) on 90-ft fields when available — these fields have limited availability and different scheduling constraints than standard fields, so days of the week and start times vary more than other divisions.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Registration/registration-main.html (age-matrix table, Juniors row: "2-3" events/week, "90ft avail." + footnote 9).

### G6. Schedule type

- **NCLL:** **RESOLVED 2026-09-03** — NCLL fields its own Junior team(s) via its own draft (see G1) and plays a District 8 schedule against individual opponent towns — per baseball-hub.html: "Junior teams represent NCLL in District 8 play, facing peers from across North Seattle. Regular opponents include Ballard, Queen Anne, Magnolia, Richmond, Northwest, and Northeast Seattle. Regular season sets seeds for District and All-Star tournaments." Co-leaguing (combining rosters with a neighboring league) is a real, LL-sanctioned option NCLL *could* use for the regular season too, but per direct board/registrar confirmation, NCLL rarely does — it's used almost exclusively for All-Star team formation (see G10), not the regular Junior season.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html (Junior Division card, "District Competition" panel) + _Programs/allstars-faq.html (co-leaguing Q&A, cited fully in G10) + direct confirmation from the registrar, 2026-09-03. This closes the ambiguity the initial draft flagged here — the `[Co-league]` tag on allstars-main.html refers to the All-Star tournament team specifically, not the regular season.

### G7. Player pool / call-ups

- **LL Rulebook:** LL permits mid-season replacement/movement under player-agent control (Regulation III(d) / IV) — the general mechanism exists at the rulebook level.
- **NCLL:** No Juniors-specific call-up or pool-player program found documented (unlike the Majors↔AAA Call-Up program — see baseball-majors-rules.md A3/G7). Whether NCLL Juniors calls up from Majors, and how, isn't resolved by any general rule — in a co-league year (see G10), this is something the co-league agreement with the partner league(s) would have to spell out explicitly, since it isn't NCLL's call alone.

### G8. Standings

- **NCLL:** Standings/seeding ARE tracked — baseball-hub.html states directly that "regular season sets seeds for District and All-Star tournaments," which requires some standings mechanism. Not documented in the same GameChanger-specific detail baseball-majors-rules.md G8 has for Majors.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/baseball-hub.html ("District Competition" panel).

### G9. Post-season

- **D8 Interleague:** End-of-year "World Cup"-style **wood-bat** tournament — pools, top 2 per pool advance to single-elimination, seeding set about one month out. The wood-bat requirement is Junior-specific (Intermediate's version is not wood-bat). Cite: D8 Suppl. Junior Baseball, rev. 2024-03-22.
- **NCLL:** Regular-season standings feed District and All-Star tournament seeding (see G8). Beyond that, no NCLL-specific internal Junior post-season tournament (like Majors' or AAA's own Championship) was found documented — Juniors' post-season appears to run entirely through the D8/All-Star track rather than an NCLL-internal bracket.

### G10. All-Star / tournament team

- **LL Rulebook:** Same tournament-player eligibility baseline as baseball-majors-rules.md G10 (8 regular-season games, residency/birth-certificate verification, roster max 14) — more directly satisfiable here than at Intermediate, since a Juniors-age player's regular season IS Juniors itself. These eligibility rules don't change if the team ends up co-leagued (see NCLL row) — LL's own guidance is explicit that "the experience, commitment, and tournament path are the same" either way.
- **NCLL:** League age 13–14 Baseball Juniors All-Star team. **Marked `[Pending]`** on `_Programs/allstars-main.html`, same gating pattern as Intermediate (needs a coach and sufficient player interest to field at all) — District 8 Tournament begins **June 29** (2026 timeline) at Shorewood HS. State advancement is listed TBD for 2026 (unlike Majors' confirmed Walla Walla state site). If the Junior All-Star team wins District AND State, 2026 is a Regionals year for this division specifically — "only the Washington State Majors (12's) and Juniors champions advance to Regionals in San Bernardino, CA, and potentially the Little League World Series in Williamsport, PA" (a notable distinction: most other NCLL age divisions' 2026 champions do NOT advance past State).

  **Co-leaguing, explained:** the `[Co-league]` tag on this division means NCLL's Junior All-Star team may combine with one or two neighboring leagues to form a joint team — Little League rules allow up to three leagues to co-league. It's used when a single league doesn't have enough players or a qualified coach to field a competitive independent team at that age level, so players don't miss the tournament entirely just because their own league can't field a full squad alone. A co-leagued player may practice and compete under another league's banner, but the experience, commitment, and tournament path stay the same as an independent team. Families are notified directly if their child's age group will be a co-league team. Per direct board/registrar confirmation, co-leaguing is *possible* for NCLL's regular season too, but NCLL rarely uses it there — it's almost always an All-Star-team-formation tool, which is exactly the Juniors situation here. **Confirmed 2026-09-03: NCLL's co-league partners are always fellow D8 CITY-group leagues** (Ballard, Magnolia, Northwest Seattle, Queen Anne, RUG, Northeast Seattle, Shoreline — NCLL's own home field, Lower Woodland, sits in this group), never a suburban North-End league. The 2026 D8 Junior baseball champion co-op was "Queen Anne / RUG / Ballard" — a real example of what these three-league co-ops look like, but NOT necessarily NCLL's own specific partnership; NCLL's exact Junior co-league partners (if fielded) should be confirmed against the current-season D8 Junior schedule rather than assumed from that example. (Contrast baseball-senior-rules.md G10, where NCLL's specific Senior partnership — RUG + Northwest Seattle — is directly named in the 2026 D8 tournament brackets.)
- **Notes:** Cite: _Programs/allstars-faq.html ("What is co-leaguing and what does it mean for my child?" + the preceding Q&A on fielding an independent team, which adds: "Softball co-leagues are more common at the Juniors and Seniors levels" — implying baseball can and does co-league at this level too, just perhaps less predictably). Also: _Programs/allstars-main.html (age-matrix "[Co-league]" tag, timeline "June 29: Baseball Juniors District 8 All-Star Tournament Begins [Pending]", tournament-details table "Juniors [Pending] · Jun 29+ · Shorewood HS", and the Regionals footnote). Same selection-committee process as baseball-majors-rules.md G10 documents league-wide, no Juniors-specific variation found. This resolves the G6 ambiguity the initial draft flagged — co-leaguing is a G10 (All-Star) concept, not a G6 (regular season) one, confirmed directly.

## Open board decisions

*(Also tracked in `open-board-decisions.md` — update both together.)*

**Resolved 2026-09-03:** the doc's top open item — what "[Co-league]" means for Baseball
Juniors — is closed. It's an All-Star-team-formation concept (LL allows up to 3 leagues to
combine when one league lacks enough players or a qualified coach for an independent team),
not a regular-season arrangement. NCLL's regular Junior season is its own independent draft
and roster (see G1/G6); co-leaguing is technically available for the regular season too, but
NCLL rarely uses it there per direct board/registrar confirmation — see G10 for the full
explanation. Tower's 2026-09-03 follow-up closed 2 more: **C3** — Regulation VI(e)–(g)
settles the reporting baseline (same as every other division); only Juniors' local workflow,
and a co-league year's shared-method question, remain open. **G4** — Regulation III(a) sets
the bound (10–15 players, or up to 20 for a single Junior team); only the specific target
NCLL wants within that bound is open. Both narrowed from "not documented" to "documented
bound, open local choice" — same treatment baseball-aaa-rules.md gave its own C3.

The remaining 4 are confirmed genuine local/co-league decisions, not documentation gaps —
Tower found nothing authoritative for any of them, and for two (D13, G7) a co-league year
would need the partner league's agreement too, not just NCLL's:

1. **F5 — Game-ball provision for Juniors' regular-season games.** D8 only addresses
   tournament-context provisioning (LL Tournament Rules govern there); the "home team
   supplies" pattern other divisions use is convention, not a citable rule — NCLL's (or the
   co-league arrangement's) to set.
2. **F6 — Score/pitch-count reporting for Juniors.** Pure NCLL ops, confirmed not a gap.
3. **D13 — Does the bat-throwing local rule extend to Juniors?** It's an NCLL-invented rule
   with no LL/D8 basis — extending it needs NCLL's board, and in a co-league year, the
   partner league's agreement too.
4. **G7 — Does a Juniors call-up/pool mechanism exist?** LL permits mid-season player-agent
   movement at the rulebook level (Reg III(d)/IV), but whether/how NCLL Juniors calls up
   from Majors isn't resolved by any general rule — in a co-league year, this is something
   the co-league agreement with the partner league would need to spell out explicitly.
