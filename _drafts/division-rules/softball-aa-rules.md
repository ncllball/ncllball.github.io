## A. Division identity

### A1. Age eligibility (league age)

- **LL Rulebook:** **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit, Tower-verified) — "Regulation V" was the wrong citation.** Regulation V is "Selection of Players" (draft methods), not age eligibility. Regulation IV(a), Minor League Division: "Any candidate with amateur status who will attain the age of 7 years before January 1 and who will not attain the age of 13 before January 1 of the year in question shall be eligible to compete in the Minor League Softball Division (subject to the local league Board of Directors alignment of this division)." AA sits inside this national Minor League band; NCLL's A/AA/AAA split is the local league's own "alignment" of that division. Cite: Regulation IV(a), `2026-little-league-softball.md` line 1011.
- **D8 Interleague:** LA 7, 8, 9 (10 with DA approval) — CONFIRMED via Tower.
- **NCLL:** **7–9 is the registration target band**, with 8's specifically directed to register here. 10's can join but it's not advised. Registration age band: 7–9 (birth dates Jan 1, 2017 – Dec 31, 2019).
- **Notes:** Cite: _Registration/registration-main.html (footnote 2 — "AA Softball (7–9) is a coach-pitch/player-pitch blend for 7's to 9's. 8's should register here. While 10's can join, it's not advised. Fall and winter birthdays may play down with grade-level peers.").

### A2. Purpose / what this division develops

- **NCLL:** "An early player-pitch transition division (coach pitch relief as needed). The emphasis remains development first while introducing player pitching, initial stealing concepts, and expanded defensive awareness."
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aa-division.html` (introduction).

### A3. Participation in two divisions

- **NCLL:** n/a — no mechanism found or plausible at this age band.

## B. Field & equipment

### B1. Base path distance

- **LL Rulebook:** **60 feet — CONFIRMED**, same as every softball level. Cite: LL Rule 1.04 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### B2. Pitching distance & mound type

- **LL Rulebook:** **35 feet — CONFIRMED**, the standard Little League Minor Softball distance. **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit)** — the earlier citation to "Rule 1.07 NOTE 3–4" was wrong; there are no such notes. Rule 1.07 itself states all three division distances as inline sub-items: "(1) Minor League: 35 feet; (2) Little League (Majors): 40 feet; and (3) Junior/Senior League: 43 feet." Cite: LL Rule 1.07 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aa-division.html` ("Distance," under Pitching).

### B3. Ball standard

- **D8 Interleague:** **11″ ball** (not a safety ball, a real step up from A's 10″ Level-1 safety ball) — "11\" softball (yellow, raised seam) standard." Cite: D8 City AA softball doc — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B4. Bat standard

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03; CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit) — the 34″ figure is not universal; Minor Softball (which AA sits within) caps at 33″.** LL Rule 1.10 — one certification standard at every level, but length isn't uniform: "The bat shall be no more than 33 inches (34 inches for Junior/Senior League) in length." Non-wood bats must carry the current certification mark and be printed with a BPF (bat performance factor) of **1.20** — not previously stated in this doc. Barrel diameter ≤2¼″. **2026 change, propagated 2026-09-04 (rulebook-verify pass):** pine tar or any other similar adhesive substance is now permitted on the bat handle/grip at all levels (Rule 1.10 Note 2) — reverses the prior prohibition; may not be applied to the barrel/taper or cover the BPF mark, penalty for violation. Thumb protectors are permitted; choke-knobs and choke-up assists are not (Rule 1.10 A.R. 2). Cite: `2026-little-league-softball.md` "2026 Significant Updates" (lines 59–61) and the full Rule 1.10 text (lines 1594–1602).
- **NCLL:** Typical gloves 10″–12″; discourage oversized trap pockets on pitchers' gloves specifically.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aa-division.html` (Equipment).

### B5. Home-run distance / outfield line

- **NCLL:** n/a — not mentioned.

### B6. Helmets / protective equipment

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03.** Batting helmets NOCSAE-approved, league-provided, required for batter/runners (Rule 1.16). Catcher's gear required any time catching (Rule 1.17). **Correction, project-wide:** LL has no rule requiring a defensive/fielding mask at any other position — Rule 1.14 only requires a glove; infield/pitcher masks are NCLL/local choice only.

## C. Pitching

### C1. Pitching format — the core AA Division mechanic

- **LL Rulebook:** **The full Rule 8.01 delivery mechanics apply to the player-pitcher portion specifically** — underhand, hand below hip, both feet in the 24″ circle at the start, no crow-hop, leaping legal, ≤2 arm revolutions, one step forward at release (see facts table for full detail) — Tower, 2026-09-03. The coach-relief portion (see NCLL row) isn't subject to 8.01 the way a live player pitcher is.
- **NCLL:** **Player-then-coach pitch — a real A→AA progression.** Games begin with player pitchers when possible. **If 3 consecutive walks or HBPs occur:** optional switch to coach pitch to restore pace. Coach pitch is underhand, firm, belt-to-mid-thigh zone. No formal pitch count — managers monitor fatigue (control loss, wrist drag, off-balance follow-through) instead. Multiple pitchers encouraged; avoid over-relying on one player. Spin pitches (drop, curve, rise) not taught at this stage.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aa-division.html` ("Player vs Coach Pitch," "Innings & Usage," "Mechanics Focus," under Pitching).

### C2. Coach/relief mechanics

- **NCLL:** ⚠ The exact mechanic for the 3-walk/HBP coach-pitch handoff (does the coach finish only that batter, or the rest of the inning? does the player pitcher's count carry over?) isn't spelled out beyond the general trigger — same category of gap as baseball AA's C2 "2 innings" ambiguity.

### C3. Pitch-count limits & required rest

- **NCLL:** **No formal pitch count** — Little League Softball uses innings-pitched guidance rather than pitch counts (unlike baseball). Managers monitor fatigue signs directly; encourage multiple pitchers early in the season "so more players gain circle experience before playoffs."
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aa-division.html` ("Innings & Usage").

### C4. Catcher → pitcher restriction

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated for AA Softball.

### C5. Walks / HBP / balks

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — no balk exists in softball at any level, ever.** An illegal pitch is simply a ball on the batter. Cite: LL Rule 8.05 PENALTY — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** **Standard 4-ball walk applies** (a real divergence from A Softball's no-walks rule — the C1 pace-of-play trigger exists precisely because walks are now live). **HBP awards first base** — "instruct players to turn away, not step forward" (also a divergence from A, where HBP does NOT award first).
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aa-division.html` ("Hit By Pitch," under Pitching).

### D5. Runs-per-half-inning cap

### D6. Dropped third strike

- **LL Rulebook:** **OFF — CONFIRMED**, the LL default at Minor Softball. **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit)** — Rule 6.09(b) is scoped to Majors, Juniors, **and Senior** League (a 3-way list, not just Majors/Juniors). Not a local AA choice, matches the rulebook baseline exactly.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook — not independently restated on the recovered page, but the rulebook default applies regardless.

### D7. Stealing / leadoffs

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — no leadoffs; AA is Minor Softball, locked to "not until the pitch is batted or reaches the batter."** "Minor League" rule-scoping (Rule 7.13) covers the coach/machine/player-pitch sub-divisions together — A, AA, and AAA are all "Minor League" for this purpose. The "a league may adopt the looser 'on release' timing" flexibility (Rule 7.13 NOTE 2) is written **only for the Major Division** — there's no equivalent permission at Minor, so AA has no rule-based path to "on release" the way Majors genuinely does (see softball-majors-rules.md D7). Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **Corrected 2026-09-03:** the recovered page's claim that runners "may leave when the ball is released" is **wrong, not a local option** — fixed outright. **D8's actual AA rule goes the other direction — stricter than even the Minor default**: a runner may not leave the base at all until the ball crosses the plate (see D7a's cross-reference, and A's own doc, which states the same stricter D8 A/AA standard). A league tightening beyond the LL default is permitted; loosening beyond it, which the recovered page wrongly claimed, is not.
- **NCLL:** Per D8's actual rule — no leaving until the ball crosses the plate (matching A). **Stealing:** early season, limited to 2B & 3B once the ball has crossed the plate; late season/interleague, confirm whether stealing home is allowed (varies by opponent — clarify at the plate meeting). No delayed steals during the windup.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aa-division.html` ("Leaving Early," "Stealing," under Base Running — leadoff-timing claim corrected against Tower's citation).

### D7a. Look-back rule *(softball-specific, no baseball equivalent)*

- **LL Rulebook:** Applies league-wide in principle (Rule 7.13 NOTE 1–2 + 7.08(a)(5)) — a runner off base with the pitcher holding the ball in the circle, not making a play, must immediately commit to advancing or returning, or is called out. Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Not independently addressed on the recovered page.
