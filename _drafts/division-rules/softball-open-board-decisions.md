## 1. Seniors softball — RESOLVED 2026-09-03: All-Star/tournament-only, same shape as baseball Intermediate

Tower's original handoff said no Seniors softball exists at all. Direct research into NCLL's
live pages found conflicting content (a real registration row, an All-Star eligibility
listing) that didn't fit that claim cleanly. **Tower then re-checked every D8 softball site
page specifically for this** (regular-season rules, tournament brackets, schedules, general
info, tournament pages) and confirmed: **no D8 Senior softball exists at any level — not
absent-from-the-docs-on-hand, but genuinely nowhere on the D8 site.** D8 softball tops out at
Juniors. This directly parallels D8 baseball Seniors, which has no District Tournament and
gets a direct bye to WA State (see baseball-senior-rules.md G10) — Senior divisions skip the
district layer in both sports because too few D8 leagues field them.

**Reconciliation:** LL Senior League Softball is a real, chartered national LL division (its
own tournament path: State → Western Region → Senior League Softball World Series, Lower
Sussex DE) — so if NCLL fields a team at all, it can only be an **All-Star/tournament team**,
gated on a coach and enough players, entering directly at State. This is the same shape as
baseball Intermediate (`[Pending]`, "as we did last year, pending a coach and sufficient
player interest" — see baseball-intermediate-rules.md header). The All-Star page listing
"Seniors Softball" while its tournament-dates table omits it is this same gating pattern, not
a page-level contradiction.

**softball-seniors-rules.md rewritten accordingly** — LL Rulebook layer filled in full
(flexible Reg V age structure, field/equipment standards matching Juniors, 7-inning games,
mercy rule, international tie-breaker, DP/Flex-vs-CBO incompatibility), D8 layer marked
n/a-confirmed (not just absent), G10 reframed around the All-Star-only reality.

**What's still genuinely open — direct questions for the VP of Softball, not documentable
from any source (full detail in softball-seniors-rules.md's own Open board decisions
section):**
1. Does NCLL actually charter this team every year, or only some years (coach + player-count
   gating)?
2. What league-age band does NCLL itself call "Senior" softball vs. Juniors? (Reg V leaves
   this genuinely locally flexible — registration-main.html/allstars-main.html/
   softball-hub.html currently disagree: 14–16 / 15–16 / excluded-capped-at-14.)
3. Regular season: do Senior-age players play up in Juniors (LA15 admitted with DA approval),
   or is there a separate arrangement?
4. If fielded, does the All-Star team get a direct bye to State like baseball Seniors, or
   some other qualifying path?
5. **New discrepancy found while applying the resolution:** Tower's LL mercy-rule citation
   (4 innings, 3½ if home ahead) doesn't match the recovered NCLL page's stated tier (5
   innings, 4½ if home ahead — matching Juniors' tier instead). Not resolved either way.
6. Should `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/seniors.html` be published, kept as draft,
   or deleted?

## 1a. LL-layer fill complete, 2026-09-03 — closed several ⚠ gaps, opened two new ones

Tower delivered a full LL Rulebook fill across all six softball docs: the look-back rule
(Rule 7.13 NOTE 1–2 + 7.08(a)(5)), headfirst-slide scoping (Rule 7.08(a)(4) — out below
Juniors, permitted at Juniors and Seniors), the explicit no-balk rule (Rule 8.05), full
Rule 8.01 illegal-pitch/delivery mechanics (including the "leaping is legal" reversal older
coaches won't know), the dropped-ball/"no pitch" rule (Rule 8.07(a)), batting-helmet (Rule
1.16) and catcher's-equipment (Rule 1.17) specifics, and courtesy-runner's full citation plus
a 2026 change (may courtesy-run for both pitcher and catcher at once). This closed D11/D14
as open items across every division doc.

**One correction, project-wide:** an earlier pass's B6 rows implied LL requires defensive/
fielding face masks in some contexts. **LL has no such rule** — Rule 1.14 only requires a
glove; any infield/pitcher mask policy is NCLL/D8-local, not an LL mandate. Fixed in every
doc's B6 row and shared facts table.

**Two leadoff-timing questions surfaced while applying the fill — one was a page error
(now corrected outright), one is a genuine open board question:**
- **AAA's and AA's D7 — RESOLVED, both page errors, corrected outright.** "Minor League"
  rule-scoping (Rule 7.13) covers A/AA/AAA together; the "on release" flexibility (NOTE 2) is
  written only for the Major Division — there's no equivalent permission at Minor. The
  recovered pages' "on release" claims for both AA and AAA were simply wrong, not local
  variances. AA's actual rule is even stricter than the Minor default (no leaving until the
  ball crosses the plate, matching A); AAA's is the Minor default itself (batted or reaches
  the batter). Both docs now state the corrected rule directly.
- **Majors' D7 — still a genuine open NCLL choice, not a citation error.** Rule 7.13 does
  grant the Major Division the option to adopt the stricter Minor timing instead of its own
  "on release" default. NCLL hasn't stated which it uses anywhere found — see
  softball-majors-rules.md D7.

## Cross-division patterns worth answering once

- **Umpiring/STACK threshold** — confirmed at AAA/Majors/Juniors (STACK Officials required);
  A/AA explicitly do NOT require STACK. The exact AA umpiring structure itself (home/visitor
  split, etc.) is inferred from AAA's cross-reference, not independently confirmed for AA.
- **Scoring/standings status** — A: confirmed no score kept at all. AA: undocumented middle
  position (unclear either way). AAA/Majors/Juniors: standings implied by End of Season
  Tournament seeding but not detailed with baseball's GameChanger-level specificity, and
  Juniors' own page explicitly hedges ("may be maintained, district dependent"). Worth a
  single board answer covering AA through Juniors.
- **Roster size targets (G4)** — not documented for any of the five confirmed divisions.
  Worth one board conversation covering all five, same pattern as the baseball tracker's
  roster-size item.
- **AA's actual interleague status (G6)** — A is confirmed in-house-only; AAA/Majors/Juniors
  are confirmed real D8 interleague. AA sits in between — its own page's intro implies
  interleague happens ("managers will clarify rule variance... at the plate meeting") but
  nothing else on the page confirms a real D8 AA schedule. This determines whether the D8
  City AA supplement is load-bearing for AA's regular season.
- **Team formation / draft mechanics (G1)** — not confirmed as a real draft for AAA or
  Majors softball (contrast baseball's own confirmed 2024-adopted AAA draft); softball-hub.html
  implies placement/graduation between divisions without describing the mechanism.
