## A. Division identity

### A1. Age eligibility (league age)

- **LL Rulebook:** **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit, Tower-verified) — "Regulation V" was the wrong citation.** Regulation V is "Selection of Players" (draft methods), not age eligibility. Regulation IV(a), Minor League Division: "Any candidate with amateur status who will attain the age of 7 years before January 1 and who will not attain the age of 13 before January 1 of the year in question shall be eligible to compete in the Minor League Softball Division (subject to the local league Board of Directors alignment of this division)." AAA sits inside this national Minor League band; NCLL's A/AA/AAA split is the local league's own "alignment" of that division. Cite: Regulation IV(a), `2026-little-league-softball.md` line 1011.
- **D8 Interleague:** LA 8, 9, 10, 11 — CONFIRMED via Tower.
- **NCLL:** **9–11 is the registration target band**, with 10's specifically directed to register here. Registration age band: 9–11 (birth dates Jan 1, 2015 – Dec 31, 2017). Fall/winter birthdays may play down with grade-level peers.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Registration/registration-main.html (footnote 3 — "AAA Softball (9-11) is for 9's to 11's. 10's should register for AAA. Fall and winter birthdays may play down with grade-level peers.") + _Programs/softball-hub.html ("AAA Softball is for league-age 9–11 athletes ready for full player pitch, quicker reads, and bigger roles each inning... Graduating AA players land here when they are ready for full player-pitch pace.").

### A2. Purpose / what this division develops

- **NCLL:** "As a Double Goal League, we emphasize both player development and competitive success... This level is often a player's first full season of player pitch." Goals: fundamentals, confidence, multi-position participation, situational awareness and strategy.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` (introduction).

### A3. Participation in two divisions

- **NCLL:** No source found describing NCLL dual-rostering into/out of AAA Softball (unlike baseball's Majors↔AAA Call-Up program) — but see F5 (Pool players), which is a genuinely different mechanism: a league-managed pool, not a dual-roster.

## B. Field & equipment

### B1. Base path distance

- **LL Rulebook:** **60 feet — CONFIRMED**, same as every softball level.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### B2. Pitching distance & mound type

- **LL Rulebook:** **35 feet — CONFIRMED**, "the Little League Softball Minor / AAA standard." **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit)** — the earlier citation to "Rule 1.07 NOTE 3–4" was wrong; there are no such notes. Rule 1.07 itself states all three division distances as inline sub-items: "(1) Minor League: 35 feet; (2) Little League (Majors): 40 feet; and (3) Junior/Senior League: 43 feet." Cite: LL Rule 1.07 — Tower, 2026-09-03; matches `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html`'s own statement exactly ("set at 35 feet from the back point of home plate").
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### B3. Ball standard

- **D8 Interleague:** **11″ ball** — "Official 11\" Little League softballs are used (yellow cover, raised seams)." Cite: D8 City AAA softball doc — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B4. Bat standard

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03; CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit) — the 34″ figure is not universal; Minor Softball (which AAA sits within) caps at 33″.** LL Rule 1.10 — one certification standard at every level, but length isn't uniform: "The bat shall be no more than 33 inches (34 inches for Junior/Senior League) in length." Non-wood bats must be printed with a BPF (bat performance factor) of **1.20** — not previously stated in this doc, plus the current certification mark (USA/ASA equivalent). Barrel diameter ≤2¼″. **2026 change, propagated 2026-09-04 (rulebook-verify pass):** pine tar or any other similar adhesive substance is now permitted on the bat handle/grip at all levels (Rule 1.10 Note 2) — reverses the prior prohibition; may not be applied to the barrel/taper or cover the BPF mark, penalty for violation. Thumb protectors are permitted; choke-knobs and choke-up assists are not (Rule 1.10 A.R. 2). Cite: `2026-little-league-softball.md` "2026 Significant Updates" (lines 59–61) and the full Rule 1.10 text (lines 1594–1602).
- **NCLL:** Discourage heavy bats that compromise swing mechanics.

### B5. Home-run distance / outfield line

- **NCLL:** n/a — not mentioned; softball fields don't carry the same fixed HR-cone convention baseball's lower divisions use (see softball-majors-rules.md B5 for where a real HR line first appears, at Majors).

### B6. Helmets / protective equipment

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03.** Batting helmets NOCSAE-approved, league-provided, required for batter/runners (Rule 1.16). Catcher's gear required any time catching (Rule 1.17). **Correction, project-wide:** LL has no rule requiring a defensive/fielding mask at any position but catcher — Rule 1.14 only requires a glove. The recovered page's "required in some interleague play" / "mandatory for pitchers where adopted by local policy" language is NCLL/D8-local, not an LL mandate — worth being precise about that distinction if this ever goes public-facing.
- **NCLL:** Face masks on batting helmets "strongly recommended (and required in some interleague play)" — a local/D8 choice. Fielding masks strongly recommended for infielders, mandatory for pitchers "where adopted by local policy" — also local, not LL. Catcher's gear: helmet w/ dangling throat guard, chest protector, leg guards, catcher's mitt.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` (Equipment Checks).

## C. Pitching

### C1. Pitching format — kid pitch begins

- **LL Rulebook:** **EXPANDED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit) — the specific pitcher's-plate foot position, not just "inside the 8-ft circle."** Rule 8.01: "(b) Both feet must be on the ground within or partially within the 24-inch length of the pitcher's plate. (d) Prior to pitching, the pitcher shall take a position with his/her pivot foot in contact with the pitcher's plate. This contact must be on or partially on the top surface of the pitcher's plate. The non-pivot foot must be on or behind the pitcher's plate." The 8-ft circle (Rule 7.13's look-back rule) is a separate concept from this foot-position requirement — don't conflate them. Same underhand/windmill motion, 35-ft distance — not division-specific beyond that. Cite: LL Rule 8.01(b),(d).
- **NCLL:** **Full kid pitch — any rostered player may pitch if she demonstrates safe mechanics in practice.** Encourage multiple pitchers; "development outweighs short-term game outcome at this level." Focus on command before movement pitches — riseballs/drops/curves not required at this level.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Who Pitches," "Mechanics & Instruction," under Pitching).

### C2. Illegal pitch enforcement

- **NCLL:** Umpires call obvious illegal pitches (crow hopping, replanting). Early season, most violations are handled as instructional warnings unless they create a real advantage.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Illegal Pitch").

### C3. Pitch-count limits & required rest

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — LL uses innings-pitched, not pitch counts, for softball.** Minors: max 12 innings/day; 7+ innings pitched in a day → 1 mandatory rest day; one pitch thrown = one inning charged. Cite: LL Regulation VI(b) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **D8 Interleague:** **≤3 innings pitched per game through April 30** — a D8-specific early-season workload cap on top of the LL baseline. Cite: D8 City AAA softball doc — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Managers monitor fatigue directly (loss of control, dropping elbow, slowing arm circle, decreased velocity); rotate pitchers early in the season "so more players gain circle experience before playoffs."
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Pitching Limits & Rest").

### C4. Catcher → pitcher restriction

- **NCLL:** "To reduce overuse, avoid having the same player catch a full game and then pitch significant innings in the same day where possible" — a softer, guidance-framed version of baseball's hard catcher/pitcher restriction rule, not a numeric threshold.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Catcher / Pitcher Usage").

### C5. Walks / HBP / balks

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — no balk exists in softball at any level, ever.** An illegal pitch is simply a ball on the batter. Cite: LL Rule 8.05 PENALTY — Tower, 2026-09-03. Full Rule 8.01 delivery mechanics apply at AAA (see facts table) — a violation there is likewise called a ball, not a balk.
- **NCLL:** Standard 4-ball walk applies (see D-section for the walk-continuation restriction, a genuinely AAA-specific rule). HBP awards first base; batters instructed not to bail early and to turn away properly.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Hit By Pitch," under Pitching).

## D. Game play

### D1. Innings for a complete game

- **NCLL:** **6 innings or 2 hours, whichever comes first** — see D2.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Game Length / Time").

### D2. Time limits

- **NCLL:** 6 innings or 2 hours; no new inning should start after the time limit reaches 1:50 unless both managers and the umpire agree and daylight permits. Target efficient (2-minute) changeovers to maximize innings played.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Game Length / Time," under Other Considerations).

### D3. Weekday curfew

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated for AAA Softball.

### D4. Mercy / run-ahead rule

- **NCLL:** **10-run rule after 4 innings (3½ if the home team leads).** Managers may continue with an instructional scrimmage if both agree, but the official score ends at that point.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Mercy Rule").

### D5. Runs-per-half-inning cap

- **NCLL:** **5-run limit per half-inning** (stops when the 5th run scores). The final declared inning (or 6th) is open — no cap — "to allow comeback opportunity"; umpire and managers confirm before it starts.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Inning Run Rule").

### D6. Dropped third strike

- **LL Rulebook:** **OFF — CONFIRMED**, the LL default at Minor Softball, same as A/AA. **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit)** — Rule 6.09(b) is scoped to Majors, Juniors, **and Senior** League (a 3-way list, not just Majors/Juniors). Not a local AAA choice.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D7. Stealing / leadoffs — walk-continuation restriction (genuinely AAA-specific)

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — no leadoffs; the applicable timing is "not until the pitch is batted or reaches the batter."** For LL softball rule-scoping, "Minor League" covers the coach/machine/player-pitch sub-divisions, so D8's A, AA, AND AAA are all "Minor League" for this purpose — Rule 7.13 NOTE 1 sets that timing for all of them. The "a league may adopt the other timing" flexibility (Rule 7.13 NOTE 2) is written **only for the Major Division** — there's no equivalent permission at Minor. Cite: LL Rule 7.13 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **Corrected 2026-09-03:** the recovered NCLL page's claim that runners "may leave the base when the pitcher releases the ball" is **simply wrong, not a local option** — AAA has no rule-based path to the looser "on release" timing the way Majors genuinely does (see softball-majors-rules.md D7). Fixed outright rather than left as an open discrepancy.
- **NCLL:** Stealing of 2B and 3B is permitted once the ball is batted or reaches the batter (not "on release" — corrected). Advancing home on a passed ball/wild pitch/overthrow is permitted "unless a local pre-season notice states otherwise." **"Continuation" on a walk is NOT allowed** — a walked runner may not advance home→2nd while the pitcher has the ball in the circle (advancing on a wild pitch for ball four is NOT continuation, and is still permitted). No delayed steal attempts prior to release — movement must begin after, not during, the circle motion.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Leaving the Base," "Stealing," under Base Running — leadoff-timing claim corrected against Tower's citation) + Tower's walk-continuation and leadoff-timing citations, 2026-09-03. The walk-continuation restriction itself is undisputed and remains the single most AAA-specific rule in the whole softball ladder — worth preserving in full on any public rebuild.

### D8. Bunting

- **NCLL:** "Bunting is optional — use it sparingly, focusing first on core swing development."
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Offensive Coaching Focus").

### D9. Batter's-box rule

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated for AAA Softball specifically.

### D10. On-deck / bat safety

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — not permitted at Minor Softball.** Cite: LL Rule 1.08 NOTE 1 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D11. Courtesy runner

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03.** A Local League Option (Rule 7.14(b) + 3.04), D8 adopts it at every division it runs interleague for — with 2 outs, courtesy-runs for the pitcher and/or catcher of record; under CBO, the runner is the last player put out. 2026 change: may courtesy-run for both pitcher AND catcher at once if both are on base with 2 outs.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook — not independently restated on the recovered page.

### D12. Infield fly

- **D8 Interleague:** **IN effect but lenient** — "umpires call it only when the ball will 'more likely than not' be caught with little effort" — a judgment-call standard, the same wording pattern baseball's AAA infield fly uses. Cite: D8 City AAA softball doc — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### D13. Bat throwing

- **NCLL:** "One warning per team for an accidentally thrown bat; subsequent occurrences may result in the batter being declared out for safety" — a third distinct penalty structure (A: sit half-inning; AA: batter out on repeat; AAA: also batter-out-on-repeat, but phrased as "may result in," softer than AA's flat statement).
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Throwing Bats," under Hitting).

### D14. Headfirst sliding

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — OUT at Minor Softball (A/AA/AAA).** A.R.: no "must slide" rule — a runner is never required to slide. Cite: LL Rule 7.08(a)(4) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** **Feet-first sliding encouraged on close plays. Head-first slides while advancing are NOT allowed; head-first return to a base IS permitted** — matches the rulebook exactly, and the clearest, most explicit statement of this rule anywhere in the softball ladder so far.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Sliding," under Base Running).

### D15. Malicious contact

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated as its own rule for AAA Softball.

### D16. Overthrows / live ball *(AAA-specific row, no equivalent numbering above)*

- **NCLL:** Ball remains live on overthrows unless it enters dead-ball territory (then standard base awards apply). Teach runners to listen to base coaches and pick up the next base decisively.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/aaa-division.html` ("Overthrows / Live Ball").
