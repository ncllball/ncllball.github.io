## A. Division identity

### A1. Age eligibility (league age)

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03.** League age 13–16, **or a locally-defined band within 12–16** — LL Regulation V gives Junior/Senior softball a flexible age structure; the local league defines its own Junior-vs-Senior split (unlike baseball, where the Junior/Senior line is fixed nationally). Cite: LL Regulation V / Regulation IV(a).
- **D8 Interleague:** n/a — confirmed, no D8 Senior softball structure exists to set an interleague age band.
- **NCLL:** **Genuinely unsettled which band NCLL itself uses.** Three different numbers appear across live NCLL pages: registration-main.html's Seniors Softball row states 14–16 (birth dates Jan 1, 2010 – Dec 31, 2013); allstars-main.html's eligibility table states 15–16 for the All-Star team specifically; softball-hub.html's "ages 6-14" cap implies this age band isn't even offered. Given Reg V explicitly leaves this to the local league (see LL Rulebook row above), none of these are "wrong" the way a citation error would be — NCLL simply hasn't stated one authoritative band across its own pages. This is now the doc's top open item (see below), not a research gap — Reg V's flexibility means there's no external source that could resolve it; only NCLL's own choice can.
- **Notes:** Cite: _Registration/registration-main.html (footnote 4) vs. _Programs/allstars-main.html (eligibility table) vs. _Programs/softball-hub.html (intro paragraph).

### A2. Purpose / what this division develops

- **LL Rulebook:** Same Reg I "prepare for eventual selection" framing every division cites, though at Senior League the more relevant framing is LL's own: a bridge to adult/travel/college softball, mirroring high-school-style play.
- **NCLL:** Per the recovered (low-confidence) page: "This level most closely mirrors High School style play under the Little League structure while retaining community, inclusion, and developmental intent."
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/seniors.html` (introduction).

### A3. Participation in two divisions

- **NCLL:** n/a — no mechanism found. See G3 for the real adjacent question: does a Senior-age player's *regular season* happen in Juniors instead (LA15 is admitted to Juniors with DA approval — see softball-juniors-rules.md A1), since no Senior regular season exists to participate in at all.

## B. Field & equipment

### B1. Base path distance

- **LL Rulebook:** **60 feet — CONFIRMED**, same as every softball level. Cite: LL Rule 1.04 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Recovered page states "60' base paths," consistent.

### B2. Pitching distance & mound type

- **LL Rulebook:** **43 feet — CONFIRMED**, same distance as Juniors. Cite: LL Rule 1.07 NOTE 3–4 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Recovered page states "43' pitching distance," consistent.

### B3. Ball standard

- **LL Rulebook:** **12″ — CONFIRMED**, same as Majors/Juniors. Cite: LL Rule 1.09 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Recovered page: "12\" Little League softballs (raised seam)," consistent.

### B4. Bat standard

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower.** One softball bat standard at every level including Senior League — LL Rule 1.10; ≤34″, 2¼″ barrel, current certification mark. Same as every other softball division — softball has no USABat/BBCOR-style divergence at the top of its ladder the way baseball's Senior division does (see baseball-senior-rules.md B4).

### B5. Home-run distance / outfield line

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated on the recovered page; likely 200 ft matching Majors/Juniors, not confirmed.

### B6. Helmets / protective equipment

- **LL Rulebook:** Same as every softball division: batting helmets NOCSAE-approved (Rule 1.16), catcher's gear required any time catching (Rule 1.17). **LL has no defensive/fielding-mask requirement for any other position** — Rule 1.14 only requires a glove; corrected project-wide 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Recovered page: fielding masks strongly recommended for infielders (NCLL/local choice, not LL); full catcher's gear with dangling throat guard mandatory — same standard as Juniors.

## C. Pitching

### C1. Pitching format

- **LL Rulebook:** Same underhand/windmill motion, 43-ft distance — not division-specific beyond the distance (see B2).
- **NCLL:** Recovered page: "Expanded repertoire acceptable (fastball, change-up, movement pitches) with mechanics integrity."

### C3. Pitching limits & required rest

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — no LL innings-pitched restriction at Junior/Senior League softball** (same as Juniors — see softball-juniors-rules.md C3), local board/district may impose one. Cite: LL Regulation VI(b) — Tower, 2026-09-03. Since no D8 Senior softball structure exists, there's no D8-local restriction either the way Juniors gets one (LA12/13-14/15 tiers) — if NCLL fields a tournament-only team, any pitching-workload management would be purely NCLL's own call.

### C5. Walks / HBP / balks

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — no balk exists in softball at any level, ever**, including Senior League. An illegal pitch is simply a ball on the batter. Cite: LL Rule 8.05 PENALTY — Tower, 2026-09-03.

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not documented independently; recovered page content largely restates Juniors' own guidance without Senior-specific detail.

## D. Game play

### D1. Innings for a complete game

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — 7 innings**, same as Juniors. Cite: LL Rule 4.10(a) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Recovered page: "7 innings regulation," consistent.

### D2. Time limits

- **NCLL:** Recovered page: "no new inning after local field/site time cap (typically ~2:00; confirm scheduling notice)" — vaguer than any other softball division's time-limit statement. Since no D8 structure exists to set a firm figure the way it does for Juniors (2:15/2:30), this vagueness is now explained rather than suspicious: there's genuinely no D8-mandated time limit to cite. Any time limit for a Senior tournament team would be tournament-rules-governed (host site's own time rules), not a D8 regular-season figure.

### D3. Weekday curfew

- **NCLL:** n/a — no regular season exists to have a weekday curfew.

### D4. Mercy / run-ahead rule

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — mercy at 4 innings (3½ if home ahead)**, per the LL rulebook standard for Senior League softball. Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03. **Note this is NOT the recovered page's own claim** ("10-run rule after 5 innings, 4½ if home ahead," matching Juniors' tier instead) — the recovered page's mercy tier is one inning later than what Tower's LL citation gives. This specific discrepancy is worth a direct check: either the recovered page is simply wrong (consistent with its low-confidence status), or NCLL/tournament play uses a different local standard than the LL rulebook default. ⚠ Flagged, not resolved.
- **NCLL:** Per the recovered page (uncertain which is authoritative — see above): 10-run rule after 5 innings (4½ if home ahead).

### D6. Ties

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — international tie-breaker rule starting the 8th inning** for a tie game — a genuinely Senior-League-specific rule not found at any lower softball division (Juniors and below don't reach a tie-breaker mechanic in this doc set). Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** n/a — not addressed on the recovered page.

### D7. Stealing / leadoffs

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — runner may not leave base until the pitch is released**, same timing as Majors/Juniors. Cite: LL Rule 7.13 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not independently addressed on the recovered page beyond generic base-running language.

### D14. Headfirst sliding

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — PERMITTED**, same as Juniors (the flip stays on above Juniors too — it doesn't revert). Sliding back INTO a base headfirst always legal at every level; no "must slide" rule. Cite: LL Rule 7.08(a)(4) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Recovered page: "Sliding diversity (hook, pop-up, backdoor) situationally applied" — consistent with headfirst sliding being a live, legal technique at this level.

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not documented independently; recovered page content largely duplicates Juniors' own language.

## Open board decisions

**RESOLVED 2026-09-03:** the doc's original top item — does NCLL Seniors Softball exist at
all — is closed. It reconciles the same way baseball Intermediate does: no D8 regular-season
or district-tournament structure exists (confirmed, not just absent), so any NCLL Senior
Softball team can only be an All-Star/tournament team, gated on a coach and enough players,
entering directly at State (mirroring baseball Seniors' district-skip pattern). The
all-star-page listing without a tournament-dates row is this same `[Pending]`-style gating,
not a contradiction.

What's genuinely still open — direct questions for the VP of Softball, not documentable from
any source found:

1. **Does NCLL charter Senior League Softball every year, or only when there's a coach and
   enough players** (the same gating language Intermediate baseball uses — "as we did last
   year, pending a coach and sufficient player interest")? Registration-main.html's "runs
   only if enough players register" phrasing is consistent with this, but doesn't confirm
   how often it actually clears that bar.
2. **What league-age band does NCLL itself call "Senior" softball vs. Juniors?** LL
   Regulation V leaves this genuinely locally flexible (see A1) — this isn't a citation gap,
   it's a real board-level choice NCLL needs to have made and state once, consistently,
   across registration-main.html/allstars-main.html/softball-hub.html (which currently
   disagree: 14–16, 15–16, and "excluded, capped at 14," respectively).
3. **Regular season: do Senior-age players play up in Juniors** (which admits LA15 with DA
   approval — see softball-juniors-rules.md A1) **, or is there a separate co-op/in-house
   arrangement** for the handful of players too old for Juniors but wanting to keep playing
   before a Senior Softball tournament team (if any) forms?
4. **The Senior Softball All-Star team, if fielded — what tournament does it actually enter,
   and does it go straight to State like baseball Seniors' bye,** or is there some other
   qualifying step (a co-league arrangement with neighboring leagues, similar to how baseball
   Senior's RUG+Northwest Seattle partnership works — see baseball-senior-rules.md G10)?
5. **D4 discrepancy** — Tower's LL citation says mercy at 4 innings (3½ if home ahead); the
   recovered NCLL page says 10-run after 5 (4½ if home ahead), matching Juniors' tier
   instead of the LL Senior standard. Worth a direct check on which is actually current.
6. **Should `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/seniors.html` be published, kept as
   draft, or deleted?** Its existence in a "programs-review"/cleanup folder suggests someone
   already flagged it for a decision.
