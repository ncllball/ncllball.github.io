## B. Field & equipment

### B1. Base path distance

- **LL Rulebook:** **60 feet — CONFIRMED**, same as every softball level. **Note the contrast with baseball**: baseball's Juniors jumps to a full 90-ft diamond at this age; softball's base paths never change from 60 ft at any level. Cite: LL Rule 1.04 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook. "60' base paths" confirmed on the recovered page too.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/juniors.html` (Field Specifications).

### B2. Pitching distance & mound type

- **LL Rulebook:** **43 feet — CONFIRMED**, the Juniors Softball standard (a step up from Majors' 40 ft). **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit)** — the earlier citation to "Rule 1.07 NOTE 3–4" was wrong; there are no such notes. Rule 1.07 itself states all three division distances as inline sub-items: "(1) Minor League: 35 feet; (2) Little League (Majors): 40 feet; and (3) Junior/Senior League: 43 feet." Cite: LL Rule 1.07 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook. "43' pitching distance (Juniors Softball standard)" confirmed on the recovered page.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/juniors.html` (Field Specifications).

### B3. Ball standard

- **D8 Interleague:** **12″ ball** — "12\" Little League softballs (raised seam)." Cite: D8 Juniors softball doc — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B4. Bat standard

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03; citation sharpened 2026-09-04 (Codex audit) — the 34″ figure genuinely is Juniors' own number, not a universal one.** LL Rule 1.10's exact text: "The bat shall be no more than 33 inches (**34 inches for Junior/Senior League**) in length" — Juniors is the division this exception is actually written for; Minor Softball (A/AA/AAA) and Majors Softball cap at 33″ instead (see those docs' B4). Non-wood bats must be printed with a BPF (bat performance factor) of **1.20** — not previously stated in this doc, plus the current certification mark. Barrel diameter ≤2¼″. **2026 change, propagated 2026-09-04 (rulebook-verify pass):** pine tar or any other similar adhesive substance is now permitted on the bat handle/grip at all levels (Rule 1.10 Note 2) — reverses the prior prohibition; may not be applied to the barrel/taper or cover the BPF mark, penalty for violation. Thumb protectors are permitted; choke-knobs and choke-up assists are not (Rule 1.10 A.R. 2). Cite: `2026-little-league-softball.md` "2026 Significant Updates" (lines 59–61) and the full Rule 1.10 text (lines 1594–1602).
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### B5. Home-run distance / outfield line

- **D8 Interleague:** **200 ft** — CONFIRMED via Tower, same distance as Majors.
- **NCLL:** Per D8.

### B6. Helmets / protective equipment

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED via Tower, 2026-09-03.** Batting helmets NOCSAE-approved, league-provided (7 for Juniors — the one division with a different count, since the on-deck batter also needs one), required for batter/runners/on-deck batter (Rule 1.16). Catcher's gear required any time catching (Rule 1.17). **Correction, project-wide:** LL has no rule requiring a defensive/fielding mask at any position but catcher — Rule 1.14 only requires a glove.
- **NCLL:** Infield defensive masks "strongly recommended (pitcher, 3B especially)" — an NCLL/local choice, not an LL rule. Full catcher's gear with a dangling throat guard required.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/juniors.html` (Equipment).

## C. Pitching

### C1. Pitching format

- **LL Rulebook:** **EXPANDED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit) — the specific pitcher's-plate foot position, not just "inside the 8-ft circle."** Rule 8.01: "(b) Both feet must be on the ground within or partially within the 24-inch length of the pitcher's plate. (d) Prior to pitching, the pitcher shall take a position with his/her pivot foot in contact with the pitcher's plate. This contact must be on or partially on the top surface of the pitcher's plate. The non-pivot foot must be on or behind the pitcher's plate." The 8-ft circle (Rule 7.13's look-back rule) is a separate concept from this foot-position requirement — don't conflate them. Same underhand/windmill motion; **43-ft distance** (see B2) — not division-specific beyond that. Cite: LL Rule 8.01(b),(d).
- **NCLL:** Full kid pitch. "43' distance introduces need for deeper command & change-up sophistication." Encourage development of a change-up; defer rise/drop/curve pitches until consistency is established.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/juniors.html` (Pitching).

### C2. Illegal pitch enforcement

- **NCLL:** Illegal pitches (replant/leap) enforced; "early season corrective instruction encouraged" — same graduated-enforcement approach as AAA/Majors.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/juniors.html` (Pitching).

### C3. Pitch-count limits & required rest

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — no LL restriction exists for Junior/Senior softball pitching** (unlike Minor/Majors' 12-innings/day + rest-day rule). Cite: LL Regulation VI(b) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **D8 Interleague (local, since LL sets no Junior limit):** **LA12 → 12 innings/day, 7+ → 1 mandatory rest day** (the same Minor/Majors-style limit, applied because a 12-year-old could be playing up into Juniors). **LA13–14 → no restriction.** **LA15 → may not pitch at all.** One pitch thrown = one inning charged, same convention as every other softball division. CONFIRMED via Tower — this age-tiered structure is the single most detailed, genuinely Juniors-specific rule in the whole softball ladder.
- **NCLL:** Per D8. Monitor mechanics for overuse: posture collapse, shoulder recoil, reduced hip drive.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/juniors.html` (Pitching) + Tower, 2026-09-03. **The LA15-may-not-pitch restriction is a real, concrete data point bearing on the Seniors-softball taxonomy conflict flagged in this doc's header**: it implies NCLL/D8 does anticipate LA15 players present on a Juniors softball roster (otherwise why restrict their pitching specifically?) — worth surfacing to whoever reconciles that conflict.

### C4. Catcher → pitcher restriction

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not explicitly stated for Juniors Softball (AAA/Majors both have a guidance-framed "avoid same-day heavy catch+pitch" note — Juniors' recovered page doesn't restate it, though the same principle likely applies).

### C5. Walks / HBP / balks

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — no balk exists in softball at any level, ever.** An illegal pitch is simply a ball on the batter. Cite: LL Rule 8.05 PENALTY — Tower, 2026-09-03. Full Rule 8.01 delivery mechanics apply at Juniors.
- **NCLL:** Standard 4-ball walk applies (see E1's continuous-lineup context). No walk-continuation restriction — that's AAA-only (see softball-aaa-rules.md D7).

## D. Game play

### D1. Innings for a complete game

- **NCLL:** **7 innings regulation** — the only softball division with a 7-inning game (A/AA/AAA/Majors are all 6 or shorter).
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/juniors.html` (Game Structure).

### D2. Time limits

- **NCLL:** **Time guideline ~2:00** (district/light dependent) on the recovered page. Tower's more precise D8 citation: **2:15/2:30** structure — no new inning after 2:15, no new batter after 2:30, same precise figures as Majors. **No weekday curfew clause** — CONFIRMED via Tower, a real divergence from baseball Juniors, which has an explicit 9:15/9:30 pm weekday curfew (see baseball-junior-rules.md D3); softball Juniors has no such clause.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/juniors.html` (Game Structure) + Tower, 2026-09-03.

### D3. Weekday curfew

- **D8 Interleague:** **CONFIRMED — none.** A genuine, stated divergence from baseball Juniors, not just an undocumented gap. Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per D8 (none).

### D4. Mercy / run-ahead rule

- **NCLL:** **15 after 4 (3½ if home ahead) / 10 after 5 (4½) / 8 after 6 (5½)** — CONFIRMED via Tower, shifted one inning later than Majors' tiers to match the 7-inning game length, the exact same shift pattern baseball's own Intermediate/Junior/Senior mercy tiers use relative to Majors/AAA.
- **Notes:** Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03.

### D5. Runs-per-half-inning cap

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not explicitly restated for Juniors on the recovered page or in Tower's delivery beyond the mercy tiers (D4) — unlike A/AA/AAA/Majors, which all have an explicit 5-run-cap-then-open-final-inning structure. Worth confirming whether Juniors uses the same 5-run cap or relies solely on the mercy rule.

### D6. Dropped third strike

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — applies at Juniors, LL standard, same as Majors.** "The batter becomes a runner on an uncaught 3rd strike if 1B is unoccupied or there are 2 outs." Cite: LL Rule 6.09(b) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D7. Stealing / leadoffs

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — runners may not leave base until the pitch is released**, same timing as Majors. Cite: LL Rule 7.13 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** "Leave on release; aggressive reads on passed balls encouraged." Introduce delay-steal timing and pitcher-rhythm recognition. **No D8 restriction on stealing beyond the standard LL rule** — CONFIRMED via Tower, unlike AAA's walk-continuation restriction, which doesn't carry up to Juniors.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/juniors.html` (Base Running) + Tower, 2026-09-03.

### D8. Bunting

- **NCLL:** "Bunting, slapping, and power approaches can be layered as appropriate" — the most tactically open framing of any softball division, no restriction. **Follows LL rules — no D8 restriction**, CONFIRMED via Tower.

### D9. Batter's-box rule

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not stated for Juniors Softball specifically.

### D10. On-deck / bat safety

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — permitted at Juniors.** **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit)** — Juniors isn't the only softball division where this is on; the rulebook's own exception reads "the on-deck position is permitted in Junior and Senior League," so Senior softball gets it too (softball has no Intermediate tier the way baseball does, so this is a 2-division exception, not baseball's 3-division one). Helmet required, on-deck circle used. Cite: LL Rule 1.08 NOTE 1 — Tower, 2026-09-03. This mirrors baseball's own on-deck flip point conceptually, though softball's flip happens one tier higher — baseball flips on at Intermediate (the tier below Junior), softball waits until Juniors itself.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook — genuinely new mechanic for any NCLL softball player/coach moving up from Majors, since no lower softball division has an on-deck circle at all.

### D11. Courtesy runner

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — LL Rule 7.14(b) + 3.04**, same citation as Majors. A Local League Option D8 adopts; with 2 outs, courtesy-runs for the pitcher and/or catcher of record; under CBO, the runner is the last player put out. **2026 change:** may courtesy-run for both at once if both are on base with 2 outs. Cite: Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D12. Infield fly

- **LL Rulebook:** **Applies — LL standard, unmodified by D8**, same as Majors. Cite: LL Rule 2.00/6.05 — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** Per rulebook.

### D13. Bat throwing

- **NCLL:** ⚠ Not explicitly restated for Juniors on the recovered page (AAA/Majors both state a one-warning-then-out structure — Juniors' page doesn't repeat it, though the same league-wide pattern likely applies).

### D14. Headfirst sliding

- **LL Rulebook:** **CONFIRMED — PERMITTED at Juniors** (out at Tee Ball/Minor/Majors). **CORRECTED 2026-09-04 (Codex audit)** — Juniors isn't the only softball division where this flips on; the rule's out-clause is scoped to "Tee Ball, Minor, or Major Divisions only," so Senior softball also permits headfirst advancement, same as Juniors. **Correction to this doc's own earlier draft:** an initial pass here assumed softball's headfirst-while-advancing rule "isn't tier-dependent the way baseball's Rule 7.08(a)(4) is" — that was wrong. It's the same rule, same citation, and it flips at the same relative point in the ladder (the top two divisions) as baseball's own Junior/Senior flip. Cite: LL Rule 7.08(a)(4) — Tower, 2026-09-03.
- **NCLL:** "Sliding variety (hook, pop-up, backdoor) taught progressively" — consistent with headfirst sliding now being a live, legal technique at this level, unlike every softball division below it.
- **Notes:** Cite: `_temp/.cleanup/programs-review/Softball/juniors.html` (Base Running).
