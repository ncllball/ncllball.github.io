# Kindy Game Rules (coach one-pager, draft)

Coaches Toolbox › Game Day. Source: Kindy Coach Pitch Division Meeting Spring 2026 (coaches' meeting doc); John confirmed on 2026-11-05 that no score is kept. Not published yet.

## The game
- **No new inning after 45 minutes.** Games sit in 75-minute Saturday slots.
- **No score is kept. No umpires.** On offense one coach pitches and another stands behind the batter; on defense coaches are in the field.
- Rules adapt through the season. Keep it challenging, but right for the kids.

## Pitching and batting
- **Coaches pitch to their own team from one knee**, up to about **6 pitches** (depending on the kid), then the ball goes on the **tee**.
- **Everyone bats each half-inning.** No walks, no strikeouts, no bunts or half-swings.
- A ball must go **10 feet and stay fair** for runners to advance.
- All players on base for the last at-bat of the inning score.

## Outs and base running
- **Runners can be called out, but outs aren't counted.**
- No stealing, leading off or sliding.

## Fielding
- Up to **11 fielders** (no more than 5 in the outfield). **No catcher**: the pitcher covers home.
- **Rotate positions.**

## Field
- 45-ft bases, 35-ft pitching distance, soft tee-ball safety balls.

## Safety
- No bat in a player's hands unless they're at the plate.
- A helmet for every batter and runner.
- Lightning: 30/30 rule. Injuries go to the Safety Officer the same day.
