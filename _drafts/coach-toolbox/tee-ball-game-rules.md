# Tee Ball Game Rules (coach one-pager, draft)

Coaches Toolbox › Game Day. Source: Tee Ball Division Meeting Spring 2024 (coaches' meeting doc); John chose this format for 2027 on 2026-11-05. Not published yet.

## The game
- Games run **1 hour**, about 3-4 innings. **No new inning after 45 minutes.**
- Have your team there **10 minutes early** (the playground is fine until it's time).
- **No score is kept. No umpires.** Coaches run the field.

## Batting
- **Tee only**, with the safety ball. No pitching, walks or strikeouts: players swing until the ball is in play.
- **Each inning starts with the bases loaded** (fewer kids sitting in the dugout).
- **The whole lineup bats every inning.**
- **Every hit is a single.** A coach can call a very short hit foul and put the ball back on the tee.
- The **last batter is the "home run hitter"**: every runner runs home, grand-slam style. Fielders wait until the last runner crosses home.
- No stealing or leading off.

## Outs
- **Outs are fun, but they don't count.** Every batter stays on base. Cheer the fielder who made the play.

## Where the coaches stand
- One at the **tee**, one in the **dugout**, one at **1st base**, one in the **outfield**.

## Safety
- No bat in a player's hands unless they're at the plate. There's no on-deck area.
- A helmet for every batter and runner.
- Catchers (if you use one) wear full gear and stand back against the backstop, then return the ball to the tee.
- Lightning: 30/30 rule. Injuries go to the Safety Officer the same day.
