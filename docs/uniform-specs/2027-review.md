## 1. Decided (apply to specs.md, then the picker)

### Vendor
- Still **Protime Sports** for 2027 (Chris, sheet Q1).

### Player shirts
| Division | 2027 model | Was (specs.md / picker) | Source |

- **Drop 4003 entirely** (Chris: "I want to eliminate 4003"). That makes the missing 4003 Youth XL measurement moot.

### Hoodies
- The hoodie is **8203** in the sheet (Chris: "what are the PC90 options? I'm not familiar with that code"). specs.md and both pages still say PC90H/PC90HY in 15–16 places.
- **Players (AAA and up): no size choice.** Protime sends one size up from the jersey, and the order form needs a note saying so (Chris, Lauren; sheet row 11 + comment). T-Ball to AA get no hoodie.
- **Coaches (AAA and up): 8203 with a size choice**, Adult S–4XL (sheet row 18). T-Ball to AA coaches get no hoodie.

### Coach shirts
- **ST350 (unisex) or LST350 (ladies)** for every coach in both sports (Lauren, sheet rows 12–16).
- **ST550 collared shirt is cut** (Chris: "Confusing registration and they weren't very popular anyway").
- **Softball A/AA coaches no longer pick 4408 Womens.** That's the girls' uniform; they get ST350/LST350 like everyone else (Chris).

### Hats
- **T-Ball, Kindy, A, AA (both sports): no hat size question.** All players get Youth and all coaches get Adult (Lauren's yellow note, sheet hat rows 1, 2, 5, 8, 11).
- **PTS30 (baseball AAA and up, players and coaches): XS–SM, SM–MD, LG–XL only.** No Fitted Youth for players or coaches (Chris, Lauren: it only comes in a few colors).
- **GB905 stays out of the picker.** It was a one-off special order for coaches who needed a bigger hat.
- **Softball AAA and up players: 4302 visor only.** HC10 is the coaches' hat (Chris).
- **Softball AAA and up coaches: HC10** Small / M–XL.

### Socks & belts
- Players: included, no size question.
- **Coaches: none.** Remove them from the coach path in the picker (Chris).

### Picker and registration
- **End of the picker:** it needs a Submit/Save-style finish. "Back" and "Start Over" do the same thing (Chris).
- **Registration hookup (agreed):** each registration question is numbered with a numbered dropdown (e.g. *Q13. What hat size do you want to order? 1: Small … 6: Men's medium …*), and the end of the picker shows the same text (*Q13 … 6: Men's medium*), so families know which question to find and which option to pick. Chris: "I think that makes sense. I'll give another round of QA when you're done."
- **"Over 75 re-orders last year"** is in the picker intro (`CMS/uniform-2027.html` line ~2979). Nobody knows the real number, so the line should be reworded without it. Chris wants 2027 to be the baseline for tracking re-orders. Chris asked which page says it, and the answer is the Uniform 2027 picker (tabid 2249029).
